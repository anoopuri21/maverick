## 1. What was asked

An elite, professional content team for the Maverick education niche that can take any task
from **market research** through **natural, humanized content** to **production-ready
delivery**, optimized at **SEO, AEO and GEO** levels, with **zero AI traces** in any report
or content file, always in **simple English**.

## 3. The ten resources — who, why, how

| # | Resource | Why it is on the team | How it is used |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T1** Project Orchestrator | Every task needs one accountable owner for brief, gates and delivery; research shows quality collapses without a coordinator | Opens every task with the brief template, sequences stages, tracks client blanks, ships the final package |
| **T2** Market & Audience Research | Content fails when facts are generic; Gulf education demand data drives every decision | Produces sourced, dated market stats + personas mapped 1:1 to keywords and routes (Stage S1) |
| **T3** Competitor Intelligence | The page wins on a gap; the gap must be proven with live prices, not memory | Four-tier matrix with dated SERP-observed anchors; the counter-positioning raw material (S1) |
| **T4** SEO Strategist | Classic ranking is still the entry ticket for both humans and AI engines | Two-tier keyword map, one home per phrase, meta, links, local SEO inputs (S1 + S6) |
| **T5** AEO/GEO Specialist | 2026 reality: students ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and AI Overviews before they click; nobody in the Gulf bachelors SERP is optimized for it yet | Answer capsules, FAQ↔schema 1:1, extractability review, citation strategy, llms.txt/robots requests, prompt-test measurement (S3 + S6) |
| **T6** Education Niche SME | The client's hard requirement: the whole team must be niche-expert; regulation wording is high-stakes | Owns the knowledge base every role reads; guards the claims whitelist and R-pack phrasings; vetoes every draft |
| **T7** Brand & Messaging Strategist | Conversion needs a locked message hierarchy before a word is written | Positioning, pillars, two-audience tone, CTA ladder. The writer's contract (S3) |
| **T8** Senior Copywriter | Simple-English, keyword-placed, claim-tagged drafting is a specialist craft | Writes the full draft in the counsellor voice; tags claims; keeps placeholders loud (S4) |
| **T9** Humanizer Editor | The no-AI-trace promise needs a dedicated owner with fresh eyes; the writer cannot grade their own rhythm | Banned-pattern sweep, rhythm and concreteness editing, read-aloud + counsellor-desk tests, 10-point scorecard ≥ 9/10 (S5) |
| **T10** QA & Production Reviewer | Production-ready must be proven, not asserted; this project's own audits caught 12-month-mill claims, stale regulation framing, typo traps | Independent fact re-verification, mechanical + placeholder + copyright sweeps, schema/meta checks, verdict line, package assembly (S2 + S7) |

**Independence rules:** T9 never edits its own drafts' source role; T10 reports to no one's
preferences. A P0 stops the pipeline.

## 5. How a task runs (first assignment ready)

1. Drop the task on T1 → brief filled (Gate 0).
2. Research trio works in parallel: T2 market, T3 competitors, T4 keywords; T6 supplies the
   fact packs (Gate 1/1b). T10 independently verifies everything (Gate 2).
3. Client Approval #1 → T7 locks the message, T5 locks the answer plan (Gate 3).
4. T8 drafts, T9 humanizes and scores, T4/T5 verify optimization (Gates 4–6).
5. T10 runs the full sweep and packages; verdict APPROVED → Client Approval #2 → build.

**First assignment:** the Bachelors LP Stage 5 content
(`../bachelors/00-work-plan.md`). Everything upstream is done and verified; the team starts
writing as soon as Approval #1 is signed.
