## 7. Glossary (the team's shared vocabulary)

- **Top-up.** Converting an existing diploma/HND/credits into a full honours degree, usually in one final year. The fastest *legitimate* route to degree level.
- **HND.** Higher National Diploma (UK). Credit-bearing; eligible for top-up.
- **Ofqual.** The UK qualifications regulator. Regulates diplomas (QUALIFI, Gatehouse), not degrees.
- **QUALIFI.** Ofqual-regulated UK awarding body; its Level 5/7 diplomas feed top-up degrees.
- **MoHESR.** UAE Ministry of Higher Education & Scientific Research; runs the 2025 foreign-degree recognition policy (R2).
- **MoE.** UAE Ministry of Education; began online-degree recognition in 2023 (R1).
- **MOHRE.** UAE Ministry of Human Resources & Emiratisation; accepts accredited online degrees for private-sector work permits (R5).
- **Attestation.** Legalising a degree for use in the UAE: FCDO apostille (UK) → UAE Embassy/MOFA; Dataflow/QuadraBay verification first.
- **Equivalency (Certificate of Recognition).** Formal MoHESR document needed for government roles, regulated professions, some visa categories. Not needed for most private-sector jobs.
- **Rotational intakes.** Rolling start dates through the year, not one September intake.
- **Weekend-tolerant.** Timetables that survive a full-time Gulf job.
- **AED-first.** Prices in dirhams; INR only as a dated parenthetical for the Indian-expat rail.

## 8. Claim sources: what may enter any Maverick text

Only three kinds of claim are allowed (the whitelist; full policy in `guidelines/G06`):
1. Facts from the client's own catalogue (`uploads/listing.pdf`) and client-confirmed inputs.
