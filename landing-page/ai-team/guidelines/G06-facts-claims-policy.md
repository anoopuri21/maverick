# G06 · Facts & Claims Policy
**Owner:** T6 · **Enforced at:** Gate 4 (claims map), Gate 7 (final audit)
**One rule:** if a claim cannot be traced to an allowed source, it does not ship.

---

## 1. The claims whitelist: only three classes

| Class | Allowed source | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **a** | Client's own catalogue + client-confirmed inputs (`uploads/listing.pdf`, fees, durations, testimonials, office facts) | "BBA with 7 specialisations from Rushford Business School" |
| **b** | Public facts with a named source and a date | "UAE HE admitted 57,035 new students in 2024–25, +13% (MoHESR via The National, Nov 2025)" |
| **c** | Client-verified statistics with a source | anything the client confirms in writing |

Everything else is rejected. Estimates, "roughly", "industry insiders say", remembered
figures: all out, or downgraded to a tracked `[VERIFY]` note.

## 2. Regulation copy: the locked phrasings

Recognition statements use the R-pack (`02-niche-knowledge-base.md` §3) word-for-word where
locked. The private-sector-safe sentence is the default close:
*"Recognised for private-sector use; MoHESR recognition route available. Confirm your
category with an advisor."*

**Automatic P0:** "visa guaranteed" · "recognised for all purposes" · "government job
guarantee" · "100% acceptance" · any equivalency promise · any credit-cap number quoted as
current · MoE-2023 presented as the headline fact (MoHESR-2025 is primary).

## 3. Precision rules

- Degrees ≠ diplomas. Ofqual/QUALIFI regulate diplomas; named universities award degrees.
  Every occurrence of "UK-recognised" states which sense it means.
- Accreditation claims only from the partner fact sheet in `02` §4. Excluded claims stay
  excluded (RBS ACBSP/AACSB: agent-page gossip, not on RBS's own site).
- One duration per programme family, from the client-confirmed table only. Unconfirmed?
  Write the delivery model, not a number.
- Competitor figures: published prices only, observed live, dated; category criticism only,
  tied to a regulation source.

## 4. The do-not-promise list

No salary figures · no "best / top / #1 / guaranteed / 100%" · no invented testimonials ·
no university logos before written permission (text-only names until then) · no stock photos
presented as our office or students · no urgency that is not a real intake date.

## 5. Claims map artifact

Every deliverable carries its claims map at handoff:

```
| Claim (where it appears) | Class | Source + date | Verified by |
```

Gate 7 requires 100% coverage. A single unmapped claim = P0.

## 6. Fact lifecycle

- Facts enter the knowledge base with source + date.
- Facts age: competitor prices 90 days, market stats 180 days, regulation immediately on
  change. T6 runs the expiry check each task.
- When a fact changes: update `02`, list every live asset that used the old version,
  schedule the fix.

## 7. Why this policy is strict

Education is a life-decision purchase. Search engines treat it accordingly (high E-E-A-T
bars, YMYL-style scrutiny), and so do parents with their children's money. A single
overstated recognition claim can cost a family a visa category, and Maverick its name.
Strict sourcing is not bureaucracy; it is the product.
