# Maverick AI Content Team — Working Environment
**Project:** Maverick Business Academy (London) · education niche · GCC/UAE market
**Created:** 2026-09-14 · **Status:** Live — ready for the first task (Bachelors LP content, Stage 5)

This folder is the complete working environment for Maverick's AI content team:
ten specialist roles, seven guidelines, two templates, and one pipeline.
The team takes a content task from market research to a production-ready,
publish-safe deliverable, optimized for SEO, AEO (answer engines) and GEO
(generative engines), written in simple, natural English.

## The three hard promises of this team

1. **Niche-first.** Every role carries the Maverick knowledge base. Nobody writes a word about UK degrees, UAE recognition, or Gulf students without the facts in `02` behind it.
2. **No AI traces.** Every deliverable passes the humanization protocol (`guidelines/G02`) and the production sweep (`guidelines/G07`). Content reads like a senior admissions advisor wrote it: simple, concrete, human. No tool mentions, no agent labels, no machine patterns in anything client-facing or published.
3. **Production-ready means ready.** Zero placeholders, zero unverified claims, schema valid, meta within limits, humanization scorecard attached. If a gate fails, the piece does not move.

## Ground rules for working here

- Every stage has a gate. Gates are not suggestions.
- Every external number carries a source and a date.
- Client-owned facts (fees, durations, testimonials, logos) stay as tracked placeholders until the client confirms. They never ship.
- File names follow `03-content-pipeline.md` §6. Numbered, dated, obvious.
- Deliverables live in the page's own folder (e.g. `landing-page/bachelors/`). This folder holds the team's rulebooks, not the output.

This team **is** that pipeline, upgraded for content work: the same discipline, plus three

fact guardian, and a humanizer editor. Stage 5 of the bachelors work plan
(`../bachelors/00-work-plan.md`) is this team's first assignment.
