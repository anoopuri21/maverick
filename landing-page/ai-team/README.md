# Maverick AI Content Team · Working Environment
**Project:** Maverick Business Academy (London) · education niche · GCC/UAE market
**Created:** 2026-09-14 · **Generalized:** 2026-09-15 · **Status:** Live, page-agnostic

This folder is the complete working environment for Maverick's AI content team:
ten specialist roles, seven guidelines, two templates, and one pipeline.

The team is **generic, not page-bound**. Give it any content task, for any page, and it runs
the same discipline: fresh market research, a competitor comparison with dated price anchors,
point-by-point research notes, a plan and strategy, then natural, quality content. If the
client supplies keywords, the team validates and uses them. If not, the team researches a
location-based keyword set and writes to it. One task may be the Masters landing page; the
next may be a bachelors page, a single programme page, or a guide. The roles, gates and
standards do not change between pages.

Every deliverable is optimized for SEO, AEO (answer engines) and GEO (generative engines),
written in simple, natural English.

## The four hard promises of this team

1. **Niche-first.** Every role carries the Maverick knowledge base. Nobody writes a word about UK degrees, UAE recognition, or Gulf students without the facts in `02` behind it.
2. **No AI traces, absolute.** Every deliverable passes the humanization protocol (`guidelines/G02`) and the production sweep (`guidelines/G07`). Zero em dashes, zero en dashes, zero banned words, zero machine rhythm. Content reads like a senior admissions advisor wrote it: simple, concrete, human. No tool mentions, no agent labels, no machine patterns in anything client-facing or published.
3. **Clean client documents.** Anything the client reads, and every PDF or Word file handed over, ships with zero placeholders and zero internal instructions. Unconfirmed facts use delivery-model wording or stay out; they are never left as markers.
4. **Production-ready means ready.** Zero unverified claims, schema valid, meta within limits, humanization scorecard attached. If a gate fails, the piece does not move.

## Ground rules for working here

- Every stage has a gate. Gates are not suggestions.
- Every external number carries a source and a date.
- Every page gets fresh research. Market, competitor and keyword work is run for the page in
  hand, not copied from another page's pack.
- Client-owned facts (fees, durations, testimonials, logos) stay as tracked placeholders in the
  internal brief only, until the client confirms. They never ship.
- File names follow `03-content-pipeline.md` §6. Numbered, dated, obvious.
- Deliverables live in the page's own folder (e.g. `landing-page/masters/`). This folder holds the team's rulebooks, not the output.

## Where this team sits in the existing project

The Bachelors LP SOP (`../bachelors/01-bachelors-lp-sop.pdf`) defines an 11-role page pipeline.
This team **is** that pipeline, upgraded for content work: the same discipline, plus three
roles the market now demands: a dedicated AEO/GEO specialist, an education niche SME as
fact guardian, and a humanizer editor.

The team has already produced one full page end to end (the Bachelors LP) and audited a
second (the Gulf Masters LP). From here it takes any page task, in any order, on the same
pipeline.
