   Rushford alone, 6 MBAs and 16 EMBAs at GAU" is a true, catalogue-sourced ladder. Block 6

   Block 7 must use catalogue names verbatim.

## 2. Competitor benchmark (live observation, 2026-09)

What is visibly working for each tier, and what we take from it:

| Competitor move (observed) | Who | Evidence | Our adoption (neutral framing in SOP) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scholarship/offer rails on every page; "up to 50% scholarship" headlines | Amity, Middlesex | amityuniversity.ae fees page; mdx.ac.ae scholarships | Offer rail slot (block 10 + announcement bar): filled only with client-confirmed offers; honest-by-default |
| Open days + campus tours as the main booking CTA; WhatsApp chat to admissions on the same page | Middlesex | mdx.ac.ae/campus-open-days | Event rail: monthly "meet your counsellor" sessions at the Sharjah office + WhatsApp Q&A; book via WhatsApp |
| Multiple contact rails (chat / call / WhatsApp / request a call) | Middlesex, Amity | contact pages | Add "Request a call" micro-CTA as A/B variant next to the form |
| Third-party review volume as the trust engine (123 edarabia reviews, 4.3 portal ratings) | Westford, agents | edarabia, bachelorsportal, eduopinions | Post-launch review loop: permission-based Google Reviews + directory profiles; pull real ratings onto the page only once they exist |
| Named testimonials with photos high on the page; weekend/online flexibility in the first screen | Westford | mywestford.com bachelors UAE page | Proof block stays named + permissioned (our rule is stricter); flexibility chips already in hero |
| City-list social proof ("Dubai, Sharjah, Abu Dhabi, Riyadh, Jeddah...") | Westford | same | GCC city strip in block 13 (matches areaServed schema) |
| Rolling admissions + published intakes + acceptance transparency | Middlesex | shiksha listing | Intake calendar strip kept; add "who can apply" entry-route lines per family (client-confirmed) |
| Directory-style browsing UX (filters, 90+ programmes badges) | portals, Middlesex | bachelorsportal, mdx open-day copy | Block 7 build spec: filter chips (field, delivery) on the programme grid |
| Segmented funnels: parent-facing (cost, safety, aid) vs student-facing (life, majors); source-matched landing variants | industry practice | higher-ed conversion research [1] | CRO roadmap: UTM variants (parent-led vs student-led hero subline); nurture sequence per audience |
| Post-enquiry nurture (email/WhatsApp sequences) outperforming one-off follow-up | industry practice | same | Nurture spec: WhatsApp ack in minutes, call in 1 business day, day-3 recognition PDF, day-7 parent FAQ (Zapier) |
| Soft CTAs beat "Apply Now"; sticky CTA bars lift programme-page CVR | industry practice | same | Our CTA system already soft-first; sticky CTA already specced. Add CTA-copy A/B seed |

[1] Higher-education conversion research: segmented funnels +20–40% relevance lift; soft CTA
variants outperform; sticky CTA bar recommended; nurture is the highest-impact channel
([Acceleroi 2026 benchmarks](https://www.acceleroi.com/blog/higher-education-conversion-rates),
[Foundry CRO 2026](https://foundrycro.com/blog/higher-education-marketing-benchmarks-2026/)).
These numbers stay in this doc only.

**What we deliberately do NOT adopt:** scholarship percentage headlines without client
confirmation (we never invent aid), review-volume buying, "90+ programmes" style inflation
(we name, not inflate), acceptance-rate theatre (our transparency is entry routes, not
percentages).
