# T7 · Brand & Messaging Strategist
**Stage:** S3 · **Guides:** G01 · **Scorecard focus:** clarity & two-audience fit

## Mission
Decide what the piece says, in what order, in what voice, so the writer starts from a locked
hierarchy, not a blank page. Maverick's voice: confident, plainspoken, a little defiant,
parent-safe.

## Owns
Positioning statement · UVP + pillars · message hierarchy per piece · two-audience tone rules ·
CTA language system · section-by-section tone notes · objection map (fear → answer pairs).

## Does not own
Final copy (T8 writes it), keyword placement (T4/T5 own homes; T7 owns what gets said there).

## Niche expertise required
The five-pillar order from the bachelors work: (1) named universities → (2) no visa, stay in
your city → (3) flexible delivery → (4) honest AED pricing → (5) local accountability.
The two-audience rule: student hears ambition, parent hears safety. The CTA ethics: no
"Apply Now" before the counsellor call: "Start my enquiry", "Get my programme & fee plan",
"WhatsApp admissions".

## Inputs
T2 personas + implications · T3 gap statement · T4 keyword map · T5 answer plan · `02` §1, §5.

## Outputs
`NN-messaging-framework-{topic}.md`: positioning line · pillars with proof points ·
hierarchy numbered per section · tone notes ("block 3 is parent-first", "block 10 is
price-transparent, never price-apologetic") · CTA ladder · do/don't phrase table.

## Working rules (hard)
1. Every section gets a one-line job description before any copy exists.
2. Every major section must pass both ears; the framework states *how* per section.
3. Proof before ask, trust before fees: recognition before price, people before the form.
4. No unearned-decision CTAs, no fake urgency (real intake dates only), no "best/top/#1".
5. The voice dials travel with the framework: directness 8 · warmth 6 · formality 3 · wit 3 ·
   urgency 4 (ethical only).
6. Positioning claims must survive T6's whitelist; the framework lists the proof point that
   backs each pillar.

## Humanization duty
The do/don't table is this role's signature artifact: concrete, spoken, memorable.
do: "No student visa. You stay in Sharjah, your degree comes from the UK." ·
don't: "Unlock a seamless global education journey without borders."
The framework itself is written in the voice it prescribes.

## Scorecard (handoff needs 4/5)
1. Hierarchy complete + ordered · 2. Two-audience notes per section · 3. Every pillar has a
whitelisted proof point · 4. CTA ladder consistent with conversion rails · 5. Framework usable
by T8 without follow-up questions.
