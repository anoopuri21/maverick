## 3. Gaps & Opportunities for Maverick

**G1 — The "ladder" gap (biggest differentiator).** No competitor sells Level 3 diploma → Level 8 → BSc → MBA → MSc → DBA under one roof. Aston/Henley/Bayes each sell *one* degree. Maverick's page should frame this as **"start where you are, finish where you want"**, which converts Persona B (switchers) and C (founders) that premium schools structurally can't serve.

**G2 — Entry-route transparency.** "MBA without entry requirements" is a live, high-intent SERP. Aston wins it ethically ("2:2 or relevant professional experience"). Maverick's actual routes (degree; **OFQUAL-recognised PgD (120 credits) + 3–4 yrs managerial experience**; HS diploma + English-medium pathway) are more flexible than most mid-market rivals — but are currently buried. Surface them as a clean 3-route table.

**G3 — Speed.** 10–15 month MBAs and 10–12 month diplomas beat the 24-month norm of Bayes/Aston/Aberdeen. "In 12–15 months, not 24" is a winnable, defensible claim — with a caveat line ("programmes vary by awarding partner").

**G4 — Payment flexibility as a message.** Aston's "from £875/month" is doing heavy lifting. Maverick's site already has "fee plans" in the UAE landing CTA. Make instalments an explicit bullet + a monthly-figure anchor in the pricing section.

**G5 — No-visa + recognition dual callout for Persona D.** Aberdeen's two lines ("no student visa required", "degree title won't say online") are the exact objections Persona D carries. Maverick's London HQ + Dubai office + English-medium programmes are the *better* answer for GCC learners.

**G6 — Conversational rail is uncontested territory.** Every premium competitor is phone/form-only. Maverick's WhatsApp-first pattern (already in production) is a genuine CRO edge for UK price-sensitive and international buyers — keep, amplify, and measure it.

**G7 — Trust-proof specificity beats brand age.** Competitors lean on rankings (FT, EQUIS, "1st in UK"). Maverick can't out-rank them and shouldn't try. It wins on *named recognition*: Ofqual-regulated QUALIFI, IACBE/YÖK/YÖDAK-accredited GAU, University of Gloucestershire MBA, England & Wales registered, operating since 2012 (UAE heritage). Specific > prestigious.

**G8 — The premium sites' weakness is "you."** Their pages are institutional. Maverick's founder/faculty section (named people, Saturday classes, admissions team that replies in a day) is warmer and more accountable — lean into it.

## 4. Positioning Implication (one-liner for Agent 5)

> **Premium schools sell the brand. The price floor sells speed. Maverick sells the honest middle: a recognised qualification, a realistic entry route, a real price, and a finish date you can hold them to.**

### Claims discipline (carry to QA)
- Never claim "accredited MBA" generically — say *what* is accredited (QUALIFI/Ofqual recognition; specific university partners; IACBE/YÖK/YÖDAK for GAU).
- Ranking claims: none — Maverick has none; fabricating "top 100" would be a compliance failure (ASA/Ofcom + UKPRC).
- "UK accredited" → prefer **"UK-recognised"** (Ofqual-regulated awarding) for diplomas; **"university-awarded"** for partner degrees.
- Fee figures: **placeholder until client confirms** — the production site already follows this rule (MLP design-system checklist).
