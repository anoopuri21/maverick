**Implications**
1. **Demand is growing and business is the #1 field** → BBA (RBS, 7 specialisations) is the anchor offer; BSc (GAU, 10 programmes) is the second rail; "progression to MBA" is a real, market-validated story (M4, M9, M10).
2. **The audience is 35% international in Dubai and 42% of that is Indian** → page must be expat-native: AED pricing primary, INR parenthetical (dated), no "study abroad" framing — the student is already here.
3. **Supply is deep (400+ programmes) and trust is the scarce asset** → differentiation is recognition + local accountability, never programme count.
4. **Campus demand rising (M8) ≠ threat**: the campus audience (18–21, family relocating) is not our primary persona. Ours is the working/staying prospect the campus model can't serve (visa, time, cost). The page should *acknowledge* campus as the traditional path, then reframe — not fight it.

## 6. Implications for the page (decisions carried into copy)

1. **Message hierarchy (final):** (1) real named universities → (2) no student visa, stay in your city → (3) weekend-tolerant/online flexible → (4) AED + instalments, honest vs campus vs mill → (5) Sharjah office + human counselling + since 2012. *(Matches SOP §4 pillars — verified consistent.)*
2. **Two-audience tone (locked):** student hears ambition/flexibility; parent hears recognition/safety/cost control. Recognition block (block 3) is written parent-first.
3. **Regulation copy = R1–R6 only**, date-stamped, with "confirm your category with an advisor" close. The 2025 MoHESR conditional-recognition policy (R2) is the page's trust spine — no competitor cites it.
4. **Price framing:** AED everywhere; INR once, parenthetical, dated (M3/M10); competitor anchors verified in SERP only; Maverick fee = [FEE PLACEHOLDER until client confirms].
5. **The 12-month-mill answer:** category-level, sourced to R2 conditions — "short-timeline bachelors may not meet MoHESR recognition conditions" — never "X college is a scam".
6. **Top-up is a conversion flank, not a footnote:** dedicated route card (QUALIFI L5/L7 → UK honours) for the P3 persona — highest-intent buyer in the whole market.
7. **Cross-rails (internal links):** Europe relocation option → `/global-bachelors-pathway` ("prefer to relocate? we have European pathways too"); progression → `/online-mba-masters-uae` once un-holded. Maverick owns both ends of the student journey — unique in this SERP.
9. **Real statistics visuals (3, live-page designs — data locked):** (a) PIE in the Problem section — Dubai private-HE international share 35% / local 65% (KHDA 2024–25; international +29% YoY); (b) PIE in the Programme overview — catalogue mix 7 BBA (39%) / 10 BSc (56%) / 1 BA (Hons) (5%) from listing.pdf; (c) BAR in Fees — total degree cost in AED: campus 105k–195k (35k–65k/yr × 3 yrs, Fateh Jan-2026), S P Jain 100k/4yr (educations.com), fast-track 3.5k–25k (coursetakers Apr-2026), Maverick bar = client-confirmed only. Style: brand navy/red, source line always visible. **No invented numbers — chart data = sourced fact or client-confirmed.**

8. **Do-not-promise list:** no visa guarantees, no equivalency guarantees, no salary figures, no "best/top/#1/guaranteed", no unverified stats, no university logos before permission (SOP §8).
