Recognition in the UAE

The 2025 rules, explained simply.

Short answer: yes for private-sector use under the 2025 MoHESR rules, with conditions; government roles and regulated professions follow the formal equivalency route.

We make no visa promises and no blanket recognition promises. You get the dated policy, the conditions in plain words, and a counsellor who knows both.

Is an online bachelor's degree valid in the UAE?

Yes, under the current 2025 rules, with conditions. On 10 March 2025, MoHESR published its policy for degrees earned through distance, online, open or correspondence study. Recognition is conditional: the awarding institution must be accredited in its home country, the programme must follow its approved structure, and credit transfers must be licensed. The recognition fee for a bachelor's is Dh100, verification runs through Dataflow or QuadraBay, the process is fully online, takes around 30 working days, and has a three-month appeal window. The Ministry of Education began recognising online degrees in 2023, with Engineering, Medicine and Law excluded.

Private jobs and government jobs

For most private-sector jobs, an accredited degree plus verification is enough. The formal Certificate of Recognition is needed for government roles, regulated professions and some visa categories. Confirm your category with a counsellor before you enrol.
