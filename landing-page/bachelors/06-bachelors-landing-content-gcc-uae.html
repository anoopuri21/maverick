Section 12 · Recognition

Degree Recognition in the UAE: The 2025 Rules, Explained Simply

Short answer: yes for private-sector use under the 2025 MoHESR rules, with conditions; government roles and regulated professions follow the formal equivalency route. The details, dated, are below.

Is an online bachelor's degree valid in the UAE?

Yes, under the current 2025 rules, with conditions. On 10 March 2025, MoHESR published its policy for degrees earned through distance, online, open or correspondence study. Recognition is conditional: the awarding institution must be accredited in its home country, the programme must follow its approved structure, and credit transfers must be licensed. The recognition fee for a bachelor's degree is Dh100, verification runs through Dataflow or QuadraBay, the whole process is online, takes around 30 working days, and there is a three-month appeal window. The UAE's Ministry of Education began recognising online degrees in 2023, with Engineering, Medicine and Law excluded.

Private jobs and government jobs

For most private-sector jobs, an accredited degree plus verification is enough. The formal Certificate of Recognition is the document needed for government roles, regulated professions and some visa categories. Confirm your category with a counsellor before you enrol, and we will walk you through it.

Putting a UK degree to use in the UAE

The standard route is a UK FCDO apostille, then UAE Embassy and MOFA stamping, with Dataflow or QuadraBay verification as the usual first step. We guide our students through every stage. This is guidance, not legal advice.

Our promise

We make no visa promises and no blanket recognition promises. What you get is the dated policy, the conditions in plain words, and a counsellor who knows both.
