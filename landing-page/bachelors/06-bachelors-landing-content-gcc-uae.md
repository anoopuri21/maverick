### Block 9: Recognition and careers

**H2:** Recognition in the UAE, explained like we would to our own family.

**H3 (R5 home): Is an online bachelor's degree valid in the UAE?**
The short answer: yes, under the current 2025 MoHESR framework, with conditions. On 10 March 2025, MoHESR published its recognition policy for degrees earned through distance, online, open or correspondence study. Recognition is conditional: the awarding institution must be accredited in its home country, the programme structure must be the approved one, and credit transfers must be licensed. The bachelor's recognition fee is Dh100, verification runs through Dataflow or QuadraBay, the process is fully online, takes around 30 working days, and there is a 3-month appeal window. The UAE's MoE began recognising online degrees in 2023, with Engineering, Medicine and Law excluded.

**Private sector vs government (locked phrasing):** for private-sector jobs, an accredited degree plus verification is usually sufficient. The Certificate of Recognition (equivalency) is the formal document needed for government roles, regulated professions and some visa categories. Confirm your category with an advisor before you enrol, and we will walk you through it.

**MOHRE:** MOHRE accepts accredited online and distance degrees for work permits, and a bachelor's degree is expected for most professional bands of AED 4,000–8,000 a month.

**Attestation for UK awards:** the route is a UK FCDO apostille, then UAE Embassy/MOFA attestation, with Dataflow or QuadraBay verification as the standard first step. We guide you through the full route, step by step.

**The honest close:** we make no visa promises and no blanket recognition promises, ever. What you get is the dated policy, the conditions in plain words, and a counsellor who knows both.

- BSc Accounting / Accounting and Finance: accounting, audit support, finance operations.

### Block 14: FAQ (10 Q&As, 1:1 with FAQPage schema)

**1. Is an online bachelor's degree valid in the UAE?**
Yes, under the current 2025 MoHESR framework. The 10 March 2025 policy grants conditional recognition to degrees earned through distance, online, open or correspondence study, provided the institution is accredited at home, the approved programme structure is followed, and credit transfers are licensed. For online routes, no student visa is required. Confirm your personal category with an advisor before you enrol.

**2. Do I need a student visa to study with Maverick?**
No student visa is needed for our online routes. You stay in your city, keep your current visa status, and study from Dubai, Sharjah, Abu Dhabi or anywhere in the GCC. If you ever prefer a campus life abroad, we also run relocation pathways, and we will say so clearly.

**3. How long does a bachelor's degree in Dubai take through your routes?**
Each awarding university sets its own duration, and your written plan states yours before you commit. What never changes: it is a full bachelor's degree with the full credit load, not a compressed product. Top-up students complete the final stage of an honours degree after their diploma, HND or credits are assessed.

**4. What does a BBA in UAE cost with you, and can I pay by instalment?**
Plans are priced in AED and payable by instalment, and you receive the full fee plan in writing before any payment. Campus comparison: AED 35,000–65,000 a year (Fateh, Jan 2026). Fast-track 12-month offers at AED 3,500–25,000 carry recognition risk under the MoHESR conditions. Your counsellor quotes your exact plan on the first call.

**5. Can I really study while working full-time?**
Yes. Delivery is online with weekend-tolerant scheduling, which is why most of our working students keep their jobs through the degree. Live sessions are scheduled with working professionals in mind, and your counsellor maps the weekly load with you before you enrol.

**6. What is a top-up bachelor's degree in UAE?**
It is the legitimate way to complete a bachelor's degree fast. Ofqual-regulated QUALIFI Level 5 and Level 7 diplomas are topped up into a named UK honours degree. The Level 5 catalogue spans 14 specialisations, including Law, IT and Cyber Security. Bring your transcript to the counselling call: we assess it before you commit.

**7. How does attestation work for a UK-awarded degree?**
The standard route is a UK FCDO apostille, then UAE Embassy/MOFA attestation, with Dataflow or QuadraBay verification as the usual first step for recognition. We guide our students through every stage of the route. This is guidance, not legal advice.

**8. When can I start?**
Intake windows run through the year, and your counsellor confirms the next one for your chosen programme at the first call. Once your documents are ready, we hold your place. We only ever quote real intake dates.

**9. Can I do an MBA after this?**
Yes, and the ladder is already built. Rushford carries 12 MBA specialisations and 9 MSc routes, GAU adds 6 MBAs, 16 EMBAs and thesis-based MScs, and Ofqual Level 7 diplomas are available too. Many of our students plan both degrees in one counselling call.

**10. What should my parents check before I enrol?**
Three things. The awarding university by name, on the page and in your written plan. The dated MoHESR recognition policy and where your category stands. And us: London headquarters since 2012, a UAE office at Robot Park Tower, Sharjah, and a counsellor your parents can speak with directly. If any provider hides any of the three, walk away.
