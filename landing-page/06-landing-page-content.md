## §13 FAQ — "The objection floor" (8 Q&As)

1. **Is an online MBA from Maverick recognised by employers?**
Diplomas are regulated in England by Ofqual through QUALIFI, an approved awarding organisation — you can verify that on the Ofqual register. MBA and MSc degrees are awarded by partner universities, including the University of Gloucestershire and Girne American University (IACBE-accredited, recognised by YÖK and YÖDAK). Recognition is specific to the awarding body of *your* programme — our admissions team will confirm it for yours in writing.

2. **Can I get in without a 2:1 degree?**
Yes. Beyond the standard degree route, you can enter MBA-level programmes with an OFQUAL-recognised Postgraduate Diploma (120 credits) plus 3–4 years of managerial experience. School-leavers with English as their medium of instruction can start the ladder from Level 3 and progress up.

3. **How long does it actually take?**
Diplomas run 3–12 months; degrees and MBA/MSc programmes typically 10–18 months; doctorates are part-time by design. Rotational intakes mean you start when you're ready, not only in September.

4. **Do I have to do a dissertation?**
Many programmes assess through case studies, projects, and assignments; dissertation routes are available where the awarding framework requires research. Your specific assessment model is confirmed at enrolment.

5. **Can I study while working full time?**
That's the design brief. Online and hybrid delivery, Saturday classes, and flexible pacing exist precisely for professionals with 40-hour weeks.

6. **When can I start?**
Next rotational intake — enquiry to offer typically takes days. Your offer states your start date.

7. **How do fees and payment plans work?**
Final fees are confirmed in writing on your offer, and payment plans are available across most programme families. [Insert "from £X/month" once the client confirms.] There is no cost to enquire.

8. **I live outside the UK. Do I need a student visa?**
Online routes do not require a student visa. Learners study from the UK, the UAE, MENA, and Asia; we have a London headquarters and a Dubai regional office (Robot Park Tower, Sharjah).

9. **What happens after I enrol? Am I on my own?**
No. A named admissions advisor, faculty, and progress reviews continue through to completion — and you join an alumni network across the UK and the Gulf.

10. **How is this different from a £21,000 university online MBA?**
Different buyer, different job. If you want a top-ranked business school brand, buy it. If you want a recognised qualification, an honest price, flexible entry, and a finish date in 10–18 months — that's the comparison we'd ask you to make.
