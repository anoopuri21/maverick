## 1. Positioning Statement

> **For** ambitious working professionals, career-switchers, and founders in London and the Gulf **who** want a recognised business qualification without pausing their career or living on a 3-year clock, **Maverick Business Academy** is the London-based business academy that pairs **UK-recognised qualifications** (Ofqual-regulated diplomas through QUALIFI; university-awarded MBAs and MScs) with **unrealistic flexibility** — rotational intakes, online/hybrid delivery, and experience-based entry — **unlike** premium university online MBAs that cost £21,000–£52,000 and two years, **and unlike** the no-name certificate mills at the bottom of the market, **because** we've delivered this since 2012 for a global learner base from a London headquarters with a Dubai office.

## 2. Unique Value Proposition (UVP)

### Primary UVP (the one the page must own)
> **"A recognised business qualification that fits the life you're already living."**

### Support pillars (the 4 "Maverick Advantages")
| # | Pillar | Proof point (must appear on page) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Recognised, not just "online"** | Ofqual-regulated QUALIFI diplomas; university-awarded MBAs (e.g., University of Gloucestershire, Girne American University — IACBE/YÖK/YÖDAK); England & Wales registered company |
| 2 | **Entry that fits real people** | 3 explicit routes: degree · PgD(120cr)+3–4 yrs experience · pathway for HS-diploma + English-medium learners |
| 3 | **Speed with support** | 10–18 month programmes; rotational intakes (start when you're ready, not only in September); dedicated admissions team |
| 4 | **A ladder, not a one-off** | Level 3 → Level 8 → BSc → MBA/MSc → DBA/PhD — credits and progression mapped, 58 programmes, one academy |
| 5 | *(bonus, unique)* **London ⇄ Dubai footprint** | HQ Ruislip, London + Robot Park Tower, Sharjah; English-medium; no-visa online routes |

## 3. Voice & Tone

**Voice:** A sharp, well-connected practitioner — the person who sat in the boardroom *and* studied for the credential. Confident, plainspoken, a little defiant (Maverick is in the name; the brand has permission for it).

**Tone dials:** Directness 8/10 · Warmth 6/10 · Formality 3/10 · Wit 3/10 · Urgency 4/10 (ethical only)

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Rotational intakes. Start when you're ready." | "We offer a flexible and dynamic learning environment." |
| "£21k+ is what a premium online MBA costs. Ours isn't." | "Affordable and accessible education for all." |
| "No 2:1? Bring your experience — we have a route for that." | "Our diverse intake criteria ensure inclusivity." |
| "12–15 months. That's the finish line, not a promise we'll walk back." | "A journey of transformation awaits." |
| Names: "Admissions team", "Prof. Khan", "our faculty" | "Our passionate team of dedicated educators" |

**Sentence rhythm:** Short. Then longer when the point needs evidence. Short again for the CTA. (Mirror of the existing MLP's editorial voice — keep the site coherent.)

## 5. CTA Language System

| Role | Label (recommended) | Alternate (A/B) |
|---|---|---|
| Primary conversion | **Start my enquiry** | "Book my free admissions call" |
| Secondary | **Download prospectus** | "See all programmes" |
| Conversational rail | **WhatsApp admissions** | "Chat with admissions" |
| Pricing section | **Get my fee plan** | "Talk fees with admissions" |
| Final CTA | **Reserve my place** (intake context) | "Start my enquiry" |

Rules: first person, always an action the user performs, never "Submit" / "Apply now" as first touch ("apply" implies a decision the page hasn't earned yet — reserve it for the post-enquiry flow).
