## 3. PROGRAMME OVERVIEW (description)

### copy
General MBAs teach a bit of everything and hand you the rest. This one commits. Logistics and supply chain decide whether goods clear Jebel Ali on schedule or burn money in a yard, so the degree starts with the management core every MBA carries, then spends six courses on procurement, freight, distribution, risk, and supply chain design.

The shape suits people with jobs. A course lasts about three weeks, classes sit in the evening, and the assignments copy operating problems you will recognise from work. It ends with a capstone on a live supply chain issue. A lot of students borrow the problem from their own employer, and a lot of those projects come up in appraisals.

## 7. PROGRAMME STRUCTURE (structure)

### heading
Your journey, stage by stage

### intro
Four stages. The first two build the management core, the third is the specialization, and the fourth is the proof.

### stages

**Stage 1 · Management foundations**
- Managerial Economics
- Managerial Skills
- Accounting for Financial Reporting
- Accounting for Managerial Decisions
- Business Analytics
- Financial Management

**Stage 2 · Business core**
- Operations Management: Productivity & Quality
- International Business Environment
- Leadership and Managing Human Capital
- Marketing Management
- Financial Statement Analysis
- Corporate Social Responsibility, Ethics & Law
- Strategic Management: Integrating the Enterprise

**Stage 3 · The logistics specialization**
- Fundamentals of Logistics and Supply Chain
- International Logistics
- Risk Analysis and Assessment
- Distribution and Freight Logistics
- Supply Chain Modelling and Design
- Procurement
- Five electives from nine, among them Green Supply Chain, Sustainability in Logistics, Lean Logistics, and Humanitarian Logistics

**Stage 4 · The capstone**
- Capstone Project on a live supply chain challenge
