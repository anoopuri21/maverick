## 3. PROGRAMME OVERVIEW (description)

### copy
Most MBAs teach business in general and hope the rest follows on its own. This one picks a side. Logistics and supply chain decide whether goods move through Jebel Ali on time or sit in a yard costing money, and the degree is built around that reality: the full management core of an MBA first, then six courses that go properly deep on procurement, freight, distribution, risk, and supply chain design.

The shape of it suits a working week. Each course lasts about three weeks, classes sit in the evenings, and the assignments copy operating problems rather than textbook ones. It ends with a capstone on a live supply chain issue, and plenty of students take the problem from their own employer, which tends to go down well at appraisal time.

## 7. PROGRAMME STRUCTURE (structure)

### heading
Your journey, stage by stage

### intro
Four stages, each one leaning on the last. The first two carry the management core any MBA is expected to have, the third is the logistics specialism, and the fourth is where you prove the whole thing on a live problem.

### stages

**Stage 1 · Management foundations**
- Managerial Economics
- Managerial Skills
- Accounting for Financial Reporting
- Accounting for Managerial Decisions
- Business Analytics
- Financial Management

**Stage 2 · Business core**
- Operations Management: Productivity & Quality
- International Business Environment
- Leadership and Managing Human Capital
- Marketing Management
- Financial Statement Analysis
- Corporate Social Responsibility, Ethics & Law
- Strategic Management: Integrating the Enterprise

**Stage 3 · The logistics specialism**
- Fundamentals of Logistics and Supply Chain
- International Logistics
- Risk Analysis and Assessment
- Distribution and Freight Logistics
- Supply Chain Modelling and Design
- Procurement
- Five electives from nine, among them Green Supply Chain, Sustainability in Logistics, Lean Logistics, and Humanitarian Logistics

**Stage 4 · The capstone**
- Capstone Project on a live supply chain challenge
