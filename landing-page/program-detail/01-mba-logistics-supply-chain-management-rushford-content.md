## 3. PROGRAMME OVERVIEW (description)

### copy
The MBA in Logistics & Supply Chain Management builds the management core of a full MBA, then goes deep on the function that keeps Gulf trade moving: procurement, freight, distribution, risk, and supply chain design.

Each course runs around three weeks, so the degree fits around a full-time role. Projects simulate live operating situations, and the programme closes with a capstone applied to a real supply chain challenge, often inside your own company.
