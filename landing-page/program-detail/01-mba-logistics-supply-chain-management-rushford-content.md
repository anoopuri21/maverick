## 3. PROGRAMME OVERVIEW (description)

### copy
Every product that moves through Jebel Ali, every same-day delivery in Dubai, and every factory line in the Gulf depends on the same thing: a supply chain that works. This MBA trains you to run that supply chain, from procurement and freight to modelling, risk, and strategy.

The programme is built for working professionals. Each course runs around three weeks, classes sit in the evenings and on weekends, and the curriculum moves from management foundations through a full logistics and supply chain specialization. Projects simulate real operating situations, so you practise decisions before you make them at work.

It ends with a capstone on a live supply chain challenge, typically one from your own employer. Graduates leave with a 90 ECTS MBA awarded by Rushford Business School, Switzerland, and with work they can point to in their next performance review.

## 7. PROGRAMME STRUCTURE (structure)

### intro
27 courses of around three weeks each, plus a capstone, add up to 90 ECTS. The path builds from management foundations to a full logistics specialization.

### stages

**Stage 1 · Management foundations**
- Managerial Economics
- Managerial Skills
- Accounting for Financial Reporting
- Accounting for Managerial Decisions
- Business Analytics
- Financial Management

**Stage 2 · Business core**
- Operations Management: Productivity & Quality
- International Business Environment
- Leadership and Managing Human Capital
- Marketing Management
- Financial Statement Analysis
- Corporate Social Responsibility, Ethics & Law
- Strategic Management: Integrating the Enterprise

**Stage 3 · Logistics & Supply Chain specialization**
- Fundamentals of Logistics and Supply Chain
- International Logistics
- Risk Analysis and Assessment
- Distribution and Freight Logistics
- Supply Chain Modelling and Design
- Procurement
- Plus 5 electives chosen from 9, including Green Supply Chain, Sustainability in Logistics, Lean Logistics, and Humanitarian Logistics

**Stage 4 · Capstone**
- Capstone Project: a supervised project applied to a live supply chain challenge
