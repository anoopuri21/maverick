## 3. PROGRAM OVERVIEW (description)

### copy
The Bachelor of Business Administration from Rushford Business School is built for people who want the full picture of how a company works before they specialise in any one corner of it. The curriculum moves from management foundations through marketing, finance, business economics, human resources, and operations, and finishes with the strategic subjects: project management, customer management, logistics, and strategic management itself.

Along the way it builds the professional habits that employers actually screen for: communication, teamwork, problem-solving, and decision-making with real information. The degree suits school leavers heading straight into business careers, working professionals formalising what they already do, and founders who want the discipline behind the instinct. It is taught entirely online with live, interactive lectures, and it is dual certified with Maverick Business Academy, London.

## 9. WHY GCC STUDENTS CHOOSE THIS COURSE (gcc_reasons)

### gcc_heading
Why GCC students and professionals choose this BBA

### cards

**01 · Study from the Gulf, no relocation**
One hundred percent online with live lectures. No visa, no flight, no gap year, and the salary or family routine keeps running.

**02 · The degree premium is written into UAE policy**
MoHRE's 2026 guidelines recommend AED 12,000 a month for university graduates against AED 7,000 for diploma holders, and federal programs such as Nafis place bachelor's holders in a higher support tier. Policy reads the degree one rung up.

**03 · Online changes the cost equation**
Campus bachelor's programs in Dubai run AED 35,000 to 65,000 a year, and premium international BBAs are listed between AED 33,000 and 110,000. An online Swiss BBA changes what the family budget has to carry.

**04 · The duration is the credible one**
20 to 24 months sits with the 2025 ministry position that recognized degrees must follow approved program structures, and those suspiciously short 12-month offers may not meet that test.

**05 · Generalists fit the Gulf's diversified economy**
With the UAE's non-oil sector driving growth, employers keep hiring people who can move between operations, marketing, HR, and finance.

**06 · The bachelor's is a step, not a dead end**
Rushford's own postgraduate family picks up from here: 12 MBA specializations and 9 MSc routes, and many students plan both degrees in one counselling call.

Honest take: a general business BBA opens the widest spread of first roles, and the narrowest path to any single one. If you already know you want finance only or marketing only, the specialised BBAs will aim you faster; this one is for people keeping options open.
