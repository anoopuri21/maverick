## 3. PROGRAMME OVERVIEW (description)

### copy
The BBA in Human Resource Management combines core business and management subjects with specialised HR modules, building broad business knowledge together with focused HR expertise. That balance matters: the people function only makes good decisions when it understands the business it serves, and this programme teaches both halves from year one.

The first two years cover the management foundations every Rushford BBA carries, from communications and leadership to financial awareness, change management, and business operations. The specialization then adds strategic human resource management, talent management and development, and organisational behaviour with cross-cultural HRM, which in a workforce as international as the Gulf's is the daily reality rather than an elective topic.

## 9. WHY GCC STUDENTS CHOOSE THIS COURSE (gcc_reasons)

### gcc_heading
Why GCC students and professionals choose this BBA

### cards

**01 · Emiratisation made HR a compliance function**
Under Cabinet Resolution No. 18 of 2022, private firms with 50 or more staff must grow their Emirati skilled workforce annually, and MOHRE monitors it with penalties for non-compliance. Someone trained has to run that file.

**02 · Talent is the scarcest thing in the Gulf**
With expatriates making up roughly 87% of Dubai's population, attraction and retention are the business, and nine in ten GCC employers still report skills gaps, per Taggd's 2026 study.

**03 · Cross-cultural HR is the local context**
A workforce drawn from dozens of nationalities makes cross-cultural HRM a daily practice here, not an abstract module.

**04 · No career break required**
Fully online with live lectures from anywhere in the GCC, so working professionals progress academically without pausing anything.

**05 · People roles are professionalising**
Retention surveys keep pushing companies to formalise HR, which means hiring people with structured training rather than generalists who picked it up on the job.

**06 · Progression stays open**
The degree supports HR, talent, and management roles now, and postgraduate study later, including Rushford's own MBA in Human Resource Management.

Honest take: entry-level HR roles hire for communication and organisation as much as credentials, so treat the capstone-style work and internships alongside the degree as part of the plan. The qualification opens the door; your people skills keep it open.
