## 3. PROGRAM OVERVIEW (description)

### copy
By the time finance professionals reach their thirties, they are excellent at one lane and expected to comment on all of them. This Executive MBA widens the view deliberately. The executive core covers strategy, operations, human capital, and leadership, then the coursework goes deep on the global side of the business: international banking structures, cross-border finance, risk and regulation as they actually operate, and the management questions that appear when money moves across jurisdictions.

Cohorts are small and senior, drawn from banks, insurers, fintechs, and corporate treasury functions, which turns the case discussions into something close to a working group. The degree closes with a graduate research project, and participants with access to institutional data tend to produce the most interesting ones.

## 7. PROGRAM STRUCTURE (structure)

### heading
Your journey, stage by stage

### intro
Three movements: the executive core, the global banking and finance coursework, and the graduate research project.

### stages

**Stage 1 · The executive core**
- Strategic management and business economics
- Financial management and human resource management
- Operations and management information systems

**Stage 2 · Global banking and finance coursework**
- International banking structures and cross-border finance
- Risk, regulation, and governance in financial institutions
- Strategic case work across banking and finance markets

**Stage 3 · The graduate research project**
- A supervised investigation of a banking or finance question, often drawn from the participant's own institution

## 9. WHY GCC PROFESSIONALS CHOOSE THIS COURSE (gcc_reasons)

### gcc_heading
Why GCC professionals choose this course

### cards

**01 · Study inside a market measured in trillions**
Fully online, no visa, no career break. UAE bank assets passed AED 4.1 trillion in 2024, and the senior hiring that follows it runs from mid-level to C-suite.

**02 · Regulation made breadth mandatory**
Post-2024 tightening in compliance, risk, and governance means senior finance roles now screen for leaders who understand the whole institution, which is the degree's design.

**03 · Islamic finance keeps its own growth curve**
The Gulf remains the center of Islamic banking, and the institutions running it want leaders with both conventional and Sharia-compliant literacy.

**04 · Fintech raised the competitive bar**
Banks and fintechs bid for the same senior talent, and the market pays for hybrid leaders who understand both sides.

The executive format assumes you have a diary that belongs to other people, and schedules around it.
