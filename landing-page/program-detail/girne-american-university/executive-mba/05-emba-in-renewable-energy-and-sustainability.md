## 3. PROGRAMME OVERVIEW (description)

### copy
The energy transition is not a policy document; it is a capital allocation problem with a deadline, and it needs executives who can hold both halves of that sentence. This Executive MBA is built for exactly them. The executive core covers strategy, finance, human capital, and operations, then the coursework focuses on renewable energy economics, sustainability strategy, the policy frameworks shaping investment, and the management questions that appear when utilities, developers, and corporates all change business models at once.

Cohorts are small and senior, drawing from energy, utilities, construction, and corporate sustainability functions, which turns the case discussions into something close to a sector working group. The degree closes with a graduate research project, and participants close to live transition projects tend to produce the sharpest ones.

- Renewable energy economics and sustainability strategy

**Stage 3 · The graduate research project**
