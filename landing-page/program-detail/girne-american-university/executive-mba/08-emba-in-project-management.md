## 3. PROGRAM OVERVIEW (description)

### copy
At senior level, project management stops being about tools and becomes about judgment: what to promise, what to escalate, and how to keep a program credible when the ground moves. This Executive MBA works at that level. The executive core covers strategy, finance, human capital, and operations, then the coursework focuses on the disciplines that decide delivery outcomes: governance and stakeholder management, risk and commercial control, leading multi-team programs, and the organizational politics every large project actually runs on.

Cohorts are small and senior, drawing from construction, technology, consulting, and public sector delivery, which turns the case discussions into something like a program board with better coffee. The degree closes with a graduate research project, and participants running live programs tend to make it count.
