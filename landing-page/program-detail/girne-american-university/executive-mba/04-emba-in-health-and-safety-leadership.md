## 3. PROGRAM OVERVIEW (description)

### copy
Health and safety used to be a compliance folder. In the Gulf's construction, energy, healthcare, and logistics sectors, it is now a leadership function with board exposure. This Executive MBA treats it that way. The executive core covers strategy, finance, human capital, and operations, then the coursework turns each of those toward safety leadership: building cultures where people report instead of hide, managing risk at the level where money and regulation meet, and running the systems that keep large workforces alive and well.

Cohorts are small and senior, drawing from operations, QHSE, healthcare, and facilities leadership, which makes the case discussions sound like an incident review with better manners. The degree closes with a graduate research project, frequently aimed at a live problem in the participant's own operation.
