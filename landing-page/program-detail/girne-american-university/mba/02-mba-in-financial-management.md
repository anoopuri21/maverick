## 3. PROGRAMME OVERVIEW (description)

### copy
Financial management sits in an odd position: technical enough to need real coursework, broad enough to belong inside an MBA. This degree answers both. The management core covers the business around the numbers, and the financial coursework goes into analysis, planning, control, and the decisions that sit on top of all of it, with a graduate research project at the end where you investigate a financial question of your own choosing.

Girne American University runs the qualification flexibly, built for part-time and distance study, which matters because the people this degree serves are usually mid-quarter, mid-audit, or mid-close. The twelve to fifteen months bend around that instead of fighting it.

## 7. PROGRAMME STRUCTURE (structure)

### heading
Your journey, stage by stage

### intro
Three movements: the business core, the financial management coursework, and the graduate research project.

### stages

**Stage 1 · The business core**
- Economics, accounting, and the management fundamentals
- Business analytics and the international business environment
- Operations, marketing, and leadership

**Stage 2 · Financial management coursework**
- Financial analysis, planning, and control
- Funding, investment, and risk decisions
- Financial management case work across industries

**Stage 3 · The graduate research project**
- A supervised investigation of a financial question, often drawn from the student's own organisation

## 9. WHY GCC PROFESSIONALS CHOOSE THIS COURSE (gcc_reasons)

### gcc_heading
Why GCC professionals choose this course

### cards

**01 · Study inside a market measured in trillions**
UAE bank assets passed AED 4.1 trillion in 2024, and the hiring runs from mid-level upward. Fully online study means you stay inside that market while you qualify.

**02 · Regulation raised the credential bar**
Post-2024 tightening in compliance, risk, and governance means finance employers screen harder for formal qualifications. This MBA clears the screen.

**03 · Fintech bids for the same people**
Banks and fintechs compete for hybrids who understand finance and the technology around it, and that demand keeps formal qualifications scarce.

**04 · The skills gap is documented**
Across the Gulf, employers keep reporting shortages of qualified candidates, with risk and treasury among the tightest functions.

**05 · Fifteen months suits finance calendars**
Close periods and audit seasons do not allow sabbaticals. The degree's upper bound is fifteen months, and the delivery bends around the busy weeks.

**06 · The research project can target your own books**
Point the final investigation at a question from your employer's finances and finish with an analysis worth presenting, which several graduates have done.
