## 3. PROGRAM OVERVIEW (description)

### copy
International business fails in predictable places: a contract clause nobody read, a marketing campaign translated without thinking, an HR policy that collides with another country's labor law. This MBA works through exactly those failure points. The management core comes first, then coursework aimed at international trade operations, global marketing, human resource management across jurisdictions, and the leadership strategies that hold it together, with a graduate research project closing the degree.

The delivery is flexible by design, built for part-time and distance study, which suits the profile it serves: managers whose roles already span markets, and founders taking a Gulf company abroad for the first time.
