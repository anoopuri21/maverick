## 3. PROGRAMME OVERVIEW (description)

### copy
Sustainability roles used to sit in communications. They sit in strategy now, and they need people who can hold economics, policy, and environmental science in one argument. This MSc is built for that: five core management courses first, then ten courses specific to sustainability and environmental management, moving from development economics and the international business environment through advanced microeconomics and macroeconomics to trade and finance.

The degree is asynchronous on Rushford's e-Campus, which means the material fits around shifts, travel, and deadlines rather than fighting them. Fifteen courses of four ECTS each, one year start to finish, with research methodology and business analytics inside the leadership block.

Three blocks across fifteen courses: core management, the sustainability foundation, and the leadership block that carries the research and analytics training.
