| · |## 5. Answer plan (T5): questions, homes, capsules
| · |
| · |Every capsule below is 30–60 words, self-contained, and sits in the first 200 words of its
| · |section. The FAQ homes mirror them 1:1 for FAQPage schema.
| · |
| · || Question | Home | Capsule (draft, final words in 08) |
| · ||---|---|---|
| · || Is an online MBA recognised in the UAE? | Trust + FAQ #1 | Since 10 March 2025, MoHESR recognises online and distance degrees from institutions accredited in their home country, verified through Dataflow or QuadraBay in about 30 working days. For private-sector work, MOHRE accepts accredited online degrees. Government roles and regulated professions also need the formal Recognition Report. |
| · || What are MBA fees in the UAE? | Fees + FAQ #2 | Campus MBAs in Dubai run about AED 66,000 to AED 87,255 a year. Maverick's online routes sit in a lower band, quoted per programme in AED with instalment options. Ask an advisor for the fee plan for your route. |
| · || Can I do an MBA while working full time? | Why + FAQ #3 | Yes. The programmes are built around a working week: online sessions, weekend-friendly timetables and assignment-based assessment, so your salary and your study run together. |
| · || Do I need a student visa? | Hero + FAQ #4 | No. Online routes need no student visa and no relocation. You study from Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah or anywhere in the GCC, and your degree comes from the awarding university. |
| · || When is the next intake? | Journey + FAQ #5 | Intakes rotate through the year, including the September 2026 intake. You do not have to wait for a single annual start; an advisor maps the nearest start to your notice period. |
| · || What are the admission requirements? | Journey + FAQ #6 | In most cases a bachelor's degree plus academic documents. Requirements vary by university and programme; some routes value work experience in place of tests. Send an enquiry and get the exact list for your route. |
| · || Is a fast-track MBA safe? | Compare + FAQ #7 | Treat speed with care. MoHESR's 2025 recognition conditions apply to accredited institutions and approved study modes, so check the awarding body and the recognition route before you buy any 12-month promise. |
| · || Are scholarships available? | Fees + FAQ #8 | Offer and scholarship positions change by intake and programme. Ask an advisor about what applies to your intake; we never publish aid we cannot confirm. |
| · || Which specialisations exist? | MBA section + FAQ #9 | Twelve MBA specialisations at Rushford plus six MBAs and sixteen EMBAs at GAU, across finance, marketing, HR, healthcare, logistics, sustainability, real estate and more. |
| · || How is this different from a campus MBA? | Compare + FAQ #10 | Same award, different rhythm. Online removes the commute, the visa and the career break; campus adds physical attendance. The comparison table shows the trade line by line. |
