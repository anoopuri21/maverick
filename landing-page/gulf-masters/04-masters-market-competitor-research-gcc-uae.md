**Rejected facts (do not reuse):** "3.2M+ online learners by 2026", "95% employer acceptance",
"40% average cost saving" (SRS Group, 2026-05). Agency marketing figures with no official
basis, same class as the rejected "237,000+" figure in the bachelors pack.

**Implications (decisions, not observations):**
1. The MBA is the demand peak of the whole Maverick catalogue. The page leads with MBA, Master's rides second.
2. The buyer is employed. Every section answers "can I do this without quitting?" before "what does it cost?".
3. Indian-expat weight (MG3) justifies one INR parenthetical in fees and one FAQ for Indian residents, nothing more.
4. The fee story writes itself: campus MBAs cost AED 66k–175k+; the honest online band is a fraction. Use dated campus anchors, never invent our number beyond the client-authored range.
5. September 2026 is the market's intake moment (MG9). Our rotational intakes turn that into an advantage: "you do not have to wait for September."

| IMT Dubai | 1-yr MBA | AED 85,000 listed (studyfromuae, 2026-05) | Campus, total cost near AED 95k with fees |

### Tier B · Online-MBA agents (the trust swamp)

Masterei, LUBM, Exeed, Eaton, Westford and similar: they dominate the "online MBA Dubai/UAE"
SERP. Pattern observed in `01-keyword-research-gcc-uae.md` (2026-09): awarding bodies buried,
"UK-validated" claims without the university named, no physical academy behind the page.
Closest like-for-like anchor: Masterei "UK MBA" at AED 22,000 (two stages, QUALIFI + top-up).

### Tier C · Fast-track mills

12-month "pass guaranteed" MBAs at giveaway prices. The recognition risk is the story; we
criticise the category, sourced to R2, never by name.

### Tier D · Directories and content farms

educations.com (54 online MBA listings for UAE, 56 for Sharjah, observed 2026-09), coursetakers.ae,
bachelorsportal, Fateh, AECC. They own the listing, not the learner.

**The gap Maverick owns (postgraduate version):** nobody credible combines named awarding
universities + a real academy since 2012 + no student visa + weekend-tolerant delivery + AED
instalments + a walk-in Sharjah office + parent-and-board-safe recognition content.
