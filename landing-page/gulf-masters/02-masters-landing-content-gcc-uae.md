## §4 Problem — "The Dubai MBA market has two extremes"

**Label:** THE PROBLEM
**H2:** The Dubai MBA market has two extremes. You deserve a third.

**Lead:** At one end, premium campus MBAs in Dubai cost AED 87,000 to 190,000 a year and ask for a 2:1, a GMAT, and residential weekends that take you to other cities. At the other end, "fast-track, pass-guaranteed" mills sell twelve-month MBAs from universities your employer has never heard of. In between, the Gulf's working professionals pay real money — and still ask: *does this actually count?*

**Pain rails (numbered):**
1. **The fee.** Premium Dubai campus MBAs run AED 87k–190k per year. That's the annual salary you were supposed to grow.
2. **The clock.** 24–36 months, with workshop residentials that mean travel — Dubai to Manchester, Hong Kong, Singapore — mid-career, mid-Ramadan, mid-everything.
3. **The gate.** A 2:1 or a GMAT or a maturity test, plus three years of "strong progression" — and the quiet implication that your twelve years in the Gulf don't count the same way.
4. **The trust swamp.** "Fast-track." "Pass guaranteed." Dual certificates from universities nobody can verify. Employers across the GCC know the difference — and it costs you the moment they ask.
5. **The home-country question.** "Will this count when I go back?" Attestation, embassy verification, credential evaluation — nobody tells you how.

**Transition line:** Maverick was built for the third option: a master's that's **university-awarded**, a price that's **honest**, a schedule that respects a 5–9 in the Gulf, and a recognition story you can verify **before** you pay a dirham.

## §13 FAQ — "The Gulf objection floor" (10 Q&As)

1. **Is an online MBA recognised in the UAE?**
Your master's is **awarded by a partner university** (for example, the University of Gloucestershire, UK) — not by a local institute. Multinational employers across the UAE accept MBAs from reputable UK universities; where a specific employer or process needs more, we provide the attestation paperwork (UK apostille/MOFA plus home-country embassy attestation) and walk you through it before you enrol. `[VERIFY attestation wording with client]`

2. **Will my degree be recognised in my home country (India, Pakistan, Philippines, Egypt…)?**
Yes — with the right paperwork. UK-awarded and partner-university degrees are universally attested through the standard route (UK apostille/MOFA, then embassy attestation/credential evaluation in your home country). Our admissions team prepares the document list for your specific country before you start, so there are no surprises when you go back.

3. **Do I need a student visa, or to relocate to the UK?**
No. Online routes need no student visa and no relocation. Learners study from Dubai, Sharjah, Abu Dhabi, Riyadh, Doha, and Asia. We have a London headquarters and a regional office in Sharjah (Robot Park Tower).

4. **Can I study while working a full-time job in the Gulf?**
That's the design brief. Weekend and Saturday classes, hybrid delivery, and a support team in Dubai time zone exist precisely for professionals with a 5–9 — including Ramadan-season schedules.

5. **Do I need a GMAT or a 2:1?**
Most Maverick routes do not require a GMAT `[VERIFY]`. Three routes in: a recognised bachelor's degree; an OFQUAL-recognised Postgraduate Diploma (120 credits) plus 3–4 years' managerial experience; or a school-leaving certificate with English as medium of instruction, progressing up the ladder. Your advisor confirms your route on the eligibility call — free.

6. **How long does it actually take?**
MBA programmes typically run 10–18 months; MSc programmes 12–18 months. Rotational intakes mean you start when you're ready — your offer states your start date.

7. **How do fees and instalments work in AED?**
Final fees are confirmed in writing on your offer, in AED or GBP, and instalment plans are available across the master's families. There's no cost to enquire — the fee plan is the first thing you'll see after your call.

8. **Is this a "fast-track" degree?**
No — and it's worth the question. We don't sell "pass-guaranteed" or 6-month miracle MBAs. Our master's sit in standard 180-credit frameworks, with case-study, project, and assignment assessment, named faculty, and recognition you can verify on the Ofqual register or with the awarding university. Fast is a finish date in 10–18 months, not a shortcut through the standard.

9. **I might relocate mid-programme (Dubai → Riyadh, or back home). What happens?**
Your studies travel with you. The programme runs on an online platform with weekend-friendly scheduling; a relocation doesn't reset your credits. That's the same promise we make to learners who move between the Gulf and Asia.

10. **Which MBA or MSc should I choose?**
That's the 15-minute call. Tell us your field and your goal — leadership, finance, HR, operations, data — and your advisor shortlists the programmes that match. "Not sure yet" is a real option on the form, and a common starting point.
