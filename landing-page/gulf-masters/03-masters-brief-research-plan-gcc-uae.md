## 5. Strategy (locked direction)

**Positioning, one line:** a university-awarded online MBA or Master's for Gulf professionals,
without a student visa or a career break, from a London academy with a Sharjah office since 2012.

**Message order (recognition before price, people before the form):**
1. Hero: the offer + the local stack (no visa, weekend-friendly, AED instalments, Sharjah office).
2. Trust: recognition anchors (named awarding bodies, accreditations, office, history).
3. Programme overview, then why-Maverick, then the MBA families, then Master's by university.
4. Class profile + community (proof of real professionals).
5. Fees with honest AED framing and the comparison context.
6. Career outcomes, alumni, partners.
7. Learning format, then the two re-enabled sections: the 4-step journey (enquire → match →
   enrol → study) and the flexible-vs-traditional comparison.
8. FAQ as the AEO spine (12 answers, one per real question). Final CTA with the form.

**Fixes inherited from the audit (P0, all applied in `08`):**
- Zero mechanical defects ("programmed", "memes", broken sentences).
- One duration statement per family, consistent everywhere.
- Recognition story present from section 2 (Ofqual/QUALIFI for diplomas, university-awarded
  degrees, GAU and RBS accreditations, London HQ + Sharjah office, since 2012).
- No-visa line in the hero and FAQ. Weekend angle, AED framing, Sharjah office, intake line.
- Keyword stuffing cut: each of the 15 phrases gets exactly one home; no verbatim-repeated blocks.
- First-touch CTA softened ("Start my enquiry" / "Get my programme & fee plan"); "Apply Now"
  only on the sticky bar and post-consideration positions.
- Awarding body named explicitly per route, including the UCA + Rushford Global MBA.
- All stats kept only as client-authored, flagged for written verification in `09`.

**Compliance rails (G06):** no "best/top/#1", no salary promises, no visa guarantees, no
invented testimonials, recognition phrasing word-exact to the R-pack, degrees and diplomas
never blurred.
