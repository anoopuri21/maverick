## Homepage FAQ Set (8 questions)

<!-- Target keyword: "what is maverick business academy" | Branded hub -->
**Q. What is Maverick Business Academy?**

Maverick Business Academy is a **global education platform** connecting students and
working professionals worldwide with **internationally recognised programmes** — from
bachelor's and master's degrees to doctoral research and professional diplomas — delivered
through established partner universities and awarding bodies. One team, one journey:
guidance from your first enquiry to graduation.

<!-- Target keyword: "how does maverick partner university model work" | Trust/model -->
**Q. How does Maverick work with its partner universities?**

Maverick partners with **established universities and regulated awarding bodies**, and your
qualification is **awarded directly by the partner institution** — carrying that
institution's name, recognition and accreditations. Maverick's role is the journey around
the award: programme guidance, eligibility review, enrolment support and student success
services. <!-- [VERIFY: approved public wording for Maverick's positioning vs partners.] -->

<!-- Target keyword: "which programmes can i study online business" | Portfolio orientation -->
**Q. Which types of programmes can I study through Maverick?**

The portfolio covers the full qualification ladder:

- **Bachelor's degrees** (BBA, BSc, BA routes — including fast-track top-up options)
- **Master's programmes** (MBA, Executive MBA, MSc, LLM — including a dual-award Global MBA)
- **Doctoral research** (PhD, DBA pathways)
- **Professional diplomas** (Level 3 to Level 7)
- **Educational travel** through Maverick Edutainment

Whatever stage you're at, there is a defined next rung.

<!-- Target keyword: "can i study from anywhere in the world online" | Global access -->
**Q. Can I study with Maverick from anywhere in the world?**

Yes. Programmes are built for a **global audience**, with flexible online and blended
learning models that let you study from wherever you live and work. Students worldwide
join the same programmes, earn the same awards, and receive the same support — location is
not a barrier.

<!-- Target keyword: "who awards the degree certificate" | #1 trust objection at brand level -->
**Q. Who actually awards the degree or diploma I receive?**

The **partner university or regulated awarding body** — never Maverick itself. Your
certificate carries the awarding institution's name and stands on that institution's
recognition and accreditations, verifiable directly with the awarding body.
<!-- [VERIFY: confirm this framing matches all current partner agreements.] -->

<!-- Target keyword: "how to choose the right programme for me" | Guidance/funnel -->
**Q. How do I choose the right programme for my goals?**

Start with a **free consultation** — share your academic background, work experience and
career direction, and the advisory team maps you to the right level and programme
(including honest advice if a different rung of the ladder fits better). You never have to
decode qualification frameworks alone. <!-- [VERIFY: consultation positioning ("free") in
current marketing.] -->

<!-- Target keyword: "are the programmes accredited recognised" | Accreditation approach -->
**Q. Are the programmes accredited and recognised?**

Each programme carries the **accreditations and recognition of its awarding institution**
— including internationally accredited business schools and awarding organisations
regulated on formal qualification frameworks. Details for every partner are published on
our Accreditations page, and the admissions team can advise on recognition for your
specific goals. <!-- [VERIFY: cross-check wording against live Accreditations page copy.] -->

<!-- Target keyword: "student support enquiry to graduation" | Support/differentiator -->
**Q. What support will I get from enquiry to graduation?**

End-to-end support is the core of the model: **programme guidance and eligibility review**
before you enrol; **enrolment and documentation help** as you join; **academic and
assignment support, student success tracking and career guidance** while you study; and
**graduation support** at the finish. You are never left to navigate alone.
