**Q. What is Maverick Business Academy?**

Maverick Business Academy helps students and working professionals **explore programmes
and navigate applications through partner universities and awarding organisations**.
The portfolio includes undergraduate, postgraduate, research and professional diploma
routes. The exact award, delivery arrangements and support depend on the programme,
so use an eligibility review to understand the offer that fits your goals.

<!-- Target keyword: "how does maverick partner university model work" | Source: current partner agreements; approved provider files, subject to their route limitations; reviewed 2026-09-07. -->
**Q. How does Maverick work with its partner universities?**

**Maverick provides programme and application guidance around partner-delivered awards.**
The awarding institution and each organisation's responsibilities should be identified in
your offer, including teaching, assessment, registration and student support. An
institution's accreditation does not automatically cover every partner route; check the
programme-specific arrangements before enrolling.

**Q. Which types of programmes can I study through Maverick?**

The supplied portfolio spans **several qualification levels**:

- Bachelor's degrees, including routes that may recognise prior learning
- MBA, Executive MBA, MSc and LLM study, including a linked Global MBA pathway
- Doctoral research routes
- Professional diplomas at Levels 3, 5 and 7
- Educational travel through Maverick Edutainment

Current availability and entry conditions depend on the chosen offer. Moving between
levels is subject to the next programme's requirements.

<!-- Target keyword: "can i study from anywhere in the world online" | Source: inputs/listing.md; provider-specific delivery evidence; reviewed 2026-09-07. -->
**Q. Can I study with Maverick from anywhere in the world?**

**Maverick welcomes enquiries from students worldwide**, and online routes can support
study from where you live and work. Availability still depends on the programme's
admissions, delivery and attendance rules. Blended learning can include in-person
commitments; confirm the actual route, schedule and any access restrictions before
assuming that relocation or travel will never be required.
<!-- [VERIFY: HOME-02 — per-route availability, access and attendance requirements.] -->

<!-- Target keyword: "who awards the degree certificate" | Source: inputs/listing.md; current partner agreements required; reviewed 2026-09-07. -->
**Q. Who actually awards the degree or diploma I receive?**

**The awarding university or organisation should be named in your programme offer.**
For the partner qualifications in this portfolio, confirm the exact award title and
issuer, particularly where a pathway includes more than one credential. Teaching support,
recruitment and awarding responsibilities can differ; ask how your final award can be
verified directly with its issuer.
<!-- [VERIFY: HOME-01 — issuer and responsibility wording across all current agreements.] -->

<!-- Target keyword: "how to choose the right programme for me" | Source: general study/career guidance, not an outcome or admissions guarantee; reviewed 2026-09-07. -->
**Q. How do I choose the right programme for my goals?**

Start with **your existing qualifications, experience and intended next step**. Share
these with the advisory team and compare programmes by subject, entry criteria, study
commitment, total cost and awarding arrangements. If you need a qualification for further
study or a regulated profession, check the receiving organisation's requirements before
you decide.
<!-- [VERIFY: HOME-03 — consultation process and any charges; no free-service claim made.] -->

<!-- Target keyword: "are the programmes accredited recognised" | Source: reviewed provider files; https://alice.ch/en/eduqua/; https://qualifi.net/qualifications/; reviewed 2026-09-07. -->
**Q. Are the programmes accredited and recognised?**

**Recognition and accreditation must be checked for the exact programme and purpose.**
Institutional status, programme accreditation and regulated-diploma approval are different
things; one does not automatically establish the others. Ask for the awarding body and
current programme-specific evidence, then check acceptance with the relevant employer,
professional body or receiving university before relying on it.
<!-- [VERIFY: HOME-01 — current programme-specific evidence and approved public wording.] -->

<!-- Target keyword: "student support enquiry to graduation" | Source: current service commitments required; a single programme seeder is not portfolio-wide evidence; reviewed 2026-09-07. -->
**Q. What support will I get from enquiry to graduation?**

**Confirm the support included at each stage of your chosen programme.** Ask about
programme guidance, eligibility and documentation help before enrolment, then academic,
technical and assessment support during study. If careers or graduation services are
important to you, check who provides them and their scope. Support commitments should be
clear in the offer rather than assumed across all partners.
