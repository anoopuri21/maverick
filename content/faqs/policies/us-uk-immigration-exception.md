**Approval:** owner instruction, 9 September 2026

## What is authorised

Exactly one provider-level clarification in each of:

1. `approved/rushford-business-school.md` — “Does completing a Rushford Business School
   programme make me eligible to migrate to the US or UK?”
2. `approved/girne-american-university.md` — “Does completing a Girne American University
   programme make me eligible to migrate to the US or UK?”

Both live under **Immigration & Visa Eligibility**. The tokens **US** and **UK** are
permitted inside these exact questions and their answers only. This does not relax the
country-neutral rule elsewhere in either file, on another provider/page, or for another
destination. Existing full institutional-name exceptions remain unchanged.

The clarification applies across each provider's supplied programme categories. It does
not establish that every listed award is available, recognised or accepted for immigration.
The owner authorised this two-provider content batch; no intervening provider-by-provider
approval wait is required for these additions.

## Required content boundaries

- Programme completion is not, on its own, immigration eligibility or immigration approval.
- Do not imply that all graduates are barred from every visa route either.
- Separate academic evidence, study transfer, work permission, post-study work and
  permanent residence. Evaluate the actual award and individual route where required.
- Do not imply all work/residence routes require employer sponsorship; exceptions such
  as a qualifying national interest waiver have their own conditions.
- No guaranteed visa, job, residence or citizenship; no universal degree equivalence;
  no institution-wide credential-evaluator approval or migration-success percentages.
- eduQua/provider certification, memberships, credits and partner names are not visa
  decisions. A historical transfer announcement is not current pathway confirmation.
- Use current official immigration sources; attribute provider material as provider
  context, not an immigration authority's finding. Date any award-period list finding.
- Seek appropriately qualified immigration advice before relying on a programme for an
  individual migration plan.

Each answer must contain this disclaimer:

> Immigration and residency decisions rest solely with the relevant authorities. This
> information is general guidance, not immigration advice.

Evaluators may assess qualifications; they do not share the authority's visa/residence
approval power. This wording intentionally keeps those responsibilities separate.
