## Category 1 — Master of Laws (LLM)
*Single-programme category.*

### General Information

<!-- Target keyword: "uow master of laws llm online" | Source: inputs/listing.md + partner description -->
**Q. What is the Master of Laws (LLM) from the University of Wolverhampton?**

The Master of Laws (LLM) is a **postgraduate law degree awarded by the University of
Wolverhampton (UOW)**, delivered through **flexible online learning**. It deepens your
command of legal analysis, research and argument — a recognised master's-level credential
for legal and compliance careers worldwide.

<!-- Target keyword: "llm top up route level 7 diploma" | Source: partner description — top-up via L7 diploma, ~6 months (third-party) -->
**Q. How does the LLM top-up route work?**

The top-up route lets you convert a **Level 7 postgraduate diploma in a law-related field**
into a full LLM by completing the final master's stage — centred on an **independent legal
research project** — in as little as **around six months**. It is designed for
professionals who have already completed diploma-level legal study and want the full
master's award. <!-- [VERIFY: confirm the current offer is the top-up route, the exact
duration, and accepted feeder diplomas (e.g. our Level 7 Law diplomas).] -->

<!-- Target keyword: "llm without law degree" | Source: mastersportal — law/joint-law degree; non-standard entry with experience (third-party) -->
**Q. Can I do the LLM without a traditional law degree?**

Possibly, yes. Standard entry is via a **law or joint-law honours degree**, but applicants
with **significant professional experience in relevant legal or regulatory areas** may be
considered through a non-standard entry review — sometimes including an interview.
<!-- [VERIFY: non-standard entry policy for the current offer.] --> The admissions team
will assess your profile individually.

<!-- Target keyword: "llm online while working lawyers" | Source: partner description — online/distance for working professionals -->
**Q. Can I complete the LLM while working?**

Yes — the UOW Master of Laws is delivered through **online and distance learning**, built
for **working legal and compliance professionals** — you can study around court schedules,
client work or office hours, from anywhere in the world.

<!-- Target keyword: "is university of wolverhampton recognised" | Source: public-university status; wording to confirm -->
**Q. Is a degree from the University of Wolverhampton recognised?**

Yes. UOW is a **long-established public university** whose degrees are recognised
internationally by employers and academic institutions. <!-- [VERIFY: approved public

### Eligibility & Admission

<!-- Target keyword: "llm entry requirements english exemption" | Source: mastersportal + partner description (third-party) -->
**Q. What are the entry requirements for the LLM?**

Entry depends on your route:

- **Top-up route:** a completed **Level 7 postgraduate diploma** in law or a closely
  related field
- **Standard route:** an honours degree in **law or a joint-law subject**
- **Non-standard route:** significant relevant professional experience, assessed individually

English proficiency evidence is required, with **exemptions available** where your prior
education was completed in English. <!-- [VERIFY: route availability in current offer,
degree classification threshold, and English test/exemption specifics.] -->

### Careers & Outcomes

<!-- Target keyword: "what can i do with an llm career" | Source: generic, compliance-safe framing -->
**Q. What career opportunities can the LLM support?**

The LLM strengthens careers across **legal practice support, corporate counsel teams,
compliance, regulatory affairs, contracts, arbitration support, policy and academia**. For
practising professionals, it signals master's-level expertise; for career-changers, it
opens law-adjacent roles where advanced legal knowledge is valued.

*Please note: the LLM is an academic qualification. Rights to practise law are governed by
the professional regulatory body in each jurisdiction and are not conferred by the degree
itself.*

<!-- Target keyword: "phd in law after llm" | Source: standard progression; GAU PhD Law cross-link -->
**Q. Can I progress to doctoral study after the LLM?**

Yes. The LLM is a full master's-level qualification and satisfies the standard entry route
for **doctoral study in law and related fields** — including PhD programmes — subject to
admission criteria. The independent research component of the LLM is strong preparation for
doctoral-level work.

## Applying & Practical Information
*Provider-level questions. (Phase 2 addition.)*

<!-- Target keyword: "who should do an llm which professionals" | Source: generic audience mapping -->
**Q. Which professionals benefit most from an LLM?**

The LLM pays off fastest for professionals whose work touches law daily without requiring
a practising licence: **compliance and regulatory officers, contract and procurement
managers, HR leaders, company secretaries, risk managers, paralegals** stepping up, and
practising lawyers formalising specialist depth. If interpreting rules, drafting terms or
managing legal exposure is part of your role, the LLM converts that experience into a
recognised master's-level credential.

<!-- Target keyword: "llm independent legal research project" | Source: partner description of top-up route (VERIFY) -->
**Q. What is the independent legal research project like?**

It is the centrepiece of the LLM: you choose a **legal issue relevant to your field**,
analyse it critically under supervision, and propose defensible solutions — the same
skill set senior legal and compliance work demands. Graduates typically pick topics from
their own industry, so the project doubles as evidence of expertise in their day job.
<!-- [VERIFY: project scope, supervision and assessment details in our offer.] -->

<!-- Target keyword: "llm after law degree or work experience first" | Source: guidance angle (generic) -->
**Q. Should I take the LLM straight after my degree, or work first?**

Both routes work — they simply produce different advantages. Coming straight from study,
you carry momentum and research sharpness into the LLM. Coming from practice, your
professional context makes every module and the research project immediately applicable.
The admissions review considers your profile either way, so the honest answer is: start
when the qualification serves your next career move.
