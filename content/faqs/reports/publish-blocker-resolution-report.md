**Updated:** 9 September 2026

## Overview

The programme FAQ collection contains 144 questions across seven providers, alongside 18 homepage and educational-travel questions. It covers 16 programme categories and 136 entries in the programme list. Coverage does not confirm that every award or intake is available, or that a qualification is accepted for every purpose. The sections below distinguish source-supported information from details that require programme-specific confirmation.

## 1. Programme specifications: Rushford and GAU

**Source-supported information.** Rushford's online MBA catalogue lists the general MBA at 12 months/60 ECTS and the listed specialisations at 16 months/90 ECTS. Its MSc catalogue lists core 12-month/60 ECTS routes. A sampled DBA specification requires over five years of managerial or business experience for applicants with a non-business master's. GAU's catalogue identifies Counselling Psychology as an MA; its MSc designation in the programme list requires reconciliation.

**Interpretation.** Duration, credits, entry and language requirements are route-specific. An extra diploma, study extension or doctoral admission condition must not be assumed to apply to every programme.

**Programme details to confirm.** Current BBA specifications; exact doctoral awards and EPD terminology; any enhanced MSc routes; GAU award titles and thesis routes; delivery, admission, intake and payment terms.

## 2. Accreditation, regulation and qualification scope

**Source-supported information.** eduQua certifies continuing-education providers, not individual degrees. GAU identifies named IACBE-accredited business programmes. Gatehouse lists qualification number 610/7539/5 for Education Leadership and Management, coursework/portfolio assessment and an exclusivity agreement. Qualifi identifies individual regulated qualifications, and sampled Extended Level 5 routes carry 240 credits.

**Interpretation.** Institutional recognition, programme accreditation, diploma regulation, credit volume and acceptance for a particular purpose are different checks. One qualification's specifications do not establish the status of an entire category.

**Programme details to confirm.** The current accreditation scope for the selected award and delivery route; the other Gatehouse qualification titles/numbers and authorised delivery arrangements; individual specifications and receiving-university credit-transfer terms.

## 3. UWS: exact Global Business award

**Source-supported information.** The programme list uses BA (Hons) Global Business. UWS's partner list identifies BA Global Business at Level 9, separately from BA (Hons) International Business and Finance. The named partner describes a 12-month, 120-credit Global Business top-up. These sources do not establish the honours designation for the selected Maverick route.

**Interpretation.** The degree title, honours status and credit-entry route must be identified precisely. Experience can contribute to a prior-learning assessment, but does not itself constitute a degree or establish an honours award.

**Programme details to confirm.** The exact course specification, award and certificate title, honours status, accepted prior learning, remaining study, delivery mode and timetable.

## 4. UCA and Rushford Global MBA: pathway and fees

**Source-supported information.** Rushford describes a linked three-award Global MBA pathway, 12–18 months, 90 ECTS and a bachelor's entry route. The admissions landing page states that the former DreamApply portal is retired; its historical prices should not be treated as a current quotation.

**Interpretation.** The credit framework and each awarding stage should be confirmed independently. Linked awards do not imply automatic wider acceptance, and total instalment costs need to be compared with the current upfront quotation.

**Programme details to confirm.** Current partner and stage-registration arrangements; diploma title, awarding body and qualification number; entry and English evidence; thesis supervision; certificates; complete fees and sponsorship/invoicing options.

## 5. UOW: distinguish the LLM routes

**Source-supported information.** LLM Law and LLM Professional Practice (Top up) are different programmes. The professional top-up is advertised as one year part-time/distance and requires specified professional legal qualifications. This does not establish a six-month route accepting any Level 7 law diploma.

**Interpretation.** Entry and delivery must be tied to the exact programme. An LLM is an academic award, not an automatic licence to practise law. A Qualifi-to-LLM progression arrangement requires its own evidence.

**Programme details to confirm.** The selected LLM title and course specification; accepted feeder qualifications and credit recognition; duration, mode, English evidence, research supervision and fees.

## 6. Student support and educational travel

**Source-supported information.** Support, package inclusions, certificates and travel-document assistance can vary by offer. This report does not establish universal supervision ratios, insurance cover or emergency procedures for educational travel.

**Interpretation.** The written programme offer or travel proposal should define included services and responsibilities. Safety, safeguarding, supervision, medical/emergency arrangements and insurance should be documented for the actual participants and itinerary.

**Programme details to confirm.** Programme-specific support commitments; participant and age suitability; safeguarding and risk assessments; supervision and insurance responsibilities; package inclusions/exclusions, certificates, lead times and assistance scope.

## 7. US/UK immigration: GAU and RBS qualifications

**Source-supported information.** UK and US routes have distinct study, immigration-status, employment, qualification and other requirements. Programme completion alone does not establish eligibility. The HPI list for qualifications awarded from 1 November 2025 to 31 October 2026 does not list GAU or RBS; this is not a conclusion about all routes or all other award periods.

**Interpretation.** Post-study work permission, independently qualifying work/residence routes and academic recognition must be distinguished. The GAU/RBS Immigration FAQ Report provides the relevant route explanations and dated source register. No individual qualification-equivalence outcome is established.

**Programme details to confirm.** Current rules for the intended route; the exact award and any required assessment; genuine sponsorship/transfer evidence where applicable; the individual applicant's circumstances and appropriately qualified advice.

## Scope and limitations

- Programme/provider sources were checked on 7 September 2026; the US/UK immigration sources were checked on 9 September 2026. A source date records the evidence assessment, not a promise that terms will remain unchanged.
- The report summarises published information and programme-specific requirements. It does not independently authenticate every qualification or determine its acceptance by an employer, professional regulator, evaluator or university.
- Use current written offers for availability, fees, payment terms, entry requirements and support. Use the actual itinerary and documented arrangements for educational travel.
- Immigration and residency decisions rest with the relevant authorities. General guidance is not individual immigration advice or a guarantee of eligibility.
