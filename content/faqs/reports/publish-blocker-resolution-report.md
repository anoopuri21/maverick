# Publish-Blocker Resolution Report

**Date:** 19 August 2026 · **Prepared by:** Education FAQ Content Team
**Scope:** Deep-research verification and correction of the four publish-blocking issues
identified in the FAQ content pack (7 providers, 117 FAQs). All fixes have been applied to
the approved content files. **Content remains unpublished pending final client approval.**

## Blocker 1 — Programme Durations (Rushford Business School & Girne American University)

**The issue.** Initial drafts relied on third-party directory figures for programme
durations (RBS MBA "12–24 months", RBS MSc "18–24 months", GAU MBA "1.5–2 years"
unverified), and the RBS BBA duration was missing entirely.

**Research findings.**
- The official Rushford admissions portal states a **nominal duration of 16 months
  (90 ECTS)** consistently across MBA specialisation pages.
- Official Rushford MSc pages publish a **three-route structure: 60 ECTS (~12 months),
  90 ECTS (~18 months, with master's thesis), 120 ECTS (~24 months, with capstone
  consulting project)**.
- The official Rushford BBA page publishes **180 ECTS across 6 semesters (~36 months)**.
- GAU master's programmes are consistently described across independent sources as
  **~2 years (4 semesters)**, with shorter completion possible by route — supporting the
  published "1.5–2 years" range.

**Resolution applied.** All duration answers replaced with official figures; BBA duration
added to the content; obsolete third-party ranges removed.

**Sources.** apply.rushford.ch official course pages (MBA specialisations); rushford.ch
official programme pages (MSc Business Management; BBA); corroborating independent
directories for GAU master's duration.

## Blocker 2 — Accreditation & Regulation Wording (GAU, Gatehouse, Qualifi, UWS/UOW/UCA)

**The issue.** (a) It was unconfirmed whether Gatehouse and Qualifi diplomas could be
described as regulated qualifications. (b) GAU's recognising authorities (YÖDAK, YÖK)
carry country references in their full names, conflicting with the project's strict
globally-neutral content policy. (c) University heritage claims (founding dates) were
unverified.

**Research findings.**
- **Gatehouse Awards** is confirmed as an awarding organisation **recognised by Ofqual**
  (Office of Qualifications and Examinations Regulation). Its Level 7 Diploma in Education
  Leadership & Management is verified on the official Gatehouse site as a regulated
  qualification — **QAN 610/7539/5, TQT 1200, assessed by coursework & portfolio of
  evidence**.
- **Qualifi** is confirmed as **approved and regulated by Ofqual (regulator reference
  RN5160)**; its qualifications are accredited to the Regulated Qualifications Framework
  with unique QANs (e.g. Level 5 Extended Diploma in Business Management, QAN 610/1675/5,
  240 credits).
- No safe, verified founding-date claims were required for UWS/UOW/UCA content.

**Resolution applied.** Regulator recognition statements added to Gatehouse and Qualifi
content with exact regulator naming; Gatehouse assessment wording corrected to
"coursework and portfolio of evidence". GAU authorities now referenced country-neutrally
as "the higher-education authorities **YÖDAK and YÖK**" (acronyms only, no country
descriptors), alongside IACBE international accreditation. University heritage retained as
neutral phrasing ("long-established public university") with no dates — the lowest-risk,
fully accurate formulation.

**Sources.** gatehouseawards.org (official qualification specification); regulated-
qualification listings confirming Gatehouse's Ofqual recognition; Qualifi delivery-centre
qualification specifications citing Ofqual reference RN5160 and QANs; Maverick repository
programme data (primary) for GAU's YÖDAK/YÖK/IACBE recognitions.

## Blocker 3 — UWS BA (Hons) Global Business: Top-Up Structure

**The issue.** Three answers (programme meaning, duration, entry requirements) depended on
a top-up-route assumption drawn from a single partner source.

**Research findings.** Three independent partner institutions delivering this UWS award
describe it identically: a **top-up degree awarded by the University of the West of
Scotland**, completed in **~12 months**, delivered **100% online**, with entry via a
**completed HND or equivalent qualification, or relevant work experience (subject to
approval)**.

**Resolution applied.** Top-up structure, 12-month duration and entry criteria firmed in
content; assumption flags removed.

**Sources.** Independent partner-institution programme pages (three separate providers,
consistent details).

## Blocker 4 — UCA Global MBA: Entry-Criteria Conflict

**The issue.** The official Rushford admissions portal lists entry as a bachelor's degree
in any discipline, while another listing of the same UCA award required minimum age 21,
3+ years' management experience and higher English scores.

**Research findings.** The stricter criteria belong to a **different delivery partner's
route** to the same UCA award. Our listed offer is explicitly the **Rushford-delivered
route** ("Global MBA + Rushford Business School"), for which the official portal publishes:
**bachelor's degree (or equivalent) in any discipline**.

**Resolution applied.** Source hierarchy applied — official criteria for our delivery
route retained; clarification recorded in content notes; conflict closed.

**Sources.** apply.rushford.ch official Global MBA (via UCA) course page vs. the alternate
delivery partner's listing.
