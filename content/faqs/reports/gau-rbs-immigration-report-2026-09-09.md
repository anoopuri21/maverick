# US / UK Immigration FAQ Report — GAU & RBS

**Sources reviewed:** 9 September 2026
## 1. Overview

Completing a programme does not, on its own, establish US or UK immigration eligibility. A graduate may qualify through an applicable route if all of its requirements are met. Academic recognition, a visa, post-study work permission and permanent residence are separate matters.

This report explains general US and UK requirements relevant to qualifications from Girne American University and Rushford Business School. It covers the exact award, study arrangements and individual evidence required by the chosen route, without certifying any qualification or applicant as eligible.

This is source-backed general guidance, not confirmation of any individual award equivalence, applicant eligibility or immigration outcome.

## 2. Frequently asked questions

Questions about qualification recognition and immigration eligibility.

### Rushford Business School (RBS)

**Q. Does completing a Rushford Business School programme make me eligible to migrate to the US or UK?**

**No, not from programme completion alone.** A Rushford qualification does not itself
establish eligibility for a US or UK visa or permanent residence. Graduates may qualify
through a separate route if they meet all its requirements. Where academic evidence is
required, acceptance of the exact award must be established; provider certification or a
partner award is not immigration approval.

The **UK Graduate route** requires eligible study, the relevant Student permission and
compliance with UK-study requirements. **US Optional Practical Training (OPT)** is linked
to F-1 status and qualifying study. An overseas or online programme alone does not
establish entitlement to either post-study work option. Work and permanent-residence
routes have separate requirements, which may include qualifying employment, accepted
qualifications or other prescribed evidence.

If migration is your goal, seek appropriately qualified immigration advice on your
individual circumstances and the current rules before choosing a programme.

*Immigration and residency decisions rest solely with the relevant authorities. This information is general guidance, not immigration advice.*

### Girne American University (GAU)

**Q. Does completing a Girne American University programme make me eligible to migrate to the US or UK?**

**Completing a GAU programme does not, by itself, establish US or UK immigration
eligibility.** A graduate may qualify for a visa or permanent residence through an
applicable route, but must meet that route's conditions. Any required assessment must
relate to the exact qualification; academic recognition or a study-transfer arrangement
is not immigration permission.

For the **UK**, sponsored work and the Graduate route have different requirements;
the latter requires eligible study, the relevant Student permission and compliance with
UK-study requirements. For the **US**, work and residence routes require their own
evidence, while **Optional Practical Training (OPT)** is linked to F-1 status and
qualifying study. An overseas or online programme alone does not establish these
post-study work entitlements.

Before relying on a programme for migration plans, have the current route requirements,
award details and your individual circumstances reviewed by an appropriately qualified
immigration adviser.

*Immigration and residency decisions rest solely with the relevant authorities. This information is general guidance, not immigration advice.*

## 3. Evidence and interpretation

### UK: Graduate route is not an automatic benefit of an overseas award

The official guidance requires an eligible course studied with the relevant Student or Tier 4 permission, a qualifying licensed education provider and compliance with the UK-study requirement. Applying also requires being in the UK. A UK partner's name on a qualification or an online programme completed overseas does not, by itself, establish this eligibility.

**What this means:** An overseas or online award alone does not establish Graduate-route eligibility. Historical distance-learning concessions and any qualifying study arrangements must be assessed under the applicable rules; this is not a bar on every online graduate applying through a different visa route.

**Sources:** [1](https://www.gov.uk/graduate-visa/course-you-studied)

### UK: Skilled Worker eligibility depends on a sponsored job and other conditions

The Skilled Worker route requires an approved employer, a certificate of sponsorship, an eligible occupation, the applicable salary and other requirements, including English-language evidence where required. A degree or institution name alone is not enough.

**What this means:** The sponsored job and the applicable immigration conditions determine eligibility. Holding a degree, choosing a university or completing a particular programme does not replace those requirements.

**Sources:** [2](https://www.gov.uk/skilled-worker-visa)

### UK: the HPI university list is specific to the qualification award period

Neither Girne American University nor Rushford Business School appears on the official list for qualifications awarded from 1 November 2025 to 31 October 2026. HPI requires the correct awarding institution on the list for the month and year of the award, an eligible qualification and other conditions. Ecctis assesses qualification validity and level; it does not check university-list eligibility.

**What this means:** The list finding applies only to the stated qualification award period, not to every visa route or every past/future award. A partner-issued qualification requires its own awarding-institution check. The applicable list must match the month and year of the qualification award.

**Sources:** [3](https://www.gov.uk/high-potential-individual-visa/eligibility) [4](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/high-potential-individual-visa-global-universities-list/high-potential-individual-visa-global-universities-list-2025)

### US: H-1B specialty employment and academic evidence are separate tests

For H-1B specialty occupations, the position must meet the specialty-occupation criteria and the individual must meet the qualification requirements. A relevant foreign degree equivalent to the required US degree can be one route; the rules also recognise specified alternative evidence. Employer or agent petitioning and other immigration requirements apply.

**What this means:** The position, the exact qualification or permitted alternative evidence, relevance to the role and any professional authorisation must be assessed individually. A credential evaluation is evidence, not a visa decision.

**Sources:** [5](https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/h-1b-specialty-occupations)

### US: OPT is linked to F-1 status and qualifying study

USCIS describes OPT as practical training for eligible F-1 students with qualifying study at an SEVP-certified institution, subject to the applicable study, recommendation and employment-authorisation requirements. Authorised study-abroad periods can count in specified circumstances; an overseas or online qualification alone does not create OPT entitlement.

**What this means:** OPT depends on F-1 status and qualifying study, including the applicable treatment of authorised study-abroad periods. It is not permanent residence and is not an automatic benefit of an overseas degree.

**Sources:** [6](https://www.uscis.gov/policy-manual/volume-2-part-f-chapter-5)

### US: an advanced degree alone does not establish a Green Card entitlement

EB-2 has advanced-degree and exceptional-ability eligibility provisions. Under the advanced-degree branch, the relevant US degree or foreign equivalent, or a qualifying bachelor's degree plus at least five years of progressive post-bachelor's specialty experience, is only part of the assessment. Job-offer and labour-certification requirements generally apply, subject to exceptions. A national interest waiver has additional requirements and is not granted merely for holding an MBA or doctorate.

**What this means:** An advanced degree does not satisfy every EB-2 or national-interest-waiver condition. NIW may permit self-petitioning without an employer sponsor, but its separate eligibility and waiver requirements still apply.

**Sources:** [7](https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/permanent-workers/employment-based-immigration-second-preference-eb-2)

### Provider-specific evidence: academic transfer and certification are not immigration approval

GAU's cited transfer announcement concerns the 2014–2015 academic year and a 2014 application deadline; it does not prove a current Maverick US/UK pathway. Rushford lists eduQua certification for its online division. eduQua explicitly states that it certifies continuing-education institutions, not individual courses, degrees or persons.

**What this means:** Academic transfer, institutional certification, memberships, credit totals and partner names do not establish immigration eligibility. Acceptance of an individual GAU or RBS award must be assessed for its intended use; neither universal acceptance nor universal rejection is established here.

**Sources:** [8](https://www.gau.edu.tr/en/haber/892/gau_announced_the_undergraduate_transfer_quotas_for_inter-continental_higher_education) [9](https://rushford.ch/accreditations/) [10](https://alice.ch/en/eduqua/)
