## 4. `faqs` *(6)*

**Why does a four-year degree show only three course years?**
<p>That is how GAU currently publishes the Economics grid: its panel says four years, but the listed courses stop after Year 3. The department page confirms the award but does not fill in the missing course table. This page will not invent Year 4 subjects. Ask GAU for the approved final-year specification for your intake.</p>

**Which areas of economics are actually listed on the timetable?**
<p>GAU starts with introductory economics, business mathematics and computing, followed by intermediate microeconomics and macroeconomics with statistics. Its third year names international economics, money and banking, political economy, financial economics, mathematical economics and forecasting. Those are published subjects; the elective slots and unlisted fourth year should not be mistaken for additional named courses.</p>

**Do the figures of 132 credits and 240 ECTS conflict?**
<p>They are expressed in different units. GAU's prospective description says that students complete 132 credits, while its faculty department describes the completed BSc as 240 ECTS. Neither source explains an individual transfer calculation, and the absent fourth-year grid cannot be reconstructed from either total. Have GAU confirm how its official plan applies to your entry route.</p>

**What graduate jobs does the university name for Economics?**
<p>None on the Career Opportunities tab. GAU says the programme can lead towards private or public enterprise and further study, and the department describes analysis, data and economic problem-solving as learning outcomes. Any reference here to economic analysis or policy applications is derived from that material. It is not a verified graduate destination, employer or salary claim.</p>

**Can I attend this BSc remotely and what English proof applies?**
<p>The Business and Economics faculty says class attendance is required and uses Moodle as a support platform. The programme panel says English is the taught language. GAU also sets out Foundation English School examination and exemption guidance for newly admitted English-medium students. Obtain your entry decision and actual attendance requirements from GAU; do not assume a fully online route.</p>

**Where can I confirm tuition and entry rules for my application?**
<p>The Economics programme panel shows a dash for fees, and GAU's broader tariff is dated 2025–2026. Its international admissions office says documents depend on the degree, not that one score guarantees entry to Economics. Request a current written fee schedule, any scholarship conditions and a decision on your qualifications before you make a payment.</p>
