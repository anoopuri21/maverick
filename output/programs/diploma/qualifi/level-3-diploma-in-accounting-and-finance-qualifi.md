## 2.2 `description`

```html
<p>This is a deliberately narrow award and better for it. Where Qualifi's other Level 3 business qualifications survey marketing, people, operations and culture, this one stays on the books. Its stated aim is to give learners the foundation required for a career in financial management and accountancy across a range of organisations, or to move on to further study in the subject.</p>
<p>Three subjects do the work. Financial Transactions Record-keeping establishes why entries have to be accurate and what happens when they are not. Cost and Management Information introduces the internal numbers a business uses to make decisions, which is a different discipline from reporting to the outside world. Financial Records Maintenance covers keeping the records in order once they exist.</p>
<p>The learning outcomes are correspondingly short and concrete: understand the need for accuracy, understand what cost and management accounting is for, understand the role of technology in recording management information, and understand accounting concepts and the role of financial records. Entry is open from age 16 with IELTS 6 for non-native English speakers, and the qualification number is 610/0795/X.</p>
```

## 4. `faqs` *(6)*

**Does this make me an accountant?**
<p>No. It is a foundation qualification whose stated purpose is to provide the grounding for a career in financial management and accountancy, or to support further study. Professional status in accountancy comes from chartered and certified bodies with their own examinations and experience requirements. This sits well before that point. It is the sort of qualification that helps you get into an accounts function, where the professional route can then begin.</p>

**Only three subjects — is that enough?**
<p>It is the smallest award in Qualifi's Level 3 range, and the four published learning outcomes are the shortest list too. That reflects a narrow scope rather than a thin one. If you want breadth across business, the Level 3 business management or integrated diplomas cover far more ground. If you specifically want the accounting foundation without paying for subjects you will not use, the narrowness is the point.</p>

**What are the references for?**
<p>Qualifi states that applicants are normally required to produce two supporting references, with at least one preferably academic. This is more than its other Level 3 awards ask for and worth preparing early, particularly if you left education some time ago and need to think about who can speak for you academically. Your centre will tell you what form they should take.</p>

**I left school at sixteen. Can I still apply?**
<p>Yes. Sixteen is the published minimum age and no prior qualification is listed as mandatory. What Qualifi does require is that the approved centre reviews whether you can complete the programme successfully and meet its demands, and that it considers what support you might need. So the centre will assess your readiness rather than simply checking a certificate.</p>

**Is there an English requirement?**
<p>Yes, if English is not your first language: IELTS 6 or equivalent. That is a notch above the IELTS 5.5 guidance Qualifi gives for its Level 3 hospitality award, so do not assume the requirement is the same across its range. Qualifi also notes that international qualifications are checked for appropriate enrolment, which your centre will handle as part of admission.</p>

**What comes after it?**
<p>Qualifi publishes three onward options: its own Level 4 Diploma in Accounting and Finance, a Level 4 diploma in a related area, or employment in an associated profession. Staying within Qualifi's own ladder means the progression is well defined, since it runs accounting diplomas at Levels 3, 4, 5 and 7. Where you go beyond that depends on which professional body or university you approach, and on their rules rather than Qualifi's.</p>
