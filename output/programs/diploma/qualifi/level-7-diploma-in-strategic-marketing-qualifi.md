## 4. `faqs` *(6)*

**What does "advanced standing" mean here?**
<p>Qualifi writes that the qualification lets learners work toward a relevant master's programme with advanced standing. In plain terms, advanced standing means a university accepting prior study in place of part of its own programme. The thing to understand is who grants it: the university does, not the awarding body. Qualifi names no partner institution, publishes no credit transfer arrangement and states no terms. So treat that phrase as a possibility to investigate with a specific university, in writing, rather than as something this diploma delivers.</p>

**Why is the minimum age twenty-one?**
<p>Qualifi does not explain the reasoning, it simply publishes the requirement. What we can say is that twenty-one is the highest minimum age anywhere in its range — other qualifications in this listing use sixteen, eighteen, nineteen or twenty. Combined with the expectation of a Level 6 qualification or first degree, it points to a programme designed around people who have already run campaigns and can argue about them from experience.</p>

**Is this a CIM qualification?**
<p>It is not. Qualifi publishes no relationship with the Chartered Institute of Marketing, no exemption from CIM assessments and no route to chartered marketer status. This is an Ofqual-regulated diploma awarded by Qualifi, which is a different thing from a professional body qualification, and neither replaces the other. If CIM membership or chartered status is your goal, go to the CIM and ask what it requires.</p>

**How much of it is digital?**
<p>One of the six subjects is devoted to digital and social media marketing, and Qualifi's description of the award names digital communication management among the theory areas it covers. Beyond that the published material does not break down how much digital content appears inside the other subjects, and it names no platforms, tools or certifications. If you need specific technical skills — analytics platforms, advertising tools, marketing automation — ask the centre what the teaching actually includes.</p>

**What is the research project like?**
<p>Qualifi lists a Marketing Research Project as the final subject but publishes no detail about its length, format, supervision or whether it must relate to your own employer. Several of the nine learning outcomes point towards it, particularly those on developing research and analytical capability and on assessing market-sourced data critically. The practical specifics live in the centre specification, so ask to see what is expected before you reach the final stage.</p>

**Duration and fees?**
<p>Neither appears on the official page. Qualifi accredits and certificates this qualification but delivers no teaching itself, so the schedule and the price are whatever your approved centre sets. There is no published total qualification time for this award, which means no official hours figure exists to help you judge whether a proposed timetable is realistic alongside a job. Get the intake dates, the expected duration and a complete written price before enrolling.</p>
