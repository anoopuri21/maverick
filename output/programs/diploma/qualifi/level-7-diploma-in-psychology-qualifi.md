## 3.4 `learning`

Qualifi publishes only three outcome statements for this qualification, and we have to be straight about their condition. Two of them describe teaching arrangements — computer practical sessions, data sets handed to students — rather than anything a learner achieves, and one of those two names a unit, "Issues in Scientific Research", that does not appear in the published subject list at all. A third difficulty: that same statement is about statistical technique, while the only research subject published is qualitative. We have reproduced the one usable outcome and built the rest of this list from the six published subjects and the cognitive functions named in Qualifi's summary. It is coverage, derived from the awarding body's own material, not a restatement of its outcomes.

1. Know the neuropsychological aspects of emotion and behaviour in healthy and clinical populations
2. Understand the main cognitive functions — perception, executive functions, language and attention — and their role in psychology
3. Examine psychoanalytic ideas and their bearing on mental health
4. Study the biological systems underlying cognition and behaviour
5. Apply qualitative research methodology to psychological questions
6. Consider how social and cultural context shapes psychological experience
7. Trace psychological development across the human lifespan
8. Explore rehabilitation psychology and the process of recovery and adjustment

## 3.5 `careers`

Qualifi publishes no employment data, salary figures or job destinations for this award. Three onward options appear on its page: a Qualifi Level 8 Diploma, a university to complete a master's degree, or employment in an associated profession. No university is named, and this diploma is not a master's degree.

**Read this part carefully before enrolling.** Qualifi's summary says learners will develop practical skills for a career in psychology, and two subjects touch mental health and rehabilitation. That wording can easily be read as more than it is, so here is what the published material does not say:

- The qualification is **not accredited by the British Psychological Society**, and Qualifi publishes no Graduate Basis for Chartered Membership.
- It leads to **no registration with the Health and Care Professions Council** and confers none of the protected titles — clinical, counselling, educational, forensic, health, occupational, or sport and exercise psychologist.
- It is **not a clinical or therapeutic training**. It gives no licence to offer psychotherapy, counselling or psychological assessment to anybody.
- Equivalent restrictions apply outside the UK, where each country's own regulator decides who may practise.

If practising as a psychologist is your goal, start with the BPS and the HCPC, find out what they actually require, and work backwards from there.

## 4. `faqs` *(6)*

**Can I become a psychologist with this?**
<p>No. The titles clinical psychologist, counselling psychologist, educational psychologist and the rest are protected in the UK, and using them requires registration with the Health and Care Professions Council, which has its own route and does not recognise this diploma. Nor is the award accredited by the British Psychological Society or linked to Graduate Basis for Chartered Membership. Qualifi claims none of those things; we are simply making sure the absence is visible. Check directly with the BPS and the HCPC before you plan a career around any qualification.</p>

**Why does the learning section come with a disclaimer?**
<p>Because the published outcomes do not hold up. Qualifi lists three, which is unusually few for a 120-credit award, and two of them read as descriptions of how a course might be taught — computer practical sessions, example data sets provided to students — rather than statements of what a learner will be able to do. One of those two refers to a unit called "Issues in Scientific Research" that is nowhere in the published subject list, and it talks about statistics although the only research subject here is qualitative. We could have tidied all that away. Showing it seemed more useful.</p>

**I found another Level 7 Diploma in Psychology. Is it the same award?**
<p>Almost certainly not. Gatehouse Awards publishes a Level 7 Diploma in Psychology with a different qualification number and a different size, and training providers list both without always making the distinction clear. The qualification described on this page is Qualifi's, registered as 610/2064/3. When you compare offers, ask each provider for the qualification number and the awarding body in writing, and match on those. Titles are not unique; numbers are.</p>

**Do I need a psychology degree to start?**
<p>Qualifi publishes no such requirement. Three things are listed: you must be nineteen or older, you need IELTS 6 or an accepted equivalent if English is not the language you grew up with, and two referees are normally expected. Previous study in psychology, or in anything else, goes unmentioned. That is striking for a Level 7 award, where a Level 6 qualification or first degree is the usual expectation. Since centres may apply stricter rules of their own, confirm the position with yours rather than relying on the published minimum.</p>

**What is in Rehabilitation Psychology?**
<p>Qualifi names the subject but publishes no description of its content, so we can tell you what the field generally concerns rather than what this particular syllabus covers. Rehabilitation psychology deals with how people adjust after injury, illness or disability — motivation, coping, identity, and the psychological side of recovery alongside the physical one. For the actual topics, assessment and reading, ask your centre for the subject descriptor from the specification.</p>

**How long is it and what will it cost?**
<p>Qualifi publishes neither figure, and no total qualification time either, so there is not even an hours estimate to reason from. Both answers rest with the approved centre teaching the programme, because the awarding body's role is to write, accredit and certificate the qualification rather than to deliver it. Before committing, get the start date, the expected finish, and a written price that spells out registration and certification separately.</p>
