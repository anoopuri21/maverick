<p>Sixty credits, not the usual hundred and twenty. Three subjects, not six or eight: Contemporary Issues Underpinning Leadership in CPD, Developing Research and CPD, and Effective Communication through CPD. Every title carries those three letters, and Qualifi describes the whole award as continuing professional development. Anyone presenting it as a full postgraduate workload is misreading it.</p>
<p>What makes it heavier than the credit figure suggests is the research. One published outcome asks you to conduct primary research in the field of leadership — gathering your own evidence rather than summarising somebody else's. Another asks you to critically evaluate and integrate theories, models and methods in formal research. Qualifi also states an intention worth quoting: to look past the usual examination of Anglo-American management models.</p>
<p>Registered under number 603/4994/3, it is the most compact award at this level in the listing, and no study-hours figure is published for it.</p>

## 3.4 `learning`

Qualifi publishes ten outcomes for this award. Two pairs among them say much the same thing — two on critical reflection, two on communication — so we have consolidated rather than padding the list with near-duplicates. One further outcome refers to leadership "in sport", which appears nowhere else on the page and looks like material carried over from a different qualification; we have left it out rather than imply a sports pathway that does not exist. Everything below comes from the published set.

1. Critically reflect on issues at the forefront of leadership practice
2. Understand reflective practice and what it means for your personal and professional development
3. Weigh strategies for effective leadership across a broad range of contexts
4. Conduct primary research in the field of leadership
5. Review, critically evaluate and integrate theories, models and methods from formal research, and put them to practical use
6. Apply analytical thinking and problem-solving to both theoretical and real situations
7. Communicate ideas clearly in speech and in writing, adjusting style to the audience and the format
8. Carry ideas and concepts from one domain across into another

## 3.5 `careers`

No employment rates, destination lists or salary figures are published by Qualifi for this award, and inventing them is not an option. Two onward routes appear on its page.

1. A university partner, to complete a relevant master's degree, subject to approval
2. Direct entry into employment in an associated profession

Credit where it is due on the wording: "subject to approval" is Qualifi's own hedge, and it is the frankest phrasing used on any route in this range. It is still worth spelling out what it means in practice. The diploma does not award a master's degree. No partner university is named and no arrangement is published. Approval rests with the receiving institution, which will also decide what sixty credits at Level 7 are worth against its own entry criteria — and sixty is half what most of these diplomas carry, which may matter to an admissions tutor. Ask the university before you enrol, not after.

No professional body recognition is published either. The award confers no chartered status, no membership of any leadership or management institute, and no licence of any kind.

## 4. `faqs` *(6)*

**Why is this only 60 credits?**
<p>Because it was built as continuing professional development rather than as a full postgraduate course, and Qualifi says so repeatedly. All three subject titles contain the letters CPD. The practical effect is a shorter, tighter piece of study aimed at someone already leading people who wants structured time on the ethics of doing it. Be careful how you describe it afterwards: it is a Level 7 qualification, genuinely, but at half the credit value of the diplomas sitting alongside it, and admissions tutors and employers can read a credit figure.</p>

**What does the primary research involve?**
<p>Qualifi publishes the requirement — conduct primary research in the field of leadership — without publishing a format, a length or an assessment method. In plain terms, primary research means collecting evidence yourself: interviews, observation, survey responses, case material from your own organisation, then analysing it honestly rather than confirming what you already believed. It is the element that makes this award weigh more than its sixty credits suggest. Ask your centre what form it takes, what ethical approval it needs and how it is marked.</p>

**What does "beyond Anglo-American management models" mean?**
<p>It is Qualifi's own phrase, and we are quoting rather than interpreting it heavily. Most leadership theory taught in English comes out of British and North American business schools and carries their assumptions about hierarchy, individual achievement and how disagreement is handled. Stating an intention to look past that suggests room for other traditions and for the circumstances learners actually work in. How far any given centre delivers on it will vary, so it is a fair question to put to the one you are considering.</p>

**Does it make me a qualified or chartered leader?**
<p>There is no such thing in any regulated sense, and this award certainly does not create one. It carries no chartered status, no membership of a management or leadership institute, and no professional licence. What it provides is evidence of regulated study at Level 7 in a specific area of practice, plus a piece of original research you can point to. Anyone seeking a chartered designation should go to the relevant institute and ask what it recognises before assuming this contributes.</p>

**What is the price of a 60-credit award?**
<p>Whatever your approved centre charges, since Qualifi publishes nothing on cost or duration. Do not assume half the credits means half the price of a 120-credit diploma; centres price on their own delivery costs, and the research element needs supervision time. With no study-hours total against the award, nothing official exists to judge a proposed schedule by. Ask what tuition covers, what supervision you get for the research, and whether registration and certification sit inside the quoted figure.</p>

**How is it different from the strategic leadership diplomas?**
<p>Subject matter, mostly. The strategic management and leadership awards at this level deal with direction setting, organisational performance, finance and change — what the organisation should do. This one deals with conduct: how decisions are reached, whose interests are weighed, and how a leader accounts for that. It is also half their size and expects original research instead of broader coverage. People sometimes take one of each over time, but they are not substitutes for one another.</p>
