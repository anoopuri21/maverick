## 3.4 `learning`

Qualifi publishes twelve outcomes for this award, more than any other in this listing. The eight below are the most distinctive; the full set is in the centre specification.

1. Apply and critique different implementation standards for occupational health and safety management
2. Evaluate what strategic leadership contributes to risk management
3. Review how globalisation affects an organisation's safety culture
4. Analyse the impact of artificial intelligence on occupational health and safety risk management
5. Apply the principles of safety concepts and safety calculation
6. Analyse ergonomic principles, human factors and workplace design to improve occupational safety
7. Evaluate industrial hygiene principles, hazard recognition and exposure prevention strategies
8. Assess fire safety principles, fire prevention systems, emergency response and waste management

## 3.5 `careers`

Qualifi publishes no employment statistics, salary data or job destinations for this award. Two onward options appear on its page:

1. A Qualifi Level 7 Diploma
2. Directly into employment in an associated profession

That list is worth pausing on. Almost every other Qualifi qualification in this listing advertises a route towards a master's degree through an unnamed university partner. **This one does not.** No university route of any kind is published in the progression panel, and we have not imported one from elsewhere. If postgraduate study is your plan, treat it as an open question to put to universities directly rather than as something the qualification offers.

**On professional recognition.** No NEBOSH equivalence, no IOSH accreditation and no membership entitlement with the International Institute of Risk and Safety Management are published, although an IIRSM-branded leaflet is linked from the page. We have not read that leaflet and claim nothing from it. Where a job or a tender specifies a particular certification, confirm the requirement with the body that issues it.

## 4. `faqs` *(6)*

**What makes it "international"?**
<p>Qualifi does not say. The word sits in the official title, but the published page names no countries, describes no regional variants and gives no explanation of how the content differs from the Diploma of almost the same name. Availability is stated as the UK and internationally, which is true of nearly every qualification in the range. We would rather tell you that the word is unexplained than invent a meaning for it. If it matters to your employer, ask the centre what the specification says.</p>

**How does this compare with the Diploma that is not labelled international?**
<p>They are genuinely different qualifications with different numbers. The other award publishes six subjects, seven outcomes, and an entry block requiring you to be twenty-one with a Level 6 qualification or an honours degree at 2.2, plus English and references. This one publishes eight subjects, twelve outcomes, and admits on a related Level 6 qualification, an engineering degree or three years of management experience — with no age limit, no language test and no references stated. In character, this is the more technical and operational of the two.</p>

**Is there really no age limit or English requirement?**
<p>None is published on this page, which is unusual even within Qualifi's own range — most of its Level 7 awards state a minimum age and an IELTS standard. The entry section here consists of four bullets about qualifications and experience and nothing else. Centres remain free to set additional admission conditions of their own, and many choose to, so put the question to yours directly: what evidence and what language standard does it expect in practice?</p>

**Can I use this to get onto a master's?**
<p>Qualifi does not offer that route here, and the page is slightly at odds with itself about it. The progression panel lists only a Qualifi Level 7 Diploma or employment, with no university mentioned. The written overview, separately, says the diploma provides a sound basis for progression to postgraduate study. Both statements are on the page and we have not tried to reconcile them. What is certain is that no partner university is named and no arrangement is published, so any postgraduate plan needs to start with the university itself.</p>

**The subject list looks oddly formatted. How many subjects are there?**
<p>Eight. Qualifi prints five in one block and then three more as separate single items, which makes the list look shorter at a glance. The twelve published outcomes map across all eight, which confirms the count. One of them also carries a typographical corruption in the source — "Occupa4onal" for "Occupational" — which we have corrected here. A related point: the subject called Safety Programs and Concepts is spelled exactly as Qualifi registered it, in American English. Official titles are reproduced unchanged here, never anglicised.</p>

**Price and duration?**
<p>Neither is set by Qualifi, which accredits and certificates this qualification without delivering any of the teaching. Both depend on the approved centre you choose. This award publishes no total qualification time, which leaves nothing official to measure a proposed schedule against. Before enrolling, pin down when cohorts start, when yours would finish, and what the full written price covers line by line.</p>
