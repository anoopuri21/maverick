## 2.2 `description`

```html
<p>Strategy courses tend to hand everyone the same syllabus and hope it lands. This one splits in two. Six units are fixed, so every learner covers strategic marketing, finance for managers, strategic direction, strategic planning, creative and innovative management, and corporate responsibility in the wider business environment. After that you pick a lane.</p>
<p>Five specialism routes are published: general management, human resources management, finance, marketing, and project management. Pick project management and you study planning, controlling and leading a project, procurement risk and contract management, and advanced project and logistics management. Pick general management and you take research methods plus development as a strategic manager. The choice is yours and it changes what the qualification means on your CV.</p>
<p>Admission works differently here too. Qualifi states that entry is through a centre interview, with a Level 6 qualification or a first degree expected alongside it, and it leaves a door open for managers with considerable experience but nothing on paper. It is worth 120 credits at Level 7, registered on the framework as 603/5745/9.</p>
```

## 4. `faqs` *(6)*

**How do the specialism routes work in practice?**
<p>Qualifi's instruction is short: complete all six mandatory units and choose a specialism route to reach 120 credits. The five routes are general management, human resources management, finance, marketing and project management, and each has its own published units. What the page does not tell you is how many credits sit in the core and how many in the route, because no per-unit credit values are published at all. Your centre can show you that breakdown from the specification before you commit to a route.</p>

**Why does an HR unit appear in the finance and marketing routes?**
<p>Good question, and we cannot answer it. "Contemporary HRM" is printed as the third unit of the human resources route, the finance route and the marketing route. An HR unit inside a finance specialism looks like an error in the published list rather than a design decision, but we are not going to swap in a unit that seems more sensible and present it as fact. Ask the centre to confirm the exact units for the route you want, in writing.</p>

**What happens at the centre interview?**
<p>Qualifi states that entry runs through a centre interview and that learners are expected to hold a Level 6 qualification or a first degree. It also says managers with considerable experience but no formal qualifications may be considered, subject to interview and being able to show they can handle what the programme demands. The format of that interview is set by the approved centre, not by Qualifi, so ask what it involves and what evidence you should bring.</p>

**Can I use this to get a master's degree?**
<p>Not on its own. Qualifi lists "a university partner to complete a dissertation to then receive a full master's degree" as one of three onward routes. Read that carefully: the diploma is not a master's and does not award one. No partner university is named on the page, no list exists and no terms are published. A university receiving your application decides whether to admit you, how much of your study it recognises and what else it wants. Speak to the university you have in mind before you enrol here, not after.</p>

**Is there an age limit or an English test?**
<p>Neither is published for this award. Several other Qualifi Level 7 diplomas state a minimum age and ask for IELTS 6 with two references, but this page carries none of that. We are not going to import another qualification's rules and present them as these. Centres may apply their own admission checks on top of what Qualifi publishes, so ask yours directly.</p>

**What is the timescale, and the price?**
<p>Qualifi supplies neither. Nothing appears for length, nothing for cost, and this award does not even carry a total qualification time, so no notional study-hours estimate exists to reason backwards from. Those answers belong to the approved centre that teaches the programme; the awarding body writes, accredits and certificates the qualification but runs no classes of its own. Ask for intake dates, an expected finish date and an itemised price, all in writing, before you sign anything.</p>
