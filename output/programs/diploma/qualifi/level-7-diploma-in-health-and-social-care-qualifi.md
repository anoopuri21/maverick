**One thing to be completely clear about.** This is a management qualification. It is not a licence to practise, it is not a registration, and it does not qualify anyone to deliver care, treatment or clinical work of any kind. Qualifi publishes nothing connecting it to the Care Quality Commission, Social Work England, the Nursing and Midwifery Council, the Health and Care Professions Council or any equivalent regulator in another country. If the role you want requires registration with a care regulator, that registration is obtained through the regulator itself and this diploma does not substitute for it.

## 4. `faqs` *(6)*

**Will this let me work in a care role?**
<p>Not by itself, no. Everything on this award is management content — leadership, staffing, budgets, policy, change and research — and there is no clinical, nursing or social work training anywhere in it. Qualifi makes no claim that the qualification registers you with a professional regulator or permits you to practise, and we are not going to imply otherwise. Where a job requires registration, that comes from the regulator in your country and has its own separate requirements.</p>

**What separates this from the Level 3 and Level 5 awards in care?**
<p>Purpose, mostly. The Level 3 diploma introduces the sector and recognises experience people already have. The Level 5 extended diploma is a much larger academic step that goes into policy, sociology, quality and a research project. This Level 7 award assumes you are heading for or already in a management post and builds around the functions that role involves, including the finance subject that appears at neither of the other levels. They are three different jobs, not three sizes of the same one.</p>

**Do I need a degree to enrol?**
<p>Qualifi does not say so. The entry section here names an age minimum of nineteen, an IELTS 6 requirement where English is not your first language, and two references — but no prior qualification at all. Most of its other Level 7 awards expect a Level 6 qualification or a first degree, so the omission stands out. Our advice is practical: ask your approved centre what it actually requires, because centres can and do set their own admission standards above the published minimum.</p>

**Who are these "University partners"?**
<p>Qualifi does not say, and that is the honest answer. Its page refers to completing a master's degree at "our chosen University partners" without naming a single institution, publishing a list, or describing any arrangement. So treat the route as something to investigate rather than something you have been promised. This diploma is not a master's and awards no degree. If a particular university matters to you, contact its admissions office directly, tell them exactly which qualification you are taking, and ask what they would offer.</p>

**What does the research subject involve?**
<p>Research Methods for Healthcare Professionals is the sixth subject, and Qualifi's stated aim is that learners research and implement improvement for their employer and within the sector. That suggests applied work grounded in a real service rather than a literature review, but the published page gives no detail on length, format or supervision. That detail lives in the centre specification, so request it early rather than discovering the requirements late.</p>

**Length and price?**
<p>Neither is published by Qualifi. The awarding body accredits and certificates this qualification but delivers none of the teaching, so both answers come from the approved centre you enrol with. There is no total qualification time on this award either, so no official study-hours estimate exists to help you weigh a proposed timetable against a full-time job in a care setting. Ask for intake dates, a realistic completion date and a complete written fee.</p>
