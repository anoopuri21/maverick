## 2.2 `description`

```html
<p>Being handed a team rarely comes with instructions. Rotas, budgets, awkward conversations and a sudden duty of care all land at once, usually on someone who was promoted for being good at the job rather than for managing anyone. This diploma is built for that moment, and Qualifi writes that it should help learners realise not just their own potential but that of the organisations they work in.</p>
<p>Six subjects make up the award, and the balance tells you what it is for. Managing Business Operations and An Introduction to Finance handle the mechanics. Working in Teams and An Introduction to Leadership Skills deal with the human side. Organisational Culture asks why a workplace behaves the way it does, and Workplace Welfare covers the responsibility you now carry for other people's wellbeing.</p>
<p>Everything here is mandatory, which sets it apart from several of Qualifi's other Level 3 awards where some subjects are optional. It carries 60 credits, sits at number 603/4373/4 on the Regulated Qualifications Framework, and Qualifi names a Level 4 qualification or direct employment as the routes beyond it.</p>
```

## 4. `faqs` *(6)*

**How is this different from the Level 3 Diploma in Business Management?**
<p>They are separate qualifications with separate numbers and no shared subjects. The business management award looks outward at the environment, resources, marketing and people function of a company. This one looks at the job of managing: operations, finance basics, teams, leadership, culture and welfare. Pick this if you are managing people. Pick the other if you want a general grounding in how a business works.</p>

**Do I need management experience already?**
<p>No. Qualifi expects entry through a centre interview and accepts three starting points: a Level 2 qualification, work experience in a business setting with clear career goals, or a Level 3 qualification in another subject when you want to move into management. Plenty of learners come to it in their first supervisory role, which is the situation the syllabus assumes.</p>

**Is every subject compulsory?**
<p>Yes, and that is worth knowing because it is unusual within Qualifi's Level 3 range. Several sibling awards publish a mandatory core plus an optional group. Here all six are required, so there is nothing to choose and no risk of skipping the part you find least comfortable.</p>

**What does 60 credits actually mean?**
<p>Credits are a measure of learning volume rather than of time served. Sixty credits at Level 3 places this among Qualifi's standard-sized awards at that level, roughly half the size of its 120-credit integrated diploma. Qualifi does not publish a total qualification time in hours for this award, so credits are the only official measure of its scale.</p>

**Who teaches it?**
<p>An approved centre, not Qualifi. The awarding organisation writes the qualification, regulates the standards and issues the certificate once the centre has assessed you. This is why you should direct questions about start dates, timetables, teaching format and cost to the centre, and questions about the qualification itself to Qualifi.</p>

**Can I go on to a degree afterwards?**
<p>Qualifi names a Level 4 qualification or employment as the published routes from here, and does not mention a degree year for this particular award. Some of its other Level 3 diplomas do name a university route. If higher education is your plan, check what the universities you have in mind accept rather than assuming this award carries that route.</p>
