## 2.2 `description`

```html
<p>Running an education provider is not the same as running a business, and this diploma is written on that assumption. Gatehouse aims it at aspiring and established leaders, managers and senior practitioners in education, training and learning environments who want to sharpen how they lead strategically. Every one of the six units is framed around schools, colleges and training organisations rather than borrowed from generic management.</p>
<p>The awarding body sets out what it wants graduates to show: advanced strategic thinking, ethical leadership, reflective judgement and organisational effectiveness. You engage critically with policy, governance, pedagogy, change, professional development and research, each underpinned by current theory and evidence-based decision making. Those six themes map almost exactly onto the six mandatory units, so the syllabus does what the aims promise.</p>
<p>One structural choice stands out. Reflective Practice and Professional Development is a mandatory 20-credit unit in its own right, the only qualification across the Gatehouse Level 7 set to give reflection that much weight. Assessment combines coursework with a portfolio, meaning you produce written work as well as assembling evidence from the job.</p>
```

## 3.4 `learning`
1. Set a strategic direction for an education or training organisation and explain the reasoning
2. Read education policy critically and understand the governance you answer to
3. Lead change and improvement without losing the people you are leading
4. Connect pedagogy and curriculum decisions to organisational strategy
5. Hold yourself accountable through structured reflective practice
6. Plan and support professional development for the staff around you
7. Design and carry out research in an education leadership context
8. Weigh evidence before deciding, and show how the evidence shaped the decision

## 4. `faqs` *(6)*

**Why does the title differ slightly from the one on the Gatehouse site?**
<p>Gatehouse calls it the GA Level 7 Diploma in Education Leadership and Management. The programme list we work from writes it as "Educational Leadership & Management". It is the same qualification, under the same number, 610/7539/5. We have kept the listing title as supplied and recorded the awarding body's wording here so you can match them without confusion.</p>

**Who is this actually for?**
<p>People already inside education who are moving up, or who are in a senior post and want the underpinning to go with it. Gatehouse names roles involving educational leadership, quality improvement, curriculum or pedagogical oversight, organisational development, governance and professional development. If you lead a department, a phase, a provision or a training function, you are in the intended audience.</p>

**How is it assessed?**
<p>Through coursework and a Portfolio of Evidence together. That is a deliberate difference from the portfolio-only route used on some other Gatehouse Level 7 diplomas. Expect to write assessed pieces as well as to collect and present evidence from your day job. Each unit is signed off at the centre and then moderated, internally first and externally by Gatehouse afterwards.</p>

**Do I need a teaching degree to apply?**
<p>You need an honours degree or a Level 6 qualification, in any subject, not specifically a teaching one. If you hold neither, the centre can admit you on substantial relevant professional experience in education, training, leadership or management. No minimum number of years is published for this diploma, so the centre forms its own judgement using your CV, a professional discussion, records of continuing development, statements from referees, and a short written exercise.</p>

**How long will it take and what will it cost?**
<p>Gatehouse publishes neither a duration in months nor a learner price. It publishes 360 guided learning hours and 1,200 total qualification hours, and a registration fee of £206 excluding VAT that centres pay the awarding body. The teaching fee and the timetable both come from the centre, and pricing varies for centres working outside the UK, so get both in writing.</p>

**Will this let me become a qualified teacher?**
<p>No. This is a leadership and management qualification, not an initial teacher training route, and it carries no teaching practice requirement. Gatehouse offers separate Education and Training qualifications at Levels 3, 4 and 5 for people entering teaching. If qualified teacher status is your aim, check the requirements of the jurisdiction you intend to work in before enrolling on anything.</p>
