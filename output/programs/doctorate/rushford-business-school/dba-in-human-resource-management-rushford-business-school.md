## 2.2 `description`

```html
<p>Every HR director eventually meets the question that policy cannot answer. Why did retention drop in that function and not the others? Rushford's DBA in Human Resource Management is built for people who want to answer that properly, with 30 ECTS of research design, statistics and literature review sitting ahead of the dissertation rather than bolted on at the end.</p>
<p>The programme totals 180 ECTS and splits into two pathways. Managers with under five years in post take the coursework route, which combines the business management core with the HR specialisation and finishes at 20,000 to 25,000 words. Candidates with longer experience take the research route, are assigned a doctoral mentor early, and write to 45,000 to 50,000. Delivery is entirely online and asynchronous.</p>
<p>Rushford Business School in Lucerne awards the qualification, and candidates can request the PhD title instead of the DBA before graduation, subject to one condition about how they were admitted.</p>
```

## 4. `faqs` *(5)*

**Is a master's in HR required?**
<p>No. Recognition of Prior Experience means a master's is not mandatory in any subject, though a prior bachelor's degree is. The three routes are a master's in business and management; a master's in any discipline with more than five years of managerial experience; or a bachelor's in any discipline with more than eight years.</p>

**Can I take the PhD award instead of the DBA?**
<p>Yes, on application. The choice is confirmed before or immediately after graduation requirements are complete and only one title is conferred. The exception is important: candidates admitted through Recognition of Prior Experience are eligible for the DBA award only.</p>

**What can I research?**
<p>Rushford's DBA is an applied doctorate, so the dissertation investigates a real organisational problem rather than a theoretical one. Many candidates use their own employer as the setting, which makes the work more useful and also more exposed, since the findings have to stand up to a defence.</p>

**What English evidence do you accept?**
<p>A Medium of Instruction letter from your previous university, or IELTS 5.5, TOEFL iBT 58, TOEIC 555, PTE 50 or Duolingo 90. Waivers cover native speakers, anyone schooled or degree-educated in English, and two years' work in an English-language organisation.</p>

**How flexible is the schedule really?**
<p>Delivery is asynchronous through Rushford's e-Campus, so there are no live sessions to attend and you set the pace. The practical limit is financial rather than academic: finishing early makes the remaining fees payable at that point.</p>
