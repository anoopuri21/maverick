## 4. `faqs` *(5)*

**Is a master's in HR required?**
<p>Not in HR, and not necessarily at all. Applicants come in with a master's in business and management, with a master's in some other field plus five years or more of managerial experience, or with a bachelor's and upwards of eight years. Only the final route carries a limitation: the award is the DBA.</p>

**Can I take the PhD award instead of the DBA?**
<p>Yes, on application. The choice is settled before or just after the graduation requirements are complete, and a single title is awarded. Worth repeating the one exclusion: Recognition of Prior Experience entrants are eligible for the DBA alone.</p>

**What can I research?**
<p>Rushford's DBA is an applied doctorate, so the dissertation investigates a real organisational problem rather than a theoretical one. Many candidates use their own employer as the setting, which makes the work more useful and also more exposed, since the findings have to stand up to a defence.</p>

**What English evidence do you accept?**
<p>A letter confirming your last degree was taught in English usually settles it. Otherwise the bar is IELTS 5.5, TOEFL iBT 58, TOEIC 555, PTE 50 or Duolingo 90. Native speakers are exempt, as is anyone educated in English or with two years' service in an English-language organisation.</p>

**How flexible is the schedule really?**
<p>Genuinely flexible on the academic side — e-Campus holds the material, nothing is timetabled and you decide the speed. The constraint is financial instead: clear the requirements ahead of schedule and the outstanding balance falls due there and then.</p>
