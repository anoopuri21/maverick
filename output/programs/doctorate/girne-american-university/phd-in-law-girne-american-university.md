## 4. `faqs` *(6)*

**Can I apply for the PhD in Law now?**
<p>No. Girne American University annotates the programme as "Law (To be launched)" in its own prospectus and repeats that footnote beneath its PhD list, which means the doctorate has been announced but is not admitting students. Anything you see advertised as an open intake is not coming from the university.</p>

**When will the programme open?**
<p>The university has not published a date. Our advice is to register your interest, and we will contact you when GAU removes the to-be-launched marker or opens applications. Setting a personal deadline before the university does is how applicants end up paying fees for a programme with no start date.</p>

**What will the curriculum look like?**
<p>Unknown, and deliberately not guessed here. GAU's graduate school page for this doctorate renders its title with no modules, no semester plan and no course descriptions. When the university publishes the structure, we will replace this page with the confirmed curriculum rather than an approximation of it.</p>

**What will it cost?**
<p>GAU prices all PhD programmes at €15,000 for the entire programme, charged at €750 per course, with a €9,000 anniversary offer listed in its prospectus. A €50 application fee and €350 registration fee per semester apply, and VAT is added. Whether the same terms will apply to this doctorate is not yet published.</p>

**Do I need a law degree to be eligible?**
<p>That is not published either. GAU has not released entry requirements for this doctorate, so no claim about a required LLB, LLM, master's degree or research proposal can be made yet. The Institute of Graduate Studies and Research confirms eligibility rules when the programme opens.</p>

**Is a GAU law qualification recognised outside Cyprus?**
<p>GAU states recognition by YÖDAK in North Cyprus and YÖK in Turkey, and holds NARIC recognition in the United Kingdom. Recognition of a doctorate in law for practice purposes is a separate question governed by your own bar or regulator, so confirm it there before enrolling.</p>
