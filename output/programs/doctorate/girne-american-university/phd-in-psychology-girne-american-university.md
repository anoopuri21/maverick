## 4. `faqs` *(6)*

**Does GAU offer a PhD in Psychology?**
<p>Yes. Psychology appears on Girne American University's published list of PhD programmes, alongside Business Management, Marketing, Management Information Systems, Educational Administration, Tourism and Hospitality Management and Law. The doctorate is delivered through the Institute of Graduate Studies and Research at the Kyrenia campus in North Cyprus.</p>

**Why is the curriculum not shown on this page?**
<p>Because the university has not published one. GAU's graduate school page for this doctorate currently renders the programme title and nothing further: no modules, no semester plan and no course descriptions. Rather than fill that gap with assumptions, we request the current curriculum from the Institute and share it with you in writing.</p>

**What does the doctorate cost?**
<p>GAU's prospectus prices PhD programmes at €15,000 for the entire programme, charged at €750 per course, with a €9,000 anniversary offer listed. A €50 application fee and €350 registration fee per semester apply, and VAT is added. The university does not publish a separate figure for Psychology, so confirm the programme fee for your intake in writing.</p>

**Are scholarships available for this doctorate?**
<p>Do not assume so. GAU's prospectus states that scholarships are not available for master's and PhD programmes, while some GAU programme pages show a scholarship field as available. Because the two statements conflict, ask the Institute for a written funding decision before you build a budget around any reduction.</p>

**How long does it take and when can I start?**
<p>GAU has not published a duration for this specific doctorate. Its doctoral provision elsewhere runs across taught courses, a qualifying stage and a thesis, but that structure belongs to those programmes and cannot be assumed here. The Institute confirms your duration, entry point and intake date in writing.</p>

**Is the degree recognised outside North Cyprus?**
<p>GAU states recognition by YÖDAK, the higher education board of North Cyprus, and by YÖK, Turkey's Council of Higher Education, and holds NARIC recognition in the United Kingdom. Recognition of a qualification in your own country is a separate question, so check with the relevant authority there before enrolling.</p>
