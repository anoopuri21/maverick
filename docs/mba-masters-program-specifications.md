# MBA & Master's Programme Specification Register

**Source document:** `docs/listing.pdf`
**Source pages:** 1–5
**Prepared:** 25 August 2026
**Scope:** MBA, Executive MBA, Global MBA, MSc and LLM routes for the MBA & Master's landing page.

## Source and publishing policy

The attached listing is the source of truth for the programme inventory. It confirms the university, qualification family and programme title. It does **not** provide a full specification for every route.

Where a detail is confirmed on an official Maverick course page, it is recorded as a published route reference. Where the listing or official course page does not provide a programme-specific value, the frontend must say **Confirm with admissions** rather than inventing a value.

Programme, awarding-body, delivery, duration, entry, assessment, fee and progression details can vary by route and intake. They must be confirmed before enrolment.

## Specification fields used by the landing page

Every programme record uses these fields:

- **Programme title** — exact title from the attached listing.
- **Qualification** — MBA, Executive MBA, Global MBA, MSc or LLM.
- **Partner / awarding route** — institution named in the listing.
- **Programme focus** — the subject area expressed by the programme title.
- **Duration** — published route duration where available; otherwise confirmation required.
- **Delivery mode** — published mode where available; otherwise confirmation required.
- **Assessment** — published assessment pattern where available.
- **Entry route** — published entry route where available.
- **Source status** — distinguishes catalogue confirmation from route-detail confirmation.

### University for the Creative Arts (UCA), UK + Rushford Business School, Switzerland — Global MBA

- **Qualification:** Global MBA
- **Listing source:** Page 4
- **Published route reference:** The Global MBA page describes a maximum 12–15 month route, 100% online delivery, 180 credits, six 20-credit modules and a 60-credit dissertation research project.
- **Progression reference:** The published route references an OFQUAL Level 7 Postgraduate Diploma after 120 credits and dissertation-stage progression subject to eligibility.
- **Entry reference:** Bachelor’s degree or relevant Level 7 route; managerial experience may be required for mature entry.
- **Status:** Programme title and UCA + Rushford route confirmed by listing; admissions must confirm the current awarding arrangement.
