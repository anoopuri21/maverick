## 2.2 `description`

```html
<p>BSc Marketing at Girne American University moves past the idea that marketing is only promotion. Students begin with business, economics, statistics and accounting, then examine consumer behaviour and marketing strategy. The later courses include international marketing, integrated marketing communications and a marketing seminar. Brand management and marketing research appear on the elective list, so they are possible choices rather than compulsory promises.</p>
<p>The university lists four years of English-taught study and an Internship - 30 Calendar Days unit in the third-year grid. That title does not promise a paid post or a particular employer. The Business and Economics faculty requires class attendance; its Moodle platform supports courses but is not a stated online BSc route. GAU states 132 local credits and the department awards 240 ECTS.</p>
<p>GAU publishes example marketing roles, including senior positions. Those are possible directions, not the first job a new graduate will necessarily secure. The programme panel does not give a current tuition sum or marketing-specific international entry scores. Nor does a faculty accreditation statement establish CIM exemptions. Ask for written details before deciding where this degree fits your plans.</p>
```

## 4. `faqs` *(6)*

**Is marketing research a compulsory module on this BSc?**
<p>Not according to GAU's current grid. Marketing Research appears in the elective list, alongside Brand Management and Advertising and Promotion. Required later subjects include Consumer Behavior, Marketing Strategy, International Marketing and Integrated Marketing Communications. Elective places or combinations may vary, so request the approved course list for your intake if a particular research option matters to you.</p>

**Will GAU place me with a marketing employer?**
<p>The university lists an Internship - 30 Calendar Days course in the third year, but its page does not promise a host, paid work or a role after graduation. Treat the course title as a curriculum fact. Ask how internship places are allocated and assessed, and whether any placements are available to your cohort.</p>

**Are the senior careers on GAU's page guaranteed outcomes?**
<p>No. Marketing Manager and Marketing & Communications Director appear in GAU's examples, alongside specialist and coordinator roles, but the university does not report a placement rate or starting position for graduates. Those senior titles often require experience. The career chips here reproduce named possibilities, not an assurance of employment or immediate seniority.</p>

**Does this Marketing BSc give professional CIM exemptions?**
<p>The reviewed GAU programme and departmental pages do not state any Chartered Institute of Marketing exemptions or membership entitlement. The programme panel lists higher education accreditation bodies, including IACBE, but that is not the same thing as an individual professional qualification. If a CIM route matters to you, request written confirmation of any recognition before enrolling.</p>

**Is the programme taught online and what English proof is needed?**
<p>GAU identifies English as the taught language, while its Business and Economics faculty expects classroom attendance and uses Moodle only as a support tool. Its international admissions guidance describes English proficiency testing and possible exemptions, not a marketing-specific automatic acceptance score. Ask the university which evidence and teaching timetable apply to your circumstances.</p>

**Where can I get a reliable fee for this intake?**
<p>The degree panel prints a dash in the fee field. GAU also has a general tuition schedule dated 2025–2026; that is not an individual quotation for this intake. Scholarships are marked as possible, not guaranteed. Get an itemised offer covering tuition, registration and any award conditions in writing before paying.</p>
