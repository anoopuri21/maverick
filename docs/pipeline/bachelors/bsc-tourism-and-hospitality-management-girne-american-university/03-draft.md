## 2.2 `description`

```html
<p>BSc Tourism and Hospitality Management at Girne American University is built around how a visitor's stay actually works. The published courses move from hospitality basics and tourism mathematics into front office reservations, housekeeping, food and beverage service, hospitality finance and law. The later timetable reaches revenue management and resort operations.</p>
<p>The programme is taught in English across four years at the School of Tourism and Hospitality. GAU says students have an opportunity to train at Le Chateau Lambousa, its applied training hotel. ## 4. `faqs` *(6)*
```

**Is training at the university hotel guaranteed for every student?**
<p>No. GAU says students have the opportunity to train at Le Chateau Lambousa, its applied training hotel, but it does not set out an individual entitlement or selection process on this programme page. Its curriculum also includes internship entries in the second and third years. Ask the School how hotel access and internships work for your proposed intake.</p>

**What does the four-year hospitality curriculum actually cover?**
<p>The published grid begins with hospitality and tourism foundations, then names front office management, reservations, food and beverage service, housekeeping and hospitality accounting. Later subjects include hospitality finance, tourism policy planning, revenue management, resort management and a graduation seminar. These are GAU's named course titles, not a promise that every timetable will be identical in your intake.</p>

**Why are there no career titles shown for this degree?**
<p>GAU's Career Opportunities tab is blank for this BSc. The course titles support a discussion of guest service, operations and commercial decision-making, but they do not document employers, salaries or actual graduate roles. The learning themes on this page are derived from those subjects. If you need recent outcomes, request them from the School rather than relying on guessed destinations.</p>

**Can I take the tourism BSc entirely online from the Gulf?**
<p>The official programme page does not advertise an online route. It describes training at a university hotel and lists internships, which would need practical arrangements. Before you plan travel or take on work commitments, ask GAU which parts require attendance, where training takes place and whether any approved alternative delivery route exists for your intake.</p>

**What entry and English evidence should I prepare?**
<p>GAU's international admissions guidance says required documents depend on the degree. Students entering English-medium departments may need its Foundation English School proficiency examination unless they meet a published exemption route. The Tourism page gives no subject-specific score or guaranteed admission rule. Send your qualifications to GAU for a written eligibility and language assessment.</p>

**How much does the BSc cost and is funding assured?**
<p>The programme fee field shows a dash rather than a price. GAU's wider tariff page is dated 2025–2026, so it is not a safe quotation for the next intake. The degree panel says scholarships are available, not that every applicant receives one. Ask for an itemised current offer spelling out tuition, additional charges and any scholarship conditions.</p>
