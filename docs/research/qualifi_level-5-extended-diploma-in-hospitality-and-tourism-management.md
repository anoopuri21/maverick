## 3. Qualification Overview (verbatim)

> "The qualification deals with complex and challenging organisational issues and
> opportunities. The units are closely linked to current day real world and work
> challenges and the qualification will test and develop learners' workplace
> experience and abilities.
>
> The qualification will require learners to make judgements and provide creative and
> practical solutions and ways of responding to organisational challenges and problems.
> An action based approach will be sought and learners who have strong motivation to
> progress in their chosen career and business pathway will gain significantly from the
> qualification. The Level 5 units are also designed to dove-tail into a top-up
> undergraduate year at a UK University."

Bespoke copy, not the business boilerplate. Key phrase: the Level 5 units are
**"designed to dove-tail into a top-up undergraduate year at a UK University"** —
design intent, still not a guaranteed place.

## 4. Units (8, verbatim)

1. Sustainability in Tourism and Hospitality Management
2. Operations Management in Tourism and Resort Operations
3. Management of Visitor Attractions
4. Employability and Development in the Hospitality Industry
5. Tourism Destination Management
6. Entrepreneurship in Tourism and Hospitality Management
7. Cultural Tourism Management
8. Research Project

⚠️ **Only 8 units against 240 credits** — the lowest unit-to-credit ratio in the whole
Qualifi set. Compare: S.No 33 and 45 list 12 units for the same 240 credits. Most
likely the page lists only the Level 5 half of a Level 4 + Level 5 extended award,
but **the page does not say this.** Treat as a gap and disclose; do not reconcile it.

Sustainability leading the list and Cultural Tourism Management appearing at all are
genuine differentiators from the Level 3 award (S.No 30).

## 5. Learning Outcomes (9, verbatim)

1. An introduction and insight into the Hospitality and Tourism Industries
2. Background knowledge required to address the key aspects of the Hospitality and Tourism Industry
3. Develop knowledge and understanding of the Hospitality and Tourism Industries by exploring and analysing real-life scenarios and businesses
4. A qualification that enables students to gain credit towards degree entry
5. A qualification that enables students to develop the academic standards required for study at a higher level
6. To enable learners to apply analytical and evaluative techniques to business in private and public sectors
7. To enhance analytical and evaluative skills relating to business across a number of industries
8. To develop the learner's ability to recognize and reflect on the process of personal learning and development, which facilitates the enhancement of key personal, sociable, and other transferable skills
9. To encourage the learner's self-reflection, analytical, intellectual, and transferable skills

Outcomes 1–3 read as Level 4 language ("An introduction and insight into") on a Level 5
page — further evidence the page merges both halves. Note US spelling "recognize" in
outcome 8; keep verbatim only if quoted, otherwise write British English.

## 6. Entry Requirements (verbatim, three bullets)

- qualifications at Level 3 and/or 4;
- work experience at a managerial level and demonstrate ambition with clear career goals;
- a first degree in another discipline and want to develop their careers hospitality and tourism management. *(sic — missing "in")*

**No age minimum and no IELTS requirement published** — same pattern as S.No 33, and
different from S.No 34/35/37/45 which all state 18+ and IELTS 6. Do not import.

Note the contrast with the Level 3 award (S.No 30), which is **open entry**: at Level 5
a Level 3/4 qualification, managerial experience or a first degree is expected.
