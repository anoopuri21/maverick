## 2. Information panel — verbatim field values

Every live GAU PhD programme page under `/en/prospective/program/…` renders an identical
"Information" panel. Values captured verbatim:

| Field | Value as published |
|---|---|
| Faculty | Institute of Social & Applied Sciences |
| Degree | Doctoral Degree (PhD) |
| Duration | 3 Years |
| Fee | `- €` |
| Scholarship | Yes |
| Accreditation | YÖK - YÖDAK |
| Taught Language | English |

**Note on the Institute name.** The `/en/prospective/…` pages say *Institute of Social & Applied
Sciences*. The `/en/graduate-school/…` pages say *Institute of Social Sciences* and the index page
is headed *Institute of Graduate Studies and Research*. All three names are used by GAU itself.
Prefer *Institute of Graduate Studies and Research* in prose and note the departmental institute
where a page states it.

**Note on the Fee field.** GAU renders a literal `- €`. **No doctoral tuition figure is published**
on the programme pages. → Declared gap **GAP-SOURCE-GAU-01**.

**Note on Duration.** The panel states 3 Years, but every Departmental Courses table runs to
**Year 4** (two further thesis semesters). Report the official `Duration` value and describe the
thesis continuation factually. Do not silently reconcile the two. → **GAP-SOURCE-GAU-03**.

## 4. Language requirements (Source S3) — verbatim

New students admitted to English-medium departments must sit the Foundation English School (FES)
English Proficiency Examination, unless they hold one of the listed certificates.

GAU publishes two thresholds. The **higher column explicitly includes "Master Students"**:

| Certificate | Standard English-medium departments (min 60/100 FES) | Pharmacy, English Literature, Translation & Interpretation, ELT **& Master Students** (min 80/100 FES) |
|---|---|---|
| IELTS | 5.0 | **5.5** |
| TOEFL iBT | 60 | **65** |
| TOEFL CBT | 170 | **183** |
| TOEFL PBT | 497 | **513** |
| GCE / IGCSE | C | **B** |
| FCE | C | **B** |
| CPE / CAE | C | **B** |
| SAT Writing | 340 | **350** |
| SAT Reading | 400 | **410** |
| SAT Total | 740 | **760** |
| PTE | 40 | **46** |

Footnote published by GAU against IELTS in both columns: *"(Not valid for students from Turkey)"*.

Further verbatim conditions:
- Students who successfully complete the FES programme at a university, and Master students who
  pursued their undergraduate studies in an English-medium programme, are also exempted.
- All valid documents must be submitted to Student Services and the FES administration **before**
  the student sits the FES proficiency exam. Certificates are **not accepted after** the exam.
- Students without a certificate are placed into A1, A2, B1 or B2 levels by exam grade.

**IMPORTANT CAVEAT.** The exemption table names "Master Students" in the higher column. It does
**not** name doctoral students explicitly. Content must therefore present these scores as GAU's
published postgraduate thresholds and direct applicants to confirm with the Institute, rather than
asserting a PhD-specific rule. → **GAP-SOURCE-GAU-04**.

## 5. Admission requirements (from the PhD in Business Management graduate-school page)

The only GAU doctoral page that publishes a full admissions list is
`https://www.gau.edu.tr/en/graduate-school/phd_in_business_management.html`. Verbatim:

> Application requirement for the PhD program in Business Administration;
> Have a master's degree in business and related fields;
> For those who have master's degrees in different fields, it is required to have completed the
> appropriate scientific preparation courses.
>
> Required documents for application;
> - Undergraduate diploma and transcript,
> - Postgraduate diploma and transcript,
> - English Proficiency score (see Rules and Regulations on the home page for language exam
>   information),
> - Citizens of the Republic of Turkey to have at least 55 points of equal weight in the ALES exam
> - ID card copy
>
> Students who are accepted to the program are given a scientific preparation program when
> necessary. […] A minimum grade of 3.00/4.00 is required for our students who will graduate from
> the program. Our students who will graduate from the program are required to publish at least one
> article listed by international indexes (SSCI, SCI-Expanded, AHCI, AHCI-Expanded) before making
> their thesis defense.

**Scope limit.** This list is published for the Business Management PhD only. It must **not** be
copied onto the Marketing, MIS, Tourism or Educational Administration pages as though GAU had
published it there. Those pages may describe the general shape (recognised master's degree, academic
transcripts, English evidence) and point the reader to the Institute. → **GAP-SOURCE-GAU-05**.

Glossary for prose: **ALES** = Akademik Personel ve Lisansüstü Eğitimi Giriş Sınavı, the Turkish
academic postgraduate entrance examination. The 55-point rule is stated for Turkish citizens only.

## 6. Common doctoral structure

Published on multiple GAU pages in near-identical wording:

> PhD program consists of 7 courses (21 credits in total), plus a Seminar (non-credit) and a Thesis
> (non-credit). After a success completion of the courses, students are asked to take qualification
> exam. The success pass of the Qualification Exam will allow students to start working on their
> Thesis.

Credit convention on the course tables: taught courses are **CR:(3,0)3 / ECTS:10**; the Seminar
carries **ECTS 20**; the Qualifying examination and each Thesis semester carry **ECTS 30**.

**Minimum thesis duration is NOT uniform across programmes** — GAU publishes 2 semesters for some
and 3 for others, and the Business Management pages contradict each other. Each programme dossier
records its own value. → **GAP-SOURCE-GAU-06**.

## 7. Career data

Of the five draftable GAU doctorates, only **Business Management** publishes concrete destinations
("Teaching staff at universities"; "Senior executive in the public and private sector") and only
**Management Information Systems** publishes a general statement ("Students are prepared for careers
both in academics and the industry").

On the `/en/prospective/program/…` pages the **"Career Opportunities" and "Student Opinions"
headings render with no content at all** for Business Management, Marketing, Management Information
Systems and Tourism & Hospitality.

Per the binding Phase-8 career-data honesty decision: reproduce only what is published, and add an
FAQ that tells the reader plainly that GAU's published career information is limited.
→ **GAP-SOURCE-GAU-07**.
