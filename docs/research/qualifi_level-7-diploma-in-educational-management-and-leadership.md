## 3. ⚠️⚠️ The named MSc — second only to the LLM claim for mis-selling risk

Qualifi names a **specific named degree, "an MSc in Educational Management and
Leadership"**, and describes the route as "complete a dissertation to then receive" it.
Only S.No 64 (LLM) does anything comparable. **No university partner is named**, no list
exists, no terms, costs, timescales or approval conditions are published.

`GAP-SOURCE-QF-31` at maximum strength, with the extra duty to state that:
- this diploma is **not** an MSc and awards no degree;
- Qualifi names **no** university partner;
- whether any university admits a holder, awards credit, or offers an MSc of that exact
  title is entirely that university's decision;
- readers should contact the university they have in mind before enrolling.
Forbidden: "get your MSc with just a dissertation", "leads to an MSc", "MSc pathway".
→ **GAP-SOURCE-QF-85.**

## 4. ⭐ Qualification Overview — on-topic and usable

Verbatim:
> "The qualification has been created to develop learners' understanding of **policy,
> management theory and practice in education**. It provides learners with an
> opportunity to engage with the challenges facing professionals and policy makers and
> will provide knowledge that underpins the ability to work as an effective manager in
> the education sector.
>
> The qualification also combines both theoretical and practical knowledge in the
> education profession and will develop and enhance knowledge and skills in the areas of
> **leading change, effective performance and team leadership**. Learners will be able to
> work in a variety of roles within **education administration and/or management**."

⭐ Clear, subject-specific and free of the generic business boilerplate that spoils
S.No 60, 61 and 62. "Education administration and/or management" is a precise and honest
statement of destination — it is explicitly **not** a teaching qualification, and that
distinction is the editorial spine of this page.

⚠️ The final sentence ("Learners will be able to work in a variety of roles…") is an
employability assertion with no supporting data. Attribute it to Qualifi; do not restate
as fact. The `careers` block must still open with the no-destination-data preamble.

## 5. ⭐ Units (6, verbatim)
1. Leadership Qualities and Practice in Education
2. Contemporary Issues in Education: Theory, Policy and Practice
3. Managing Change in an Educational Context
4. **Pedagogy and Practice in Education**
5. **Leading Reflective Practice in Education**
6. Research Methods in Education

Six units, 120 credits, no split, no per-unit credits → GAP-SOURCE-QF-08.

⭐ Every unit is education-specific — a markedly better list than the page's outcomes.
**Pedagogy and Practice** and **Leading Reflective Practice** are the strongest
differentiators from S.No 46 (Level 5) and from the Gatehouse award at S.No 22.

⚠️ Unit 4 (Pedagogy and Practice) is the only unit touching teaching itself. It must not
be presented as teacher training — see §7.

## 6. ⚠️ Learning Outcomes — the generic family set again, and mis-numbered

Published as eight numbered items, but items 3 and 4 are **one sentence split across two
numbers**. Read as seven:
1. To understand and apply the principles of management strategy in an educational environment.
2. Review and apply the principles of educational leadership within industry.
3. To improve the employability of learners by allowing them to explore the relationship between management theories and their practical application in education.
4. Analyse problem-solving techniques specific to education.
5. Select, collate, review and analyse information from a wide range of sources.
6. Work independently and as part of a team.
7. Manage one's own personal development and growth.

⚠️ **Outcomes 3–7 are the generic tail shared verbatim with S.No 60, 61, 62 and 64**
(see `qualifi_level-7-LEGACY-2019-FAMILY-SHARED.md` §2), including the same
"information **form** a wide range of sources" typo — correct silently to "from".
Only outcomes 1, 2 and 4 are education-specific, and even those are the generic template
with "education" substituted. → GAP-STYLE-QF-62 extends to this row.

⚠️ Outcome 2 says "educational leadership within **industry**" — a copy-paste artefact
from a business award. Report as published and note the oddity, or omit with a stated
reason. → GAP-SOURCE-QF-86.

⚠️ Numbering fault 3/4 → GAP-SOURCE-QF-36.

**Differentiate this page on its units and overview, never on its outcomes.** Supplement
the `learning` block from the six unit titles under a derived-coverage preamble.

## 7. ⚠️⚠️ Mandatory limitation — teaching status

Qualifi publishes nothing about teacher status, and the Pedagogy unit invites a false
assumption. The page must state plainly that this qualification:
- does **not** confer **Qualified Teacher Status (QTS)** and is not Initial Teacher
  Training or Education;
- is **not** a PGCE and is not accredited by the Department for Education;
- does not qualify anyone to teach in a UK school, and is not required or recognised for
  registration with any teaching regulator;
- is a **management and leadership** qualification for people working in education
  administration and management.
The same rule applies to S.No 46 and must be written here in entirely different words.
