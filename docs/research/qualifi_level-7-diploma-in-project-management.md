## 3. ⭐ Entry Requirements — the fullest at Level 7, and genuinely different

Unlike S.No 48, 49, 50 and 51, which publish only two bullets, this award publishes a
complete entry block:

- Approved centres are responsible for reviewing and deciding on the applicant's ability
  to complete the programme and meet its demands; the initial assessment must consider
  the support available to meet individual learner needs.
- Designed to be accessible without artificial barriers.
- **Applicants must be aged 19 or over** — the only "19 or over" found anywhere in the
  Qualifi set, which elsewhere uses 16 or 18.
- Expected to hold a **Level 6 qualification or a first degree**.
- IELTS 6 (or equivalent) where English is not the first language.
- International qualifications checked for appropriate enrolment to UK higher education
  postgraduate programmes. **At Level 7 this clause is contextually correct** and may be
  reported, unlike at Levels 3 and 5.
- Normally two supporting references, at least one preferably academic.

**Do not assume the two-bullet form from the sibling management awards applies here.**

## 4. Qualification Overview and stated aims (verbatim substance)

> "The aim of the QUALIFI Level 7 Diploma in Project Management is to help develop the
> learners' knowledge, understanding and skills required to deal with the complexities
> of leadership and strategic management in a business context, and to develop their
> ability to lead change in organisations."

Qualifi then lists seven opportunities the award aims to give learners:

1. Apply theoretical understanding of management to complex and current business issues, with a view to improving business practice.
2. Reflect critically on leadership skills and thereby prepare for senior project management roles within their organisation.
3. Develop a lifelong learning attitude to education and training.
4. Gain critical insight into contemporary research and leading-edge practice in project management.
5. Develop considerable autonomy in learning, and enhance the ability to plan and implement consultancy projects in a group context.
6. Generate originality and enterprise in approaching complex issues.
7. Demonstrate self-direction, initiative and autonomy in research and scholarship, towards a new contribution to knowledge in a chosen field of management.

⚠️ **Item 6 reads "complex PR issues" in the original.** "PR" is almost certainly a typo
or a leftover from another qualification — nothing else on the page concerns public
relations. **Omit "PR"**; do not infer a public relations element.
→ **GAP-SOURCE-QF-40.**

⭐ This is bespoke, substantial copy — notably more ambitious than the cluster awards,
with consultancy projects, originality and "a new contribution to knowledge". Item 7 is
near-doctoral in tone for a Level 7 diploma; report it as Qualifi's stated aim, not as a
promise of research standing.

**Twelve units against 120 credits** — the largest unit count at Level 7 so far, and no
mandatory/optional split is published. → GAP-SOURCE-QF-08.

⚠️ **Unit 11 is simply "Project Management"** on a qualification of the same name, and
unit 6 is "Principles of Project Management". The relationship between units 6 and 11 is
unexplained. Note it; do not resolve it. → **GAP-SOURCE-QF-41.**

Observations worth using: procurement and contract management, logistics, global supply
chain and finance give this a strong delivery-and-commercial flavour rather than a purely
methodological one. Risk appears twice (units 2 and 9).

## 6. Learning Outcomes — mis-numbered in the source

Qualifi prints seven numbered items, but item 2 contains **two** outcomes run together:

1. Understand and apply the principles of project management in a business environment
2. Review and apply the principles of project management within industry
3. *(printed inside item 2)* Understand and apply the principles of project management in a specific environment
4. Improve employability by exploring the relationship between theories and their practical application in the business world
5. Analyse problem-solving techniques specific to business and industry
6. Select, collate, review and analyse information from a wide range of sources *(source reads "form")*
7. Work independently and as part of a team
8. Manage one's own personal development and growth

So there are **eight** outcomes presented as seven. The split is unambiguous from the
text. → GAP-SOURCE-QF-36 (same class as S.No 49 and 51).

These are the generic management outcomes with "project management" substituted for
"business management". The real differentiation lies in the seven stated aims (§4) and
the unit list, not here.

## 8. Notes

- April 2025 specification makes this one of the freshest documents in the set.
- Overlaps S.No 47 (Business Strategy), which offers Project Management as one of five
  specialism routes. The honest distinction: there, project management is one pathway
  within a general strategy award; here it is the entire qualification across twelve units.
- The "19 or over" minimum and the full entry block make this the most formally gated
  Level 7 award found so far.
