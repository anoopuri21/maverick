## 1. ⚠️⚠️ THE MASTER'S TOP-UP CLAIM — the most sensitive issue in the group

Level 7 pages publish progression routes that reference a master's degree. Wording
seen so far, verbatim:

- **S.No 66 (Data Science):** "A Qualifi Level 7 and/or 8 Diploma, directly into
  employment in an associated profession, or an appropriate **dissertation-only for a
  Master's Degree at one of our University partnerships**."
- **S.No 48 (Ethical Leadership):** "A university partner to complete a relevant
  master's degree, **subject to approval**, or directly into employment in an
  associated profession."
- **S.No 49 (Executive Management):** "**Dissertation to complete Master's Degree at UK
  University**."

**What this does and does not establish.**

✅ Qualifi publishes these as progression routes. We may report them as such.
❌ Qualifi **names no university partner anywhere on its website** — same unnamed-partner
problem as GAP-SOURCE-QF-11 at Level 5.
❌ A Qualifi Level 7 diploma is **not** a master's degree and does not become one. Only a
recognised degree-awarding body can award a master's.
❌ Qualifi does not publish, for any award, which universities accept it, on what terms,
or whether a route is currently open.

**Editorial rule for every Level 7 page (`GAP-SOURCE-QF-31`):**
1. State the route as Qualifi publishes it, using Qualifi's own hedge where one exists
   ("subject to approval" on S.No 48 is especially useful — quote it).
2. State plainly on the same page that the diploma is not itself a master's degree.
3. State that no partner university is named and that admission is the university's
   decision.
4. Never write "leads to a master's", "equivalent to a master's", or "top up to an MBA".
5. Where a page says "at UK University" without a hedge (S.No 49), the caveat must still
   be supplied by us — the absence of Qualifi's hedge does not make it a guarantee.

This is the Level 7 equivalent of the not-a-licence rule applied at Levels 3 and 5, and
it applies to **every** row in this group that mentions a degree.
