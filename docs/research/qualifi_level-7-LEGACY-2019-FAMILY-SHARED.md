## 2. ⚠️⚠️ The shared generic outcome set — the worst duplication hazard in Level 7

S.No 61 and 62 publish **the same eight outcomes**, differing only in the subject noun
("human resource management" vs "accounting and finance") in outcomes 1–3. S.No 60
publishes six of the same eight, with the subject noun removed entirely.

The shared tail, **verbatim identical on all three**:
- "To improve the employability of learners by allowing them to explore the relationship between theories and their practical application in the business world."
- "Analyse problem solving techniques specific to business and industry"
- "Select, collate, review and analyse information **form** a wide range of sources" (typo in source on all three)
- "Work independently and as part of a team"
- "Manage one's own personal development and growth"

**Rules, mandatory:**
1. Never describe these outcomes in similar prose on more than one page. Each page gets a
   genuinely fresh construction — the dedupe scanner will catch anything else.
2. Differentiate the three pages on **units only**. The units are entirely distinct and
   genuinely subject-specific; the outcomes are not.
3. Each page must state plainly, in its own words, that Qualifi's published learning
   outcomes for this award are general business-management statements and that the
   subject-specific content sits in the unit list.
4. Correct the "form a wide range of sources" typo silently to "from" on each page.
5. S.No 60 and 61 both print their final two outcomes run together on one line
   (`GAP-SOURCE-QF-36`); S.No 62 prints all eight separately. Read all three as the full set.

→ **GAP-STYLE-QF-62** (the Level 7 analogue of GAP-STYLE-QF-37, which covers 48/49/50/51).

## 3. ⚠️⚠️ The shared master's claim — maximum-strength QF-31

"Dissertation to complete Master's Degree at UK University" is unhedged, names no
university, states no conditions and is the sole published route on all three pages.

Every one of the three pages must, in **different words each time**:
- quote the route exactly as Qualifi publishes it;
- say plainly the diploma is not a master's degree and does not award one;
- say Qualifi names no university and publishes no partner list or transfer terms;
- say acceptance and any credit given is the receiving university's decision alone;
- advise contacting the intended university before enrolling.

Forbidden on all three: "leads to a master's", "equivalent to a master's", "finish your
master's with just a dissertation", "top-up to an MBA/MSc".

## 4. Shared entry block — write it three different ways

"Level 6 Qualification or; First Degree" is the entire published block. There is **no**
centre-review paragraph, **no** age minimum, **no** IELTS requirement and **no**
reference requirement on any of the three — unlike the newer Level 7 pages (52–57).

Each page must report this exactly, and advise confirming English language and
documentation requirements with the approved centre, since Qualifi publishes none.
The phrasing must differ on each page; the "18 or over / IELTS 6 / two references"
sentence family has already collided four times at Level 5. → GAP-SOURCE-QF-54.

## 5. Shared honesty positions
- No fee, no duration, no TQT on any of the three (QF-01, QF-03).
- No destinations, salaries or employment rates (QF-04); and since the progression panel
  names no employment route at all, the `careers` block must say so rather than invent one.
- No professional body alignment published for any of them — specifically **no CIPD**
  (61) and **no ACCA, CIMA, ICAEW or CIPFA** (62). Claim none; direct readers to those
  bodies. → GAP-SOURCE-QF-63.
- Course brochures are from 2019, unread, and must be named as unread if mentioned at all.

## 6. Overview text — shared boilerplate on 60, 61 and 62

All three open from the same source paragraph about meeting "the needs of senior/middle
managers in the running of their businesses, and to facilitate their progression to
high(er) levels within their organisation". S.No 60's version is garbled
(`GAP-SOURCE-QF-58`); 61 extends it with the "they do not have to be expert in all
areas" passage; 62 trims it and adds "and accountants".

**Never paraphrase this paragraph in similar terms twice.** Treat it as boilerplate, not
as subject content, and build each overview from the units instead.
