## 3. Qualification Overview (verbatim)

> "The Extended Diploma is combination of both the Level 4 and Level 5 Diplomas. All
> aims and outcomes are duplicated.
>
> Completing the Extended Diploma has a value of 240 credits. This is equivalent to a
> Foundation Degree and as such allows access to the final year at one of our
> University partners for a related Honours Degree. Learners who do not complete
> Extended Diploma but achieve a minimum of 120 credits at Level 4 will be awarded the
> QUALIFI Level 4 Diploma in Psychology 610/2149/0."

**Three facts of high value, all unique to this page:**
1. The extended award = Level 4 + Level 5 diplomas combined.
2. Qualifi states 240 credits is **"equivalent to a Foundation Degree"**. This is the
   awarding body's own comparison and may be quoted **with attribution to Qualifi**.
   Do not present it as an independent or regulatory equivalence.
3. **A published fallback award:** a learner who does not finish but achieves at least
   120 credits at Level 4 receives the Qualifi Level 4 Diploma in Psychology
   `610/2149/0`. No other Qualifi page seen states a fallback. Genuinely reassuring
   and worth featuring.

⚠️ **"one of our University partners"** — Qualifi names no partner institution
anywhere. Do **not** imply a named or guaranteed university route. Report only that
Qualifi refers to university partners, and that acceptance rests with the university.

## 4. Units (12, verbatim)

1. The Scope of Psychology
2. Social And Developmental Psychology
3. Biopsychology
4. The Development of Psychological Attachments
5. Processes Of Human Memory
6. Research Methods and Investigating Psychology
7. Psychological Approaches to Depression and Schizophrenia
8. Visual Perception in a Psychological Context
9. Social Psychology: Pro and Anti-Social Behaviour
10. The Diversity of Addiction
11. Individual Differences: Intelligence, Personality and Criminology
12. Contemporary Issues and Debates in Psychology

Spans the Level 4 and Level 5 halves. No mandatory/optional split, no per-unit credits.

## 5. Learning Outcomes (8, verbatim — note inconsistent casing in the original)

1. Understand the key methodological issues and theorising in social psychology.
2. Understand the main theoretical v views in cognition and development. *(sic — "v views")*
3. Understand the structure and function of the nervous system and the system's location in the brain.
4. Understand learning theory of attachment.
5. understand the diagnosis and classification of schizophrenia;
6. understand the concepts of criminological psychology;
7. understand the explanations for substance abuse and dependence;
8. understand the contribution of debates to the field of psychology.

## 6. Entry Requirements (verbatim substance)

- Approved centres decide on the applicant's ability to complete and meet the demands;
  initial assessment considers available learner support.
- Accessible without artificial barriers. **Applicants must be aged 18 or over.**
- IELTS 6 (or equivalent) where English is not the first language.
- International qualifications checked for appropriate enrolment.
- Normally two supporting references, at least one preferably academic.
- **"In certain circumstances, applicants with considerable experience but no formal
  qualifications may be considered, subject to interview and being able to demonstrate
  their ability to cope with the demands of the qualification."**

No prior qualification level is required. The experience clause is a real open door
and matches the wording on the Level 3 IT award (S.No 32).
Carries the incongruous "postgraduate programmes" clause — **do not repeat it.**

## 9. Notes

- The fallback Level 4 award at 120 credits is the strongest learner-protection fact
  found anywhere in the Qualifi set. Feature it.
- Foundation-degree equivalence: quote as Qualifi's own statement, never as fact
  established by a regulator.
- Content is academic psychology (memory, perception, attachment, biopsychology), not
  counselling or therapy training. Say so, so no reader mistakes it for clinical training.
