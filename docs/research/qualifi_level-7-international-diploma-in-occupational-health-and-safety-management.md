## 3. Qualification Overview (verbatim)

> "The level 7 International Diploma in Occupational Health and Safety Management is a
> qualification for aspiring health and safety professionals. It is aimed at learners who
> are responsible for developing and applying health and safety procedures on a
> day-to-day basis in an organisation. They are likely to be managers looking to improve
> their knowledge and skills. The Diploma is designed to provide learners with the
> expertise required to undertake a career as a health and safety manager and it also
> provides a sound basis for progression to postgraduate study."

⚠️ Internal tension: the overview says it "provides a sound basis for progression to
**postgraduate study**", while the specifications panel names no university route at all.
Report both, note the mismatch, attribute nothing further. → GAP-SOURCE-QF-52.

⭐ Audience: "responsible for developing and applying health and safety procedures on a
**day-to-day basis**". Operational, hands-on — precisely the opposite of S.No 57's
strategy-translating manager. That contrast is the editorial angle for both pages.

## 4. ⚠️ Units — published as 5 then 3, but there are 8

The page prints a five-item list, then three further items as separate single-item
blocks. All eight are units:

1. Management-Driven, Risk-Based Safety Management Systems
2. Globalisation, Sustainability and Safety Culture Performance
3. Digitalisation and Incident Investigation
4. Development as a Strategic Manager
5. Safety Programs and Concepts
6. Ergonomics
7. Industrial Hygiene and Occupational Health
8. Fire Safety

The 12 published outcomes map onto all eight, which confirms the count.
→ **GAP-SOURCE-QF-53** (list fragmented across four HTML blocks; we read it as eight).

**Two source typos to handle:**
- Unit 7 is printed "Industrial Hygiene and **Occupa4onal** Health" — an obvious OCR or
  ligature corruption. Print the corrected "Occupational" and note the correction.
- Unit 5 is "Safety **Programs** and Concepts" — American spelling in an **official unit
  title**. Per the standing precedent, **do not anglicise a title**; reproduce it exactly
  and add `"Safety Programs and Concepts"` to the Qualifi block of the `WHITELIST` in
  `tools/qa_gate.py` before running the gate.

⭐ Eight named technical units — **Ergonomics, Industrial Hygiene, Fire Safety,
Digitalisation and Incident Investigation** — make this the most concretely specified
safety award in the tracker. No other Qualifi page names fire safety or ergonomics.

## 5. Learning Outcomes (12, verbatim — the longest set in the tracker)
1. Apply and make critiques of different implementation standards of occupational health and safety management.
2. Evaluate the role of strategic leadership in risk management.
3. Review the impact of globalisation in an organisation's safety culture.
4. Understand and apply the principles of sustainability in the safety sector.
5. Analyse problem-solving techniques that are specific to risk assessment.
6. Manage strategic risks in the organisation health and safety domain.
7. **Analyse the impact of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in occupational health and safety risk management.**
8. Evaluate risk control measures from a wide range of hazards.
9. Apply principles of safety concepts and safety calculation.
10. Analyse ergonomic principles, human factors, and workplace design for occupational safety improvement.
11. Evaluate industrial hygiene principles, hazard recognition and exposure prevention strategies.
12. Assess fire safety principles, fire prevention systems, emergency response and waste management.

⭐⭐ **Outcome 7 is the only mention of artificial intelligence in any outcome set in the
entire tracker**, and outcome 12's waste management is also unique. Both are strong,
genuinely distinctive content.

⚠️ Source uses "globalization" and "organisation" in the same list; write British
English in our prose, as these are body text and not titles.

Twelve outcomes exceeds the eight-item budget — select the eight most distinctive
(include 7, 10, 11, 12) and note that Qualifi publishes twelve in total.
