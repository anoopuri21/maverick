## 3. ⚠️⚠️ The progression claim — the strongest and least qualified in the tracker

"Dissertation to complete Master's Degree at UK University" is published as the **only**
progression route, with no Level 8 option and no employment option. It is phrased as a
statement of fact, with no hedge, no named university, no conditions and no mention of
university approval.

**`GAP-SOURCE-QF-31` applies here at maximum strength.** The page must:
- Quote Qualifi's route exactly as published.
- State plainly that this diploma **is not** a master's degree and does not award one.
- State that **Qualifi names no university**, publishes no list of partner institutions
  and publishes no terms for the arrangement.
- State that whether any university accepts the diploma, and on what basis, is entirely
  that university's decision.
- Advise contacting the intended university directly before enrolling.

**Never** write "leads to a master's", "complete your master's with a dissertation", or
any construction that treats the top-up as guaranteed. This is the single highest
mis-selling risk on any page in the project.

## 4. ⚠️ Qualification Overview — generic, and partly copied from another qualification

Verbatim:

> "The Qualifications have been created to develop and reward the business managers of
> today and the future, and to continue to bring recognition and professionalism to the
> management sectors. The Level 7 Diploma in Hospitality and Tourism Management
> qualification is designed for managers who have the expert and personal encouragement
> in deciphering organisational strategy into effective operational performance. This
> programme provides learners with an advanced qualification that supports their
> development as managers within the tourism and hospitality industry through a
> lifelong-learning orientation."

**Defects → GAP-SOURCE-QF-58:**
- Sentence 1 is **generic boilerplate about business managers**, plural "Qualifications",
  shared with the general management cluster. Nothing about hospitality or tourism.
- Sentence 2 is **garbled**: "managers who have the **expert and personal encouragement**
  in **deciphering** organisational strategy into effective operational performance". The
  intended wording is plainly the S.No 57 formulation, "managers who have the authority
  and personal attributes to **translate** organisational strategy into effective
  operational performance". Here it has been mangled into nonsense.
  **Do not quote this sentence. Do not reproduce "deciphering".**
- Only the final clause — development as managers in the tourism and hospitality
  industry — is actually about the subject.

The overview therefore supplies **almost no subject content**. Build the page's
description from the four unit titles, and state openly that Qualifi's published
overview is brief and largely generic.

## 5. ⚠️ Units — only FOUR for a 120-credit award
1. Marketing Approaches in Hospitality and Tourism
2. Strategic Planning Facets in Hospitality and Tourism
3. Tourism Policy in International Context and Development
4. The Culture and Society in Tourism

Four units, no split, no per-unit credits → GAP-SOURCE-QF-08.

⚠️ **Four units for 120 credits is the lowest unit-to-credit ratio in the tracker**
(elsewhere 120 credits carries six to eight units). Either units are unusually large or
the published list is incomplete. Note the ratio, do not speculate, and point readers to
the specification. → **GAP-SOURCE-QF-59.**

⚠️ Note the weighting: **three of four units are tourism-led** (policy, culture and
society, and a shared marketing unit). Hospitality appears in only two unit titles and
is never addressed on its own. Honest framing: this is a tourism-weighted award.

## 6. ⚠️ Learning Outcomes — six, generic, and none mention hospitality or tourism
1. To understand and apply the principles of management in a business environment.
2. To improve the employability of learners by allowing them to explore the relationship between management theories and their practical application in the business world.
3. Analyse problem solving techniques specific to business and industry.
4. Select, collate, review and analyse information from a wide range of sources.
5. Work independently and as part of a team.
6. Manage one's own personal development and growth.

⚠️ Published as five numbered items, with **outcomes 5 and 6 run together on one line** —
the same formatting fault logged as GAP-SOURCE-QF-36 on S.No 49, 51 and 52. Read as six.

⚠️ **Not one outcome refers to hospitality or tourism.** This is the generic Qualifi
business-management outcome set, and outcomes 1–6 here are close kin to those on other
management rows. → **GAP-SOURCE-QF-60**, and a severe dedupe hazard: these must be
written in entirely fresh prose, and the page must say plainly that Qualifi's published
outcomes are generic management outcomes while the subject content sits in the units.

⚠️ Outcome 4 has a source typo, "information **form** a wide range of sources" —
correct silently to "from".

## 9. Editorial note

This page has thinner source material than any other Level 7 row: a garbled generic
overview, four units, six off-topic outcomes and a two-line entry block. The honest
approach — describe what the four units actually cover, state clearly what Qualifi has
not published, and treat the master's route with full caution — is also the most useful
page we can build. Do not pad it with invented detail.
