### Required titles in M1 timetable (selected)

- Years 1–2: `Introduction to Business Management`; `Business Mathematics I`; `Financial Accounting I`; `Business Law`; `Statistics II`. Shared foundations; do not use them as this degree's unique selling point.
- Year 3: `Principles of Marketing`; `Consumer Behavior` (official spelling), `Marketing Strategy`; `E-Commerce`; `Internship - 30 Calendar Days` (M1 has this exact course title).
- Year 4: `International Marketing`; `Integrated Marketing Communications`; `Marketing Seminar`.
- **Electives, not guaranteed required subjects**: `Business to Business Marketing`; `Marketing Channels`; `Sales Management`; `Advertising and Promotion`; `Brand Management`; `Marketing Research`; `Retail Marketing & Management`.

## Entry, curriculum, funding, outcomes: limits

No marketing-specific admission scores on M1/M2. G5 general English-medium FES/IELTS exemptions and G4 application-letter process apply but do not guarantee admission. M1 describes an internship course; it does not guarantee a paid placement. Dated G6 faculty tuition ≠ present-day personalised fee. The senior marketing titles in GAU's career examples are **possibilities** rather than jobs a new graduate is qualified to occupy immediately. The official page has no placement rates or salaries.
