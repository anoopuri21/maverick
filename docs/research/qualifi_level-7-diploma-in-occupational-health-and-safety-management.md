## 4. Qualification Overview — bespoke and substantial (the best written in the group)

Named content, verbatim in substance:
- Develops OH&S management skills for **managers who have the authority and personal
  attributes to translate organisational strategy into effective operational performance**.
- Reflects current practice; learners develop and expand a high-level understanding of
  occupational health and safety in the workplace.
- Addresses multidisciplinary topics: **health and safety management practice, health and
  safety management systems, strategic risk intervention, strategies to improve
  occupational wellbeing, and sustainability and ethics**.
- The **Advanced Research Methods** unit develops a critical understanding of the
  philosophical, practical and ethical concepts of research within a health and safety
  environment.
- Aims to make learners **independent, self-directed learners** with the tools and
  motivation to keep learning, developing and reflecting on practice throughout a career.

⭐ "Managers who have the authority … to translate organisational strategy into effective
operational performance" is a sharply drawn audience and the strongest single line for
the editorial angle. No other page in the set defines its reader this precisely.

## 5. Units (6, verbatim)
1. Health and Safety Management Practice
2. Effectiveness of Health and Safety Management Systems
3. Factors Affecting Risk and Strategic Risk Intervention
4. Strategic Commitment to Health and Well-Being
5. Sustainability and Ethics in Health and Safety Practice
6. Advanced Research Methods

Six units, 120 credits, no split, no per-unit credits → GAP-SOURCE-QF-08.
Note the unit list maps 1:1 onto the overview's topic list — rare internal consistency,
and worth relying on.

## 6. Learning Outcomes (7, verbatim)
1. Understand the factors that influence an organisation's culture and atmosphere around health and safety.
2. Be able to evaluate organisational practices and structures relating to health and safety.
3. Understand what contributes to being able to present a plan which promotes a health and safety strategy for the organisation.
4. Be able to assess health and safety risks within the organisation.
5. Be able to promote plans and engage all levels of the business so that the strategy is actioned, monitored, and evaluated.
6. Understand the scope for ethical business practices on the part of the health and safety professional and the scope for developing engagement and commitment to ethical behaviours and measures.
7. Be able to plan and prepare a research project.

Clean, bespoke, defect-free. Seven against a required eight — supplement with one item
drawn from the overview (sustainability, or occupational wellbeing strategy), labelled
as derived.

## 7. Entry Requirements
- Centre reviews ability to complete and meet the demands; considers available support.
- Accessible without artificial barriers. **Applicants must be aged 21 or over**
  (joint highest in the set, with S.No 54).
- Learners are expected to possess **either**:
  - a minimum of a Level 6 qualification; **or**
  - an **honours degree (2.2)** with relevant knowledge and experience in the
    occupational health and safety sector.
- IELTS 6 (or equivalent) where English is not the first language.
- International qualifications checked (postgraduate clause — correct at this level).
- Normally two supporting references, at least one preferably academic.

⭐ **"An honours degree (2.2)" is the only published degree classification anywhere in
the Qualifi set.** Strong, concrete and highly quotable. Report it exactly.
