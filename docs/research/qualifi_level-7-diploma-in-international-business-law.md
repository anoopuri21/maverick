## 3. Qualification Overview (verbatim)

> "The Qualification has been created to develop and reward learners with legal skills
> and knowledge in international business law and to continue to bring recognition and
> professionalism to a wide range of sectors. The aim of this programme is to develop
> legal skills, analysis, logical thinking and knowledge that can usefully be applied in
> further study, legal profession and public service. It provides students with
> understanding of law in context of business to equip them with abilities needed to work
> with advance issues."

⚠️ Sentence 1 reuses the "bring recognition and professionalism" boilerplate also found
on S.No 60. Sentence 3 is ungrammatical ("in context of business", "work with advance
issues") — do not quote it. Sentence 2 is usable: legal skills, analysis and logical
thinking applied in further study, the legal profession and **public service**.

⭐ "Public service" as a named destination appears on no other law page and is worth
using as a differentiator.

## 4. ⭐ Units — mandatory and optional, published separately

**Mandatory (6):**
1. International Trade Law
2. Commercial Trust Law
3. Industrial and Intellectual Property Law
4. Law of Financial Crimes
5. Company Law
6. Legal Research and Research Methods

**Optional (2 listed):**
7. International Commercial Arbitration
8. Corporate Governance – Principles and Practices

⭐ This is one of only a handful of Level 7 rows publishing a **mandatory/optional
split** (with S.No 47 and 51). A genuine structural differentiator.

⚠️ Qualifi does **not** say how many optional units must be taken, nor the credit value
of any unit, nor whether both optionals are required to reach 120 credits. Report the
split as published and state the choice rules are not published; refer readers to the
specification and the centre. → **GAP-SOURCE-QF-75.**

⭐ Content is sharply commercial and genuinely international — trade law, financial
crime, intellectual property, arbitration. Nothing here overlaps S.No 63's domestic
foundation subjects, which makes the two law pages easy to separate honestly.

## 5. Learning Outcomes (7, verbatim)
1. To understand wide ranges of subjects in international business law.
2. Develop an understanding of the legal framework that governs international business transactions within industry.
3. Demonstrate an understanding of commercial trust law, company law, international trade law, intellectual property law, and the law of financial crimes in international business.
4. Develop research skills and the ability to write on key areas in international business law.
5. Select, collate, review and analyse information from a wide range of sources.
6. Work independently and as part of a team.
7. Manage one's own personal development and growth.

⭐ Outcomes 1–4 are genuinely subject-specific and usable; outcome 3 simply restates the
unit list.
⚠️ **Outcomes 5, 6 and 7 are the generic tail shared verbatim with S.No 60, 61 and 62**
(see `qualifi_level-7-LEGACY-2019-FAMILY-SHARED.md` §2), including the same "information
**form** a wide range of sources" typo — correct silently to "from". Write them in fresh
prose here; the scanner will catch any echo of the other three pages.
→ GAP-STYLE-QF-62 extends to this row.

⚠️ Source prints outcomes 2 and 3 with mid-sentence line breaks; read as single items.

## 6. Entry Requirements — **identical wording to S.No 63**
- Learners who possess qualifications at **Level 6**; and/or
- Learners who have **work experience in the legal sector**; and/or
- Learners who possess a Level 6 qualification **in another discipline** and want to
  develop their careers in business.

No age minimum, no IELTS requirement, no references, no centre-review paragraph
→ GAP-SOURCE-QF-54. On this page the "careers in business" wording is **appropriate**,
unlike on S.No 63 (GAP-SOURCE-QF-72) — which confirms the clause was written for this
award and copied onto the other. Note it; it does not change what we publish.

⚠️ Because the entry block is word-for-word identical to S.No 63's, it must be written
up in completely different prose on the two pages. Known collision risk.

## 7. Careers, duration, fees
No destinations, salary data or employment rates. Three published routes.

⚠️⚠️ **MANDATORY LIMITATION**, written differently from S.No 63 and S.No 38: this is not
a qualifying law degree, not an LLB and not an LLM; it is not an SQE or Bar route; it
confers no right to practise law, to use the title solicitor, barrister or lawyer, or to
carry out reserved legal activities in the UK or any other jurisdiction; and no SRA, BSB
or CILEX recognition or exemption is published. International learners should check the
rules of the jurisdiction they intend to work in. Direct readers to the relevant regulator.

No fee, no duration, no TQT.
