## 3. ⭐⭐ Qualification Overview — the most carefully worded page in the whole tracker

Verbatim:
> "The overall aim of this qualification is to provide **non-law graduates** with a
> grounding and knowledge of foundation law subjects which will provide a sound basis to
> continue further in a law career. **This is not a preparatory programme for the
> Solicitors Qualifying Examination (SQE) examinations** but is aimed to provide a basis
> for further legal study and/or training.
>
> The qualification **is aligned with aspects of the SQE** in order to provide learners
> with a foundation in its various domains."

Published aims:
- support the academic stage of training through study of foundation areas of law and
  **Functioning Legal Knowledge** and **Practical Legal Skills and Knowledge** domains
  aligned with aspects of the SQE, and develop learners' approach to these studies at
  master's level;
- develop analytical, evaluative and research skills appropriate to law at master's level;
- provide the opportunity for research and in-depth study of a particular area of law
  through the **research project/dissertation**.

⭐ **Qualifi disclaims the SQE itself.** This is the only page in the tracker where the
awarding body publishes its own limitation statement, and it is unusually clear. Quote
the disclaimer and build on it rather than replacing it.

⚠️ But the two SQE statements pull in opposite directions: "not a preparatory programme
for the SQE" immediately followed by "aligned with aspects of the SQE". Both must be
reported together, never separately, or the page becomes misleading either way.
→ **GAP-SOURCE-QF-68.** The honest reading: it covers subject matter that overlaps SQE
domains, but it is not SQE preparation and sitting the SQE is a separate matter governed
by the SRA.

⚠️ The aims mention a "research project/dissertation" that **does not appear in the unit
list**. → GAP-SOURCE-QF-69. Mention the aim, do not promise the unit.

⭐ "Non-law graduates" is the clearest target audience statement in the Level 7 set and
is the editorial angle for this page: a conversion-style grounding for someone whose
degree was in something else.

## 4. ⭐ Units (8, verbatim) — a real foundation-subject curriculum
1. Legal Foundations
2. Company Law and Practice
3. Contract Law and Disputes
4. Public Law and Law of the European Union
5. Torts
6. Land Law and Property
7. Criminal Law and Practice
8. Equity and Trusts

Eight units, 120 credits, no split, no per-unit credits → GAP-SOURCE-QF-08.

⭐ These are recognisably the traditional foundations of legal knowledge — contract, tort,
public, criminal, land, equity and trusts, plus company law. Strong, concrete content and
the best unit list of any Level 7 row.

⚠️ Unit 4 includes **Law of the European Union**. Qualifi offers no comment on its
treatment post-Brexit. Report the unit title as published; infer nothing about scope.
→ GAP-SOURCE-QF-70.

## 5. Learning Outcomes (7, verbatim)
1. express in a confident and professional manner their understanding of law;
2. evidence independence of thought in a legal context;
3. demonstrate a high level of academic writing relevant to the professional nature of the Diploma;
4. apply critical understanding of law principles;
5. apply concepts outside their initial context;
6. demonstrate significant, evidenced independence of thought;
7. apply proficient communication of the results of their work.

⚠️ **Outcomes 2 and 6 are the same outcome stated twice** ("independence of thought" /
"significant, evidenced independence of thought"), and 1 and 7 both concern
communication. Effectively there are four distinct outcomes.
→ **GAP-SOURCE-QF-71.** Merge the duplicates, report four or five distinct outcomes, and
supplement from the units and published aims under a derived-coverage preamble.

⚠️ These outcomes are entirely about **skills** — writing, thinking, communicating — and
none name a legal subject. The subject content lives in the units. Say so.

## 6. Entry Requirements (published in full, as three bullets)
- Learners who possess qualifications at **Level 6**; and/or
- Learners who have **work experience in the legal sector**; and/or
- Learners who possess a Level 6 qualification **in another discipline** and want to
  develop their careers in business.

⚠️ No age minimum, no IELTS requirement, no references and no centre-review paragraph
published → GAP-SOURCE-QF-54.

⚠️ Bullet 3 ends "and want to develop their careers **in business**" — on a law
qualification aimed at non-law graduates. Probable copy-paste from a business award.
Report the bullet as published and note the oddity; do not silently rewrite "business"
to "law". → GAP-SOURCE-QF-72.

⚠️ The connector is "and/or" throughout, so it is unclear whether the work-experience
route stands alone without a Level 6 qualification. Disclose; refer to the centre.

## 7. Careers, duration, fees
No destinations, salary data or employment rates. Three published routes.

⚠️⚠️ **MANDATORY LIMITATION — the strongest wording required on any law page.** The page
must state plainly that this qualification:
- is **not a qualifying law degree** and is not an LLB;
- is **not** the Solicitors Qualifying Examination, is **not** an SQE preparation course
  (Qualifi says so itself), and is not an SRA-recognised route to admission as a solicitor;
- is **not** a Bar course and confers no right to practise as a barrister;
- confers **no** right to provide reserved legal activities or to call oneself a lawyer,
  solicitor or barrister in any jurisdiction;
- carries no published exemption from any SRA, BSB or CILEX requirement.
Direct readers to the SRA, the Bar Standards Board and CILEX to check current routes.

The equivalent disclaimer already exists on S.No 38 and must be written here in entirely
different words. No fee, no duration, no TQT.
