## 3. Qualification Overview (verbatim, and it is very short)

> "The aim of the QUALIFI Level 7 Diploma in Psychology is to provide learners with an
> understanding of the main cognitive functions such as perception, executive functions,
> language, attention and their role in psychology. Learners will develop practical
> skills for a career in psychology.
>
> Successful completion of the QUALIFI Level 7 Diploma in Psychology provides learners
> with the opportunity to progress to further study or employment."

Two sentences of substance. The named cognitive functions — **perception, executive
functions, language, attention** — are the only concrete subject detail in the overview
and are worth using.

⚠️ The overview is narrowly **cognitive**, while the unit list is broad (psychoanalysis,
social and cultural, developmental, rehabilitation). The overview does not describe the
qualification it sits on. → **GAP-SOURCE-QF-47.** Build our overview from the units, and
say so.

## 4. Units (6, verbatim — note the inconsistent capitalisation in the source)
1. Psychoanalysis and Mental Health
2. Exploring psychology
3. Cognitive and biological psychology
4. Qualitative Research Methodology
5. Social and Cultural Psychology and Psychology of Human Development
6. Rehabilitation Psychology

Six units against 120 credits, no split, no per-unit credits → GAP-SOURCE-QF-08.
Unit 5 is clearly two subjects joined. Unit 4 is **qualitative** methods specifically,
which sits oddly with outcome 1's statistics (see below).

## 5. ⚠️ Learning Outcomes — ONLY THREE, AND ALL THREE ARE CONTAMINATED

Published verbatim:
1. "Develop an understanding of the most used statistical techniques used in
   psychological research. Practical problems around statistical procedures are
   delivered in **computer practical sessions**. These will involve real-world research
   questions being presented to students. Example data sets will be provided and analysed."
2. "Understand the research process, research techniques, research ethics and scientific
   writing skills will be covered in **'Issues in Scientific Research'**, and it is
   designed to help introduce students to the theoretical and practical complexities of
   the research process."
3. "Know the neuropsychological aspects of emotion and behaviour in healthy and clinical
   populations."

**Three separate defects → GAP-SOURCE-QF-48, the most serious outcome defect found:**
- Only **three** outcomes for a 120-credit Level 7 award (siblings publish 6 to 12).
- Outcomes 1 and 2 are **not outcomes at all** — they are lifted teaching-delivery
  copy from some other document, describing computer practical sessions and students
  being handed data sets. Qualifi does not teach; approved centres do, and Qualifi
  publishes no teaching method anywhere else. These sentences cannot be presented as
  what a learner will achieve.
- Outcome 2 names a unit, **"Issues in Scientific Research"**, that **does not exist in
  the published unit list**. Direct internal contradiction.
- Outcome 1 is about **statistical** technique, while the only research unit published
  is **Qualitative** Research Methodology. Also contradictory.

**Editorial decision (mandatory):** do **not** reproduce outcomes 1 and 2 as learning
outcomes. Report outcome 3 as published. Build the rest of the `learning` field from the
unit titles and the overview's named cognitive functions, under an explicit preamble
stating that Qualifi publishes only three outcome statements for this award, that two of
them describe teaching arrangements rather than outcomes and name a unit absent from the
unit list, and that the remainder of our list is derived from the published units. Also
disclose the contradiction in an FAQ. This is the strongest honesty case on any page so far.

## 6. Entry Requirements
- Centre reviews ability to complete and meet the demands; considers available support.
- Accessible without artificial barriers. **Applicants must be aged 19 or over.**
- IELTS 6 (or equivalent) where English is not the first language.
- International qualifications checked (postgraduate clause — correct at this level).
- Normally two supporting references, at least one preferably academic.

⚠️ **No Level 6 or psychology degree requirement published** — the same anomaly as
S.No 53 and 55, and far more striking here, since a conversion route into psychology
would normally be explicit about prior study. Report as published; flag for the reader.
→ GAP-SOURCE-QF-44.

## 7. Career outcomes

No destinations, salary data or employment rates. Published routes only, and note this
page's progression line **omits any mention of a named profession pathway**.

⚠️⚠️ **MANDATORY DISCLAIMER, non-negotiable (the strongest on any page in the tracker).**
Qualifi says learners "will develop practical skills for a career in psychology", and the
units name Rehabilitation Psychology and Mental Health. The page must therefore state
plainly:
- This qualification is **not** British Psychological Society accredited; Qualifi
  publishes no BPS accreditation and no Graduate Basis for Chartered Membership (GBC).
- It does **not** lead to registration as a practitioner psychologist with the Health
  and Care Professions Council, and does not confer any protected title
  (clinical, counselling, educational, forensic, health, occupational or sport and
  exercise psychologist).
- It is **not** a clinical or therapeutic training and gives no licence to practise
  psychotherapy, counselling or psychological assessment.
- Anyone intending to practise should check the requirements of the BPS and HCPC directly.

This mirrors the disclaimer already carried on S.No 35 (Level 5 Psychology) and must be
written in genuinely different words here.
