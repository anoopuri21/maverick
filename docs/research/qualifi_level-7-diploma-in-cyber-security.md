## 3. ⚠️⚠️ The most specific master's claim in the entire tracker

"A university partner, where they can complete **a further 60 credits** to receive a
**full master's degree**" quantifies the top-up. No other Qualifi page gives a credit
figure, and the phrase "full master's degree" is the most emphatic used anywhere.

Yet **no university partner is named**, no list exists, no terms are published and no
approval condition is stated. `GAP-SOURCE-QF-31` applies at maximum strength, with the
additional duty to handle the 60-credit figure carefully:
- report it as Qualifi's own published statement, attributed;
- state plainly that the diploma is not a master's degree and awards none;
- state that no partner university is named and no arrangement is published;
- state that whether any university accepts the 120 credits, and how many further credits
  it requires, is that university's decision and may differ from 60.
**Never** write "complete your master's with just 60 more credits" or anything implying
the top-up is arranged, guaranteed or priced. → **GAP-SOURCE-QF-79.**

## 4. Qualification Overview and published aims

> "The aim of the QUALIFI Level 7 Diploma in Cyber Security is to offer learners an
> advanced knowledge of the industry by deepening their awareness of cyber threats,
> vulnerabilities, and security technologies.
>
> The core objective … is to equip learners with the underpinning knowledge,
> understanding and skills required for a career in the cyber security sector."

**Published aims — "the opportunity to":**
1. Gain a recognised qualification from an internationally recognised Awarding Organisation.
2. Develop research and critical thinking abilities to carry out independent cyber security research.
3. Develop competence in security management (risk assessment, compliance and governance).
4. Gain in-depth knowledge of **network security, digital forensics, ethical hacking and cloud security**.
5. Develop an understanding of ethical and legal aspects relating to privacy, compliance and the legal frameworks that regulate cyber operations.

⭐ Aim 4 names **digital forensics, ethical hacking and cloud security** — concrete,
quotable technical content that appears in no unit title, so the aims are the only place
it is published. Use it, attributed to the aims.

⚠️ Aim 1, "a recognised qualification from an internationally recognised Awarding
Organisation", is a self-description, not a learner outcome, and "internationally
recognised" is Qualifi's own claim about itself. Do not restate it as fact; if mentioned
at all, attribute it. → GAP-SOURCE-QF-80.

## 5. ⚠️ Units — only FIVE
1. Fundamentals of Cyber Security
2. Network, Infrastructure and Systems Security
3. Applications of Cyber Security
4. Security Management and Governance
5. Cryptography

Published under a "Mandatory" heading with no optional list. Five units for 120 credits
is the lowest count of any 120-credit Level 7 row except S.No 60 — note the ratio, do not
speculate, point to the specification. → GAP-SOURCE-QF-08 and QF-59.

⚠️ **Mismatch between units and outcomes.** The ten published outcomes cover digital
investigations and forensics, incident management, business continuity, strategic
leadership and data protection — none of which has a matching unit title. Conversely
"Applications of Cyber Security" has no matching outcome. Report both lists as published
and do not try to reconcile them. → **GAP-SOURCE-QF-81.**

⭐ **Cryptography** as a standalone unit is distinctive and appears nowhere else.

## 6. Learning Outcomes (10, verbatim)
1. Understand key cryptographic principles and modes.
2. Understand the standards, regulations and laws that apply to business and government organisations in relation to encryption.
3. Understand the core principles of digital investigations.
4. Apply the types of tool that support professional digital investigations at a strategic level.
5. Plan for investigations and forensics teams.
6. Understand the physical and human resources required to manage a major suspected cyber security incident.
7. Apply Business Continuity Management to major incident planning and response.
8. Understand the role of senior leaders and strategic leadership.
9. Evaluate the management streams and performance monitoring mechanisms that relate to information security.
10. Understand how data protection legislation impacts considerations of strategy-setting and strategic leadership.

⭐ Strongly **managerial and governance-led**, not hands-on technical: investigations
planning, incident command, business continuity, senior leadership, data protection. That
is the honest positioning of this award and the clean separation from the Level 5 cyber
rows, which are more operational.

⚠️ Source typos: outcome 5 "Plan for an investigations and forensics teams"; outcome 8
"the role senior leaders and strategic leadership". Corrected silently above.

## 7. ⚠️⚠️ Entry Requirements — published levels contradict the qualification level

Published in full:
- Learners who possess qualifications at **Level 4**; and/or
- Learners who have some technical and risk management work experience in a computing or
  security business environment and **demonstrate ambition with clear career goals**;
- Learners who possess a **Level 5** qualification in another discipline and want to
  develop their careers in cyber security and/or risk management.

⚠️⚠️ **This is a Level 7 qualification whose published entry requirements name Level 4
and Level 5.** Every other Level 7 row in the tracker that specifies a prior
qualification asks for Level 6 or a first degree. The wording is near-identical to a
Level 6 or Level 7 template with the levels left unadjusted — almost certainly a
copy-paste error in the source.

**Handling, mandatory:** report the requirements **exactly as Qualifi publishes them**,
flag plainly that they are unusually low for a Level 7 award and sit out of step with
Qualifi's other Level 7 qualifications, and tell readers to confirm with the approved
centre before applying. Do **not** silently "correct" Level 4/5 to Level 6, and do **not**
present the low bar as a selling point. → **GAP-SOURCE-QF-77.**

⚠️ No age minimum, no IELTS requirement, no references and no centre-review paragraph
published → GAP-SOURCE-QF-54.

⚠️ "Demonstrate ambition with clear career goals" is a subjective criterion with no
published assessment method. Note it; refer to the centre.

## 8. Careers, duration, fees
No destinations, salary data, employment rates or employer names. Two published routes.

⚠️ **MANDATORY LIMITATION:** Qualifi publishes **no** alignment with the NCSC
(including no NCSC-certified degree status), CREST, CompTIA, (ISC)², ISACA, Cisco or any
other security certification or professional body, and no exemption from any of their
examinations. State plainly that this is an academic qualification, not an industry
certification, and that those credentials must be obtained separately. The same rule
applies at Level 5 (S.No 43/44) and must be written here in entirely different words.

No fee, no duration, no TQT.
