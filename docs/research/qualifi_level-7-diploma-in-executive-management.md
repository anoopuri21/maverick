## 3. Qualification Overview (verbatim)

> "The Qualifications have been created to develop and reward the business managers of
> today and the future, and to continue to bring recognition and professionalism to the
> management sectors. This qualification is aimed at those who are seeking middle and
> executive level roles in medium or large corporations. It provides a programme of
> learning for those who want to develop their knowledge, skills and understanding of
> organisations and how executive management can help to achieve organisational and
> strategic objectives."

⚠️ **The first sentence is the shared business boilerplate** already used at Level 3
(S.No 25) and Level 5 (S.No 33). **Paraphrase independently** — verbatim reuse will trip
the dedupe gate. → GAP-STYLE-QF-01.

⭐ The second and third sentences are specific and useful: aimed at people **seeking
middle and executive level roles in medium or large corporations**. That audience
statement is the cleanest differentiator within the strategic-management cluster. Note
it says *seeking*, so it is aimed at people moving up into those roles.

## 4. Units (8, verbatim)

1. Corporate Strategy
2. Corporate Finance and Financial Strategy
3. Human Resource Practice, Leadership and Managing People
4. Global Trade and its Impact on Strategy
5. Research, Problem Solving and Business Reporting
6. Operations, Quality and Supply Chain Management
7. Strategic Marketing
8. Project Management

**This is a genuine general-management spread** — strategy, finance, people, trade,
research, operations, marketing and projects. It reads like the functional map of an
executive role, which matches the stated audience. No mandatory/optional split and no
per-unit credits published.

Useful observation for the page: each unit corresponds to a function an executive is
accountable for, which makes the breadth the point rather than a weakness.

## 5. Learning Outcomes (8, verbatim — note the run-together line)

1. To understand and apply the principles of strategic leadership in a business environment
2. Review and apply the principles of business management within industry
3. To understand and apply the principles of strategic management in a specific environment
4. To improve the employability of learners by allowing them to explore the relationship between management theories and their practical application in the business world.
5. Analyse problem solving techniques specific to business and industry
6. Select, collate, review and analyse information form a wide range of sources *(sic — "form" for "from")*
7. Work independently and as part of a team
8. Manage one's own personal development and growth

⚠️ Qualifi prints items 7 and 8 on a single line ("Work independently and as part of a
team 8. Manage one's own personal development and growth"). They are plainly two
outcomes. Separating them is safe and is reflected above. → GAP-SOURCE-QF-36 (minor).

⚠️ Outcomes 2 and 4–8 are the **same generic set** used on the Level 3 and Level 5
Business Management awards. Only outcomes 1 and 3 carry the Level 7 marker, by inserting
the word "strategic". **Rewrite in plain language and do not reuse wording from the
S.No 25 or S.No 33 pages.**
