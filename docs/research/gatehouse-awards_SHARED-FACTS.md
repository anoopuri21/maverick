## 3. ⚠ DELIVERY MODEL — the single most important fact for this batch

Gatehouse Awards is an **awarding organisation, not a teaching institution**. It does not enrol or
teach learners. Qualifications are delivered by **approved GA centres**, which must pass centre
approval (UK initial approval £645; annual re-approval £475, per G2).

**All four tracker qualifications carry the line: *"This qualification is delivered under a GA
exclusivity agreement."*** The Level 7 Strategic Leadership and Management specification names the
exclusive partner: *"This GA qualification is delivered exclusively in partnership with an approved
GA centre, Global Silver Light."*

**Consequences that MUST shape the content:**
- Never describe Gatehouse as a university, nor imply direct enrolment with Gatehouse.
- Never present the GA fee as a learner tuition fee — see §5.
- An exclusivity agreement means the qualification is not openly available from any approved
  centre. Enquirers must be told to confirm delivery arrangements.
→ **GAP-SOURCE-GA-01**.

## 5. ⚠ FEES — do not misrepresent

The figures in G2 are **centre-facing qualification registration fees**, listed under "UK Individual
Qualification Fees", exclusive of VAT, *"effective from 1st September 2026"* for UK centres. G2
states: *"All prices are exclusive of VAT. Where VAT is applicable, it will be charged at the
prevailing rate. This is currently 20% in the UK."* and *"international centres should contact an
in-country GA Representative (where applicable) or GA directly for information about operating
outside the UK as arrangements and pricing can vary."*

**This is NOT the price a learner pays.** Learner tuition is set by the delivering centre and is
not published by Gatehouse. Content must either omit a price or state clearly that the published
figure is the awarding body's registration fee and that course fees come from the centre.
→ **GAP-SOURCE-GA-02**.

## 6. Assessment and quality assurance (common structure)

Each specification devotes Section 2 to delivery. Headings captured:
Teaching and Learning Requirements · Assessment & Quality Assurance Model · Assessment of Learners
and Portfolio Requirements · **CRAVES Requirements** · Resubmissions · Internal Moderation and
Quality Assurance Arrangements · Grading and Recording Achievement · Unit and Portfolio Sign Off ·
External Moderation and Quality Assurance Arrangements · Registering Candidates and **Unique
Learner Numbers (ULNs)** · ID Requirements · Record Keeping · Results and Certification ·
**Direct Claims Status (DCS)** · Appeals and Enquiries.

Each qualification is *"subject to the GA Centre Assessment and Standards Scrutiny and General
Moderation policy."*

Section 3 covers centre staffing: general staff requirements, requirements for teachers and
assessors, requirements for IQA (Internal Quality Assurers / Internal Moderators), CPD
requirements, teaching/learning/assessment resources, venue and equipment requirements, ongoing
support.
