## 3. Qualification Overview (verbatim)

> "The Qualifications have been created to develop and reward the business managers of
> today and the future, and to continue to bring recognition and professionalism to the
> management sectors. The Level 7 programme will require Learners to critically
> evaluate, challenge and synthesise a wide range of current management tools,
> techniques and models. The emphasis placed on current management concepts and models
> is on those that are relevant to the 21st century business environment and context and
> goes beyond the normal examination of Anglo-American management models."

⚠️ **Sentence 1** is the shared business boilerplate (Levels 3, 5 and 7).
⚠️ **Sentences 2–3 are nearly identical to S.No 48's overview** and will recur on
S.No 51 — this is Qualifi's standard Level 7 management paragraph. **Three or more
pages will share it.** Paraphrase differently on each. → GAP-STYLE-QF-01 and the new
**GAP-STYLE-QF-37** (the Level 7 management paragraph).

⚠️ **No innovation-specific sentence appears anywhere in the overview**, despite
"Innovation" being in the title. The word innovation occurs only in learning outcome 1.
Do not manufacture an innovation rationale Qualifi has not written.
→ **GAP-SOURCE-QF-38.**

## 4. Units (7, all under a "Mandatory" heading)

1. Finance for Managers
2. Strategic Direction
3. Strategic Marketing
4. Human Resource Planning
5. **Marketing Strategy for Social Enterprise**
6. **Global Marketing**
7. Strategic Management

⚠️ **Seven units against 120 credits**, all marked Mandatory, with no optional group
printed and no per-unit credits. → GAP-SOURCE-QF-08.

⚠️ **Observation that matters for honest positioning:** three of the seven units are
marketing (strategic marketing, marketing strategy for social enterprise, global
marketing) and **none is named for innovation**. The unit list is marketing-weighted
rather than innovation-weighted. Say so factually — a reader choosing this for
innovation content deserves to see the actual subject list.

⭐ **Marketing Strategy for Social Enterprise** is distinctive and appears on no other
tracker row. Genuine differentiator worth featuring.

## 5. Learning Outcomes (8, verbatim)

1. **To understand and apply the principles of strategic innovation in a business environment.**
2. Review and apply the principles of business management within industry.
3. To understand and apply the principles of strategic management in a specific environment.
4. To improve the employability of learners by allowing them to explore the relationship between management theories and their practical application in the business world.
5. Analyse problem solving techniques specific to business and industry.
6. Select, collate, review and analyse information form a wide range of sources. *(sic)*
7. Work independently and as part of a team.
8. Manage one's own personal development and growth.

**Outcome 1 is the only differentiator from S.No 49**, where the same slot reads
"strategic leadership". Outcomes 2–8 are word-for-word identical across S.No 49, 50 and
51. **This is a severe duplication hazard** — the `learning` field must be rewritten in
genuinely different plain language on each of the three pages. → GAP-STYLE-QF-37.
