## 3. Overview — headed "Course Overview", not "Qualification Overview"

Verbatim:
> "The Qualification has been designed to meet the needs of senior/middle managers in the
> running of their businesses, and to facilitate their progression to high levels within
> their organisation. Managers need to be aware of issues, able to analyse their potential
> outcomes and consequences, and decide how to react. They do not have to be expert in all
> areas of business, but they need to possess a general awareness of what specialist
> facilities and services exist, and how they can assist in carrying out that role. The
> programme includes contemporary topics reflecting key issues within the 21st – century
> business environment as well as the latest developments in management research."

⚠️ **Not one word of this overview is about human resources.** It is generic
senior/middle manager boilerplate (see the family file §6). Build our overview from the
units and say openly that Qualifi's own description is general rather than HR-specific.
→ GAP-SOURCE-QF-64.

⭐ One idea *is* usable and is a good editorial angle: a manager "does not have to be
expert in all areas of business, but needs a general awareness of what specialist
facilities and services exist". That is a decent plain-language framing of why a general
manager studies HR — but it is shared boilerplate, so express it in a construction used
on no other page.

⚠️ Source prints "21st – century" with a stray spaced en dash; do not reproduce.

## 4. ⭐ Units (6, verbatim) — the only genuinely HR-specific content on the page
1. Contemporary HRM
2. Leading, Managing and HR Development
3. Resourcing, Talent Management and Development
4. Performance Management
5. Employee Relations
6. **Investigating a Business Issue from an HR Perspective**

Six units, 120 credits, no split, no per-unit credits → GAP-SOURCE-QF-08.

⭐ This is a **recognisable, credible HR curriculum**: resourcing and talent, performance,
employee relations, development, plus an applied investigation. Unit 6 is an independent
workplace-focused project and the strongest single differentiator — it is the only
"investigate a real business issue" unit in the Level 7 set that is framed around the
learner's own organisation. Lead with the units; they carry this page.

⚠️ "HRM" and "HR" are used unexpanded in official unit titles — reproduce the titles
exactly, and expand "human resource management" on first use in our own prose.

## 5. Learning Outcomes (8 — generic, shared with S.No 62)
1. To understand and apply the principles of human resource management in a business environment.
2. Review and apply the principles of human resource management within industry.
3. To understand and apply the principles of human resource management in a specific environment.
4. To improve the employability of learners by allowing them to explore the relationship between theories and their practical application in the business world.
5. Analyse problem solving techniques specific to business and industry.
6. Select, collate, review and analyse information from a wide range of sources.
7. Work independently and as part of a team.
8. Manage one's own personal development and growth.

⚠️ Outcomes 7 and 8 are printed on one line (GAP-SOURCE-QF-36); read as eight.
⚠️ Outcome 6 has the "form a wide range of sources" typo; correct silently.
⚠️⚠️ Outcomes 1–3 are **three restatements of the same thing** — "in a business
environment", "within industry", "in a specific environment" — with no explanation of the
difference. Outcomes 4–8 are **identical to S.No 62 and overlap S.No 60**.
→ GAP-SOURCE-QF-65 and GAP-STYLE-QF-62. Differentiate this page on units, never outcomes.
