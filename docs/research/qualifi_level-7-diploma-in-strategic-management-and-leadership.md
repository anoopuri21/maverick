## 5. ⭐ Units — the only award in the cluster with genuine choice

**Mandatory (6):**
1. Manage Team Performance to Support Strategy
2. Information Management and Strategic Decision Taking
3. Leading a Strategic Management Project
4. Strategic Direction
5. Strategic Planning
6. Development as a Strategic Manager

**Optional — choose 2 from 4:**
1. Finance for Managers
2. Strategic Marketing
3. Organisational Change Strategies
4. Human Resource Planning

**Eight units in total.** This is the first Level 7 award found with a published
mandatory/optional split, and it is the cleanest structural differentiator in the
cluster.

Observations worth using:
- The mandatory six are **leadership-and-execution** focused: team performance, decision
  taking, leading a project, direction, planning, and your own development as a manager.
  Compare S.No 50, whose seven mandatory units are marketing-weighted.
- **Development as a Strategic Manager** treats the learner's own growth as an assessed
  unit, which is unusual and genuinely distinctive.
- **Leading a Strategic Management Project** means applied work, not only theory.
- The optional four let you add finance, marketing, change or HR depending on the gap in
  your own role.

No per-unit credits published. → GAP-SOURCE-QF-08.
