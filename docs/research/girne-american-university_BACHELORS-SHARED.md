## Reuse/duplication hazard

The business-faculty degrees reuse BUS101/108, accounting, statistics, economics, business law and the same faculty paragraph on Moodle, attendance and project work. The wording must **not** be reproduced across eight pages. Differentiation must follow each department's own upper-level core: hotel operations and internships (70), export/cultural studies (71), buyer behaviour and communication (73), auditing/taxation (75), economics models (76), systems/database/IT (77). Rows 72 and 74 have no such attributable departmental curriculum: do not derive one from the Faculty of Business or from the separate Accounting/MBA pages. GCC guidance should describe *questions an applicant might ask*, not assert employer demand, online delivery or Maverick services.
