## 4. ⭐⭐ The forbidden-combination rule (unique to this award)

Qualifi publishes an explicit restriction at **both** levels. Verbatim:

> Level 4: "Learners taking this qualification cannot choose the combination of
> electives that lead to a specialise qualifications in Networking, Web design or E
> Commerce. Therefore, the following combinations are not allowed – H/617/6696 and
> K/617/6697, M/617/6698 and T/617/6699, D/617/6700 and H/617/6701 as the 2 electives."
>
> Level 5: "For this qualification, learners cannot choose Y/617/6744 and D/617/6745,
> H/617/6746 and M/617/6748, T/617/6749 and K/617/6750 as their 2 electives."

*(sic: "a specialise qualifications".)*

**Plain meaning:** you may not pick the exact elective pair that would amount to one of
the three specialist awards (Networking, Web Design, E-commerce). If you want that
combination you must enrol on the specialist qualification itself — tracker rows 40, 41
and 42. The generalist award is deliberately kept general.

This is excellent, concrete, reader-useful content and **exists on no other page**. It
is the natural editorial centre of gravity for S.No 39.

⚠️ The unit reference codes are published as bare codes with no unit titles attached.
Qualifi does not say which code is which unit, and the Level 5 list skips a code
(M/617/6748 appears where the sequence would suggest otherwise). **Do not map codes to
unit names and do not publish the raw code strings** — explain the rule in plain
English instead and point to the Centre Specification. → **GAP-SOURCE-QF-21.**
