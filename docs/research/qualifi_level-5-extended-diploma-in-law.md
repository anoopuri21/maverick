## 3. ⭐ KEY FINDING — the SQE sentence, and how to handle it

Qualification Overview, verbatim:

> "The aim of the QUALIFI Level 5 Diploma in Law is to provide learners the opportunity
> to acquire a broad range of knowledge and understanding and develop a range of skills
> essential for a successful career in law. There is also the opportunity to progress to
> a higher-level qualification in law including a bachelor's degree final year. **The
> suite of qualifications in this sector will aim to provide the underpinning knowledge
> of the law contributing to preparation for the Solicitor' Qualifying Examination.**
>
> The aim of the QUALIFI Level 5 Extended Diploma in Law is to allow learner to choose
> their chosen academic path by completing a Level 4 and Level 5 Diploma in Law."

*(sic: "Solicitor' Qualifying Examination"; "allow learner to choose".)*

⚠️ **HANDLE WITH CARE — the most legally sensitive sentence in the Qualifi set.**

What Qualifi actually says is hedged three times over: the suite **"will aim to"**
provide underpinning knowledge **"contributing to preparation for"** the SQE. That is
not a claim of SRA recognition, SQE eligibility, exemption or qualifying status.

**Rules for the page:**
- Do **not** say the qualification prepares you for the SQE, qualifies you to sit it,
  or is recognised by the Solicitors Regulation Authority. Qualifi claims none of these.
- If mentioned at all, attribute precisely: *Qualifi says the law suite aims to provide
  underpinning legal knowledge that contributes to SQE preparation.*
- State plainly that this is **not** a qualifying law degree, confers **no** SRA
  recognition, and does **not** make anyone eligible to practise.
- **Recommendation: state the limitation prominently and keep the SQE reference brief.**
  A reader searching this subject is exactly the person who could be misled.

→ **GAP-SOURCE-QF-20.**

Also note the overview repeatedly says "Level 5 Diploma" while the page is for the
**Extended** Diploma — more evidence of shared Level 4/5 copy.

## 4. Units (8, verbatim)

1. Contract Law
2. Criminal Law
3. Legal Methods
4. Public Law
5. Equity and Trusts
6. EU Law
7. Land Law
8. Law of Tort

Observations:
- These are recognisably the classic foundation subjects of English law — a genuine
  strength and worth saying, as a factual observation about the subject list.
- ⚠️ **Only 8 units against 240 credits**, the same ratio problem as S.No 36
  (GAP-SOURCE-QF-13). The page likely shows one half of the Level 4 + Level 5
  combination. Disclose; do not reconcile.
- **EU Law** on a post-Brexit English law syllabus is notable. Make no claim about why
  it is there; Qualifi gives no explanation.

## 5. Learning Outcomes (8, verbatim — one per unit, in unit order)

1. Understand the principles of formation of legal agreements.
2. Understand aims and general principles of criminal law.
3. Understand the sources of law.
4. Understand the features of the UK constitution.
5. Understand historical events leading to the development and nature of Equity.
6. Understand the rationale for the Europeans Union's creation. *(sic)*
7. Understand the nature of interests in real property.
8. Understand the nature and purpose of liability in tort.

Every outcome begins "Understand" — knowledge-level, not practice-level. That supports
the honest framing: this teaches legal knowledge, it does not train practitioners.

## 6. Entry Requirements (verbatim substance)

- Approved centres decide on the applicant's ability to complete and meet the demands;
  initial assessment considers available learner support.
- Accessible without artificial barriers. **Applicants must be aged 18 or over.**
- IELTS 6 (or equivalent) where English is not the first language.
- International qualifications checked for appropriate enrolment.
- Normally two supporting references, at least one preferably academic.

No prior qualification level and no experience clause. Carries the incongruous
"postgraduate programmes" clause — **do not repeat it.**

## 7. Career outcomes

No destinations, salaries or employment rates. Three published progression routes.
"Employment in an associated profession" is Qualifi's wording — associated, not
regulated legal practice. Reflect that distinction honestly.

## 9. Notes

- The jurisdiction is English (and EU) law. Readers in the Gulf and elsewhere should
  be told the content is not their local law. Qualifi does not say this; it is a plain
  factual observation from the unit list and is important for the reader.
- The only Qualifi Level 5 award whose published progression skips to Level 7.
