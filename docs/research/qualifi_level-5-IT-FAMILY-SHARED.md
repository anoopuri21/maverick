## 1. Why this file exists

All four IT awards at Level 5 publish:

- the **same Qualification Overview, word for word**
- the **same eight Learning Outcomes, word for word**
- the **same Entry Requirements, word for word**
- the **same Centre Specification PDF**
- the **same three progression routes**
- the same credit equivalency (240), accreditation status and availability

Only the **title, the QAN and the unit list** differ.

⚠️ **GAP-STYLE-QF-16 — the single biggest duplication risk in the project.** Four pages
built from one source text will collide on the dedupe gate unless every shared
sentence is rewritten from scratch on each page, with a different angle, a different
sentence shape and different vocabulary. Planned angles are in §7.

## 4. ⚠️ GAP-SOURCE-QF-17 — the 120 / 240 credit contradiction

The shared overview ends:

> "The diploma is accredited at Level 5 and has a total equivalence of **120 credits**."

But the Specifications block on all four pages states **Credit Equivalency: 240**, and
the S.No 40 unit list says explicitly "All 12 units are mandatory to receive **240
credits**."

**Reading:** the 120-credit sentence is left over from the non-extended Level 5 Diploma
and was not updated for the extended version. **240 is almost certainly correct**, but
Qualifi publishes both figures on the same page.

**Editorial rule:** use **240** (the regulated Credit Equivalency field, corroborated by
the S.No 40 unit note), and disclose on every one of the four pages that the overview
text still says 120, directing the reader to the Centre Specification. Do not quietly
drop the contradiction.

## 5. Shared Qualification Overview (verbatim — paraphrase independently four times)

> "The rationale for the qualification is that it provides a career path for learners
> who wish to develop a broad base of knowledge and skills that will enable them to
> work in a variety of roles n the IT industry, notably in .NET programming, system
> administration and network security. Learners will also learn about entrepreneurship
> in a techno environment. The qualification is flexible in that it offers elective
> choices of networking, web design or e-commerce – the latter being much in demand in
> modern business.
>
> The technical skills and knowledge delivered through the successful achievement of
> the qualification are required and recognised internationally. The diploma is
> accredited at Level 5 and has a total equivalence of 120 credits."

*(sic: "roles n the IT industry"; "techno environment".)*

⚠️ Note the overview describes the **elective-choice** model, which only genuinely
applies to S.No 39. On the three specialist awards (40, 41, 42) all twelve units are
mandatory, so the "flexible … elective choices" sentence is **inaccurate for them**.
**Do not repeat that claim on the specialist pages.** → **GAP-SOURCE-QF-18.**

⚠️ "recognised internationally" is an unevidenced marketing claim with no named
recogniser. **Do not reproduce it.**

## 6. Shared Learning Outcomes (8, verbatim) and Entry Requirements

**Outcomes:**
1. To understand and apply the principles of IT in a range of business environments
2. To understand and apply the principles in a specific environment
3. To improve the employability of learners by allowing them to explore the relationship between management theories and their practical application in the IT world
4. Analyse problem solving techniques specific to business and industry
5. Select, collate, review and analyse information form a wide range of sources *(sic)*
6. Effectively use verbal and communication skills
7. Work independently and as part of a team
8. Manage one's own personal development and growth

Outcomes 4–8 are the same generic set used on the Level 3 and Level 5 Business
Management awards (S.No 25, 33). Only outcomes 1–3 are IT-specific. **Rewrite them
into plain language differently on each of the four pages.**

**Entry Requirements (identical on all four):**
- Approved centres decide on the applicant's ability to complete and meet the demands;
  initial assessment considers available learner support.
- Accessible without artificial barriers. **Applicants must be aged 18 or over.**
- IELTS 6 (or equivalent) where English is not the first language.
- International qualifications checked for appropriate enrolment.
- Normally two supporting references, at least one preferably academic.
- Carries the incongruous "UK higher education **postgraduate** programmes" clause.
  **Do not repeat it.**
- **No prior qualification level and no experience clause are stated.**

say "awarded by Qualifi but taught by a centre", "no fee is published", "240 credits",
