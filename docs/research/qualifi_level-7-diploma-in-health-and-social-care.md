## 3. Qualification Overview (verbatim)

> "The aim of this qualification is to provide learners with an understanding of
> management within the health and social care sector. Learners will develop practical
> skills for a management career through a range of specialised units that include how
> to research and implement improvement for their employer and within the sector.
>
> Successful completion of the QUALIFI Level 7 Diploma in Health and Social Care
> provides learners with the opportunity to progress to further study or employment or
> to complete a master's degree at **our chosen University partners**."

⚠️ "Our chosen University partners" — third variant of the unnamed-partner phrasing
(after "one of our University partners" on S.No 35 and "our Partner institution" on
S.No 43). Still no institution named anywhere. → GAP-SOURCE-QF-11 and QF-31.

⭐ **The framing is explicitly managerial**: "management within the health and social
care sector", "practical skills for a **management career**". This is the cleanest
differentiator from the Level 3 and Level 5 awards in the same subject.

## 4. Units (6, verbatim)
1. Health and Social Care Leadership
2. Managing People in Health and Social Care
3. Managing Finance in Health and Social Care
4. Health and Social Care Strategies and Policies
5. Leading Change in Health and Social Care
6. Research Methods for Healthcare Professionals

Every unit is a management function applied to the sector — leadership, people, finance,
strategy and policy, change, research. No clinical content whatsoever. Six units against
120 credits, no split, no per-unit credits → GAP-SOURCE-QF-08.

⭐ **Managing Finance in Health and Social Care** appears at no other level of this
subject in the tracker and is a strong differentiator.

## 5. Learning Outcomes (6, verbatim — one per unit, in unit order)
1. Understand theories of leadership utilised in health and social care settings.
2. Understand performance management in health and social care.
3. Understand the role of financial information in managing health and social care organisations.
4. Understand strategic and business plans for health care organisations based on environmental analysis.
5. Understand strategies for achieving quality in health and social care services.
6. Understand research approaches in health and social care contexts.

Clean, subject-specific and defect-free — no contamination, no duplication, no generic
management filler. Only six, so the eight-item `learning` field must be supplemented
from the overview's stated aims (researching and implementing improvement for an
employer and within the sector), clearly traceable to published text.

## 6. Entry Requirements
- Centre reviews ability to complete and meet the demands; considers available support.
- Accessible without artificial barriers. **Applicants must be aged 19 or over.**
- IELTS 6 (or equivalent) where English is not the first language.
- International qualifications checked (postgraduate clause — correct at this level).
- Normally two supporting references, at least one preferably academic.

⚠️ **No Level 6 qualification or first degree is published**, the same anomaly as
S.No 53. Report as published and tell the reader to confirm with the centre, since most
Level 7 siblings do require one. → GAP-SOURCE-QF-44.
