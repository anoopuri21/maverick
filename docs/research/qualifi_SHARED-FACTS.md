## 3. The awarding organisation

Qualifi Ltd is an **Ofqual-regulated awarding organisation** in England. Its
qualifications sit on the **Regulated Qualifications Framework (RQF)** and are listed on
the Register of Regulated Qualifications. Q1 states, verbatim:

> Regulated qualifications, also known as accredited qualifications, are those that are
> reviewed, recognised and monitored by the UK Regulators to ensure that they meet
> specific criteria and quality standards. The requirements for the regulated
> qualifications to be accredited are set out in the regulatory arrangements of the
> General Conditions of Recognition in England (OfQual).

> Ofqual, the Regulator in England, monitor and review the process of accredited
> qualifications before and after they are listed in the Register of Regulated
> Qualifications. For the qualification to be accredited, Qualifi has to submit their
> proposed qualification programme against the regulatory requirements and, that Qualifi
> maintains the appropriate standards and quality for the design, development and
> delivery of its qualifications.

**Delivery model (same caveat as Gatehouse):** Qualifi awards; approved centres teach.
Qualifi publishes no learner tuition fees anywhere on its site. → **GAP-SOURCE-QF-01**.

## 4. Standard page layout on `qualifi.net` (confirmed on S.No 47)

Every qualification page carries the same structured blocks, which makes extraction
reliable:

| Block | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Specifications** | Qualification title · Qualification type · Level · Accreditation status · **Credit Equivalency** · **Qualification number (RQF)** · **Progression routes** · **Availability** · link to the **Centre Specification PDF** · link to the CASS Strategy |
| **Qualification Overview** | 2–4 paragraphs of official prose |
| **Units** | Mandatory units, plus specialism/optional routes where they exist |
| **Learning Outcomes** | A numbered list |
| **Entry Requirements** | Bulleted criteria plus the experience clause |

A typical page fits in a single fetch chunk.

### Recurring Qualifi language (verified on S.No 47)

- Qualification type for Level 7: *"Vocational Related Qualification (Higher Education)"*.
- Accreditation status: *"Accredited"*.
- Availability: *"UK and international"*.
- Entry philosophy, verbatim: *"The qualification has been designed to be accessible
  without artificial barriers that restrict access and progression. Entry to the
  qualification will be through centre interview…"*
- Experience clause, verbatim: *"In certain circumstances, managers with considerable
  experience but no formal qualifications may be considered, subject to interview and
  being able to demonstrate their ability to cope with the demands of the programme."*

⚠ Because this wording repeats across dozens of Qualifi pages, it **must be paraphrased
differently on every page we publish**, or the cross-page dedupe gate will fail. Do not
lift these sentences verbatim into content. → **GAP-STYLE-QF-01**.

## 5. ⚠ The master's-degree progression claim — handle carefully

S.No 47's page states progression to *"a university partner to complete a dissertation
to then receive a full master's degree"*, and the overview repeats: *"Completing the
Diploma allows access to a dissertation at one of our University partners for a related
master's degree."*

Qualifi does **not name the university partners** on the qualification page. Content may
report that Qualifi states such a route exists, but must not promise a specific
university, a guaranteed top-up, or a named degree. Readers should be told to confirm the
current partner list and entry terms directly. → **GAP-SOURCE-QF-02**.
