## 3. ⭐ Units (12, verbatim) — all mandatory

Qualifi's own framing sentence:

> "All 12 units are mandatory to receive 240 credits."

**This single sentence is valuable twice over:** it confirms the fixed-path model, and
it is the only place in the entire IT family where Qualifi states the 240-credit figure
in its own prose — which is what settles the 120/240 contradiction recorded as
GAP-SOURCE-QF-17 in the shared file.

1. Information Technology and IT Ethics
2. Mathematics and Statistics for IT
3. PC Maintenance and Operating Systems
4. Computer Graphics Editing and Database Concepts
5. Logical IT Networking
6. Physical IT Networking
7. Technopreneurship
8. Network Security
9. C#.NET Programming
10. System Administration
11. Network Routing and Switching
12. Network Design and Administration

**Structure, derived by comparison with S.No 39 (safe to state):** units 1–4 are the
Level 4 core, units 7–10 the Level 5 core — identical across all four IT awards. The
networking identity comes from units 5, 6, 11 and 12, which are exactly the four
networking electives of the generalist award, here made compulsory.

**Networking progression within the award:** Logical and Physical networking at the
lower block, then Routing and Switching and Design and Administration at the upper —
a coherent beginner-to-designer arc. Worth describing for the reader.

## 4. Notes

- This is the award S.No 39 explicitly forbids you from assembling out of electives
  (see GAP-SOURCE-QF-21). If networking is the goal, this is the route Qualifi intends.
- Despite the specialism, the programme keeps Technopreneurship, C#.NET Programming
  and IT Ethics — so it is not purely infrastructure. Say so; a reader expecting a
  pure networking course should know four units are not about networking at all.
- Of the four IT awards, this has the clearest single job-family identity.
