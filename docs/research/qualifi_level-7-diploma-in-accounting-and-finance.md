## 3. Qualification Overview (verbatim — the shortest of the three family pages)

> "The Qualifi Diploma in Accounting and Finance is designed to meet the needs of
> senior/middle managers **and accountants** in the running of their businesses, and to
> facilitate their progression to higher levels within their organisation. It focuses on
> developing understanding, skills and abilities to equip the Learner with the awareness
> and aptitudes to be an effective **accounting and finance manager**."

⚠️ The first sentence is the family boilerplate (see family file §6); only "and
accountants" has been added. The second sentence is the one genuinely subject-specific
line on the page: the target is an **accounting and finance manager**, not a trainee
accountant. That distinction is the editorial spine here and the honest framing of what
the award is for.

⚠️ The overview omits the qualification's level in its own title ("The Qualifi Diploma in
Accounting and Finance"). Always use the full official title in our content.

## 4. ⭐ Units (6, verbatim) — strongly senior and strategic
1. Strategy and Global Finance
2. Strategic Financial Management
3. Strategic Auditing
4. Ethical Behaviour and Corporate Governance
5. Corporate Reporting
6. Financial Analyst

Six units, 120 credits, no split, no per-unit credits → GAP-SOURCE-QF-08.

⭐ Three of six units are explicitly strategic, and **Corporate Reporting**,
**Strategic Auditing** and **Ethical Behaviour and Corporate Governance** are serious,
recognisable senior finance subjects. This unit list is markedly stronger and more
coherent than the page's outcomes.

⚠️ Unit 6, "Financial Analyst", is named for a **job title rather than a subject** —
unique in the tracker. Report the title exactly as published; do not translate it into
"financial analysis" and do not infer its content. → GAP-SOURCE-QF-67.

7. Work independently and as part of a team.
