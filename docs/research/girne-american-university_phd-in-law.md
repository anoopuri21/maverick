## 5. Decision

**Do not draft a programme page for S.No 19.**

Publishing a page for a doctorate the awarding university has itself marked as not yet launched
would mislead prospective applicants and would require fabricating every substantive field. It
fails the Phase-8 official-source and no-invention criteria, and it carries a consumer-protection
risk: a visitor could enquire about, or attempt to apply to, a programme that does not currently
admit students.

Tracker action: set `Research Status` to
`Incomplete – programme annotated "(To be launched)" on the official GAU site; not yet running`.
