## 3. About Program (M1, verbatim)

> PhD in Marketing students work alongside Girne American University's Business & Economics
> Faculty. PhD candidates aim to address problems that organizations face in seeking to provide
> products and services that meet the marketplace and customer demands. The program is composed of
> seminars and applied as well as theoretical research with faculty members.

Note the distinctive detail: this is the only GAU doctorate dossier in which the programme page
names a **teaching faculty partner — the Business & Economics Faculty** — alongside the Institute.

## 5. Program Requirements (M2, verbatim)

> The program requires completion of 7 courses (21 credits in total), plus a Seminar (non-credit)
> and a Thesis (non-credit). After a success completion of the courses, students are asked to take
> qualification exam. The success pass of the Qualification Exam will allow students to start
> working on their Thesis. **The minimum duration of the thesis is 2 semesters.**

Both M1 and M2 agree on 2 semesters for this programme — no internal conflict here (unlike
Business Management).

## 10. Unique selling points supportable from official sources

- Explicit ECBE citation alongside YÖK and YÖDAK on the official graduate-school page.
- Taught in partnership with GAU's Business & Economics Faculty, as stated on the programme page.
- Shortest published minimum thesis duration among the GAU rows examined — 2 semesters.
- Research agenda framed around real organisational problems in meeting marketplace and customer
  demand, rather than purely theoretical study.
- Dedicated marketing core: consumer behaviour theory, integrated marketing communications and a
  current-issues course, plus a marketing-specific seminar.
- Six named electives spanning reporting, organisational behaviour and four finance subjects.
