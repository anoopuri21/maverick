## 3. Qualification Overview (verbatim)

> "The Qualifications have been created to develop and reward the business managers
> of today and the future, and to continue to bring recognition and professionalism
> to the management sectors. We hope that centres and learners take the opportunity
> to learn a great deal from these programmes that will provide relevant new skills
> and qualities. Completing the Extended Diploma has a value of 240 credits."

Note: written in the plural ("The Qualifications") because the text is shared across
Qualifi's business diploma family. Same boilerplate family as S.No 25. **Must be

## 4. Units (12, verbatim, no mandatory/optional split published)

1. Communications in Organisations
2. Leadership and the Organisation
3. Financial Awareness
4. Managing Change
5. Business Operations
6. Developing Teams
7. Responding to the Changing Business Environment
8. Effective Decision Making
9. Business Development
10. Business Models and Growing Organisations
11. Customer Management
12. Risk Management and Organisations

The page gives no credit value per unit and does not say whether any are optional.

## 5. Learning Outcomes (9, verbatim)

1. To understand and apply the principles of leadership in a business environment
2. Review and apply the principles of business management within industry
3. To understand and apply the principles of management in a specific environment
4. To improve the employability of learners by allowing them to explore the relationship between management theories and their practical application in the business world.
5. Analyse problem solving techniques specific to business and industry
6. Select, collate, review and analyse information form a wide range of sources *(sic — "form" for "from" in the original)*
7. Effectively use verbal and communication skills
8. Work independently and as part of a team
9. Manage one's own personal development and growth

Outcomes 2–9 are near-identical to the Level 3 Business Management award (S.No 25).
The differentiator at Level 5 is outcome 1 (leadership) and the 240-credit size.

## 6. Entry Requirements (verbatim, three bullets)

- Learners who possess Qualifications at Level 3 and/or 4;
- Learners who have work experience at a managerial level and demonstrate ambition with clear career goals;
- Learners who possess a first degree in another discipline and want to develop their careers in business.

**No age minimum and no IELTS requirement are published on this page** — unlike the
Health and Social Care, Occupational Health and Safety and Accounting awards at the
same level, which all state 18+ and IELTS 6. Do not import those rules here.

## 9. Notes

- The progression route is unusually strong: **final year** of a UK honours degree,
  not year one. This is the top-up position and is the single most marketable fact
  about the qualification. It is a published route, **not a guaranteed place** — the
  receiving university decides. Same honesty treatment as S.No 27 (GAP-SOURCE-QF-02).
- 240 credits is double the Level 3 Integrated Diploma (S.No 27) and four times the
  60-credit Level 3 awards.
- Relationship to S.No 25: same subject, two levels up, four times the size, and a
  degree-final-year rather than degree-year-one exit.
