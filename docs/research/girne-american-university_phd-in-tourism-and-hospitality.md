## 9. Unique selling points supportable from official sources

- Framed by GAU itself as a supervised research degree whose thesis must make an original
  contribution to the field.
- Revenue-side specialism rarely named at doctoral level: THM603 Yield Management for Tourism
  Industry.
- Sector-specific strategy course — Competitive Strategy for the Hospitality Industry — rather than
  a generic strategy module.
- People-side depth through Leadership in Hotel Management and Human Resources.
- Service Operations Management covering the delivery side of hospitality businesses.
- Research methods and advanced management taught up front, in Year 1 Fall, before the specialist
  hospitality courses.
- A dedicated tourism and hospitality seminar carrying 20 ECTS.
