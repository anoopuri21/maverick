## 3. Qualification Overview

Verbatim in substance:
- Learners evaluate a wide range of **process safety management standards, risk-based
  process safety techniques, and human factors and process safety culture**.
- By analysing and critiquing process safety standards, learners examine how different
  **functions and disciplines** affect an organisation's process safety strategy, its
  success, and the way it operates.
- The qualification requires a range of skills **required of executive level
  management**, including problem solving, research and analytical skills.
- It enables learners to deal with the **complexities of leadership and strategic
  management in a business context** and to develop their ability to **lead change**.

⚠️ Source uses "analyzing" — American. Body prose, so write British; do not quote.

⚠️ Tension worth noting: the award is 60 credits and four units, yet claims skills
"required of executive level management" and the ability to lead organisational change.
Report what is published without amplifying it.

⭐ The distinctive idea here is **process** safety — plant, equipment and systems — as
opposed to **occupational** safety, which is about people at work. That distinction is
the editorial spine of this page and the cleanest separation from S.No 57 and 58. It is
implicit in Qualifi's text (standards, risk-based techniques, incidents, HAZOP,
inherently safer design) and may be explained in plain language as our own framing.

## 5. Learning Outcomes (8, verbatim)
1. Apply and make critiques of process safety implementation standards.
2. Evaluate the role of strategic leadership in risk-based process safety.
3. **Review the impact of Covid in an organisation's process safety culture.**
4. Understand and apply the principles of **Inherently Safer Designs** in process safety.
5. **Analyse the anatomy of process safety incidents by applying HAZOP techniques.**
6. Manage strategic risks in the organisation process safety domain.
7. **Analyse the impact of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning in process safety management.**
8. Assess process safety culture and competency development in process safety management.

⭐ Outcomes 4, 5 and 7 name real technical content — **inherently safer design**, **HAZOP
(hazard and operability study)**, and **AI and machine learning**. Expand both acronyms
for a lay reader and flag the expansion as ours. Only S.No 58 also mentions AI.

⚠️ **Outcome 3 names Covid.** The specification dates from June 2021 and the outcome has
not been refreshed. Report it as published, note the specification's date as context,
and do not present it as current emphasis. → **GAP-SOURCE-QF-56.**

⚠️ Outcomes 1, 2, 6 and 7 are near-parallel to S.No 58's outcomes 1, 2, 6 and 7, with
"occupational health and safety" swapped for "process safety". **Never describe the two
pages' outcomes in similar prose** — a guaranteed dedupe collision. Differentiate on
HAZOP, inherently safer design and the process/occupational distinction.

## 6. Entry Requirements (published as four bullets and nothing else)
- **Bachelor's in engineering degree**; or
- **Master's in engineering degree**; or
- **Degree or diploma in chemical, instrumentation, mechanical or a relevant process
  safety discipline**;
- A minimum of **3 years' work experience** which demonstrates current and relevant
  industry knowledge.

⭐ The **most specialised entry requirement in the entire tracker** — named engineering
disciplines. This award clearly targets process industries (chemical, oil and gas,
pharmaceutical, manufacturing), though Qualifi does not name any sector; do not name
sectors as fact, only as the plain reading of the disciplines listed.

⚠️ As with S.No 58: **no age minimum, no IELTS requirement, no references and no
centre-review paragraph published.** → GAP-SOURCE-QF-54.

⚠️ The experience bullet is punctuated as a continuation rather than an alternative, so
it is unclear whether three years' experience is an additional requirement on top of a
qualification or a fourth standalone route. Disclose the ambiguity; tell readers to
confirm with the centre. → **GAP-SOURCE-QF-57.**
