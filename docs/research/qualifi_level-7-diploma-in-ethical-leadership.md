## 3. Qualification Overview — substance, with defects flagged

Qualifi's framing, closely followed:

- Created to develop **CPD (continuing professional development) in ethical leadership**,
  for people who want to draw on personal experience and develop their leadership
  understanding and skills professionally, academically and practically.
- The focus is exploration and **practical research into ethical leadership as a
  subject**, in order to apply that knowledge in a new environment.
- At Level 7 learners must **critically evaluate, challenge and synthesise** a wide range
  of current leadership roles, tools, techniques and models.
- The emphasis is on current leadership concepts and on examining ethical requirements
  relevant to the 21st-century business environment, explicitly going **"beyond the
  normal examination of Anglo-American management models"**.
- Designed to develop individual knowledge, skills and strategies in ethical leadership
  so the principles can be applied in a new environment affecting corporate performance.
- Requires building knowledge and understanding in strategic direction and ethical
  leadership.

⭐ **The strongest single idea on the page** is going beyond Anglo-American management
models. That is a genuine, quotable differentiator and particularly relevant to a
non-UK readership. Attribute it to Qualifi.

⚠️ **Typographical defects in the source:** "the impact of **thical ethical reqirements**"
and "apply the **principals** learned". Obvious errors. Do not reproduce; do not quote
these sentences verbatim. → GAP-SOURCE-QF-34.

⚠️ **CPD framing.** Qualifi repeatedly describes this as a CPD qualification. At 60
credits that is consistent — this is a focused professional-development award, not a
full postgraduate programme. Be honest about the size rather than presenting it as
equivalent to the 120-credit Level 7 diplomas.

## 4. Units (3, verbatim)

1. Contemporary Issues Underpinning Leadership in CPD
2. Developing Research and CPD
3. Effective Communication through CPD

Only three units, consistent with 60 credits. Every title carries "CPD", reinforcing the
continuing-professional-development framing. No mandatory/optional split, no per-unit
credits.

## 5. Learning Outcomes (10, verbatim substance)

1. Critically reflect on issues at the forefront of leadership.
2. Understand reflective practice and its implications for personal and professional development.
3. Critical reflection on strategies for effective leadership in a broad range of contexts.
4. Conduct primary research in the field of leadership.
5. Communicate ideas clearly and effectively, and reflect on the importance of communicating in a style appropriate to a variety of audiences and formats.
6. Review, critically evaluate and integrate information from a variety of theories, models and methods in formal research in a practical manner.
7. Apply analytical thinking and problem-solving skills to theoretical and practical scenarios.
8. Communicate effectively in oral and written format.
9. **Evaluate and review how their own skills and work can contribute to leadership in sport and in business.**
10. Identify and apply ideas and concepts from one domain to another.

⚠️ **Outcome 9 refers to "leadership in sport"** on a general ethical leadership award.
Nothing else on the page mentions sport. This is almost certainly contamination from a
sports-leadership qualification's outcome set. **Do not reproduce the word "sport"**, and
do not infer that the award has a sports pathway. → **GAP-SOURCE-QF-35.**

⚠️ Outcomes 5 and 8 both cover communication, and 1 and 3 both cover critical reflection.
Consolidate when writing the `learning` field; do not pad with near-duplicates.

⭐ **Outcome 4, "conduct primary research in the field of leadership", is substantive** —
this award expects original research, not just reading. Worth featuring.

## 9. Notes

- At 60 credits with three units, this is the most compact Level 7 award found. Position
  it honestly as focused CPD for people already in leadership roles, not as a broad
  postgraduate programme.
- "Ethical leadership" is distinct from the strategic-management cluster (S.No 47, 49,
  50, 51) — the subject is conduct and values, not strategy. Differentiation is easy.
- The primary-research requirement makes it heavier than its credit size suggests.
