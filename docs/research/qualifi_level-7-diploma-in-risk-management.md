## 3. Qualification Overview — unusually specific, and quotable

Bespoke copy. Named content, verbatim in substance:
- ISO standard **"harmonisation" programmes**
- Organisational strategy and risk plans
- Contemporary issues in **CSR risk management systems**
- **Risk financing options** available to an organisation
- Evaluating tools to assess an organisation's position in the market, and preparing a
  risk management plan to mitigate potential risks
- ⭐ **Mathematical models named outright: VaR, CVaR and EVaR**
- Developing and designing a **research proposal** on a chosen risk management topic

⭐ Naming VaR, CVaR and EVaR makes this the second Qualifi page (after S.No 66, which
names R, Python and SQL) to specify actual technical content. Highly concrete — use it,
and expand the acronyms for a lay reader as value at risk, conditional value at risk and
entropic value at risk, flagged as our expansion of Qualifi's abbreviations.

⚠️ US spellings in the source ("organizations", "program"). Write British English in our
prose; do not quote these sentences verbatim.

## 5. Learning Outcomes (6) — ⚠️ contains contamination
1. **"Understand the principles and formation of agreements."**
2. Understand aims and general principles of risk management.
3. Understand the sources of risk management.
4. Understand what strategy risk is and the different types of strategy risk.
5. Understand the significance of corporate governance in organisations.
6. Understand how to construct research methods in the context of risk management.

⚠️ **Outcome 1 is contract law language** — "the principles and formation of agreements"
is almost word for word outcome 1 of the Level 5 Law award (S.No 38, "the principles of
formation of legal agreements"). Nothing in the units or overview concerns contract
formation. This is contamination from another qualification's outcome set.
**Omit it; infer no contract law content.** → **GAP-SOURCE-QF-43.**

⚠️ Outcome 3, "the sources of risk management", also mirrors the Law award's "sources of
law" and reads oddly. Report with care; the natural meaning is sources *of risk*.

Only five usable outcomes remain, against a required eight for the `learning` field.
Supplement from the overview's named content (ISO harmonisation, CSR, risk financing,
VaR/CVaR/EVaR, risk management plans), clearly framed as subject coverage.
