## 3. ⭐ Qualification Overview — and the "advanced standing" phrase

Verbatim in substance:
- The aim is to give learners skills and understanding in **marketing and branding that
  align with good strategic decision making** to maintain an organisation's competitive
  advantage.
- Knowledge is acquired through an **integrated approach of theory** in marketing,
  branding, consumer behaviour and digital communication management, **and practice
  using real-time activities**.
- Successful completion develops strategic marketing management, consumer behaviour and
  branding skills, and the ability to focus on implementing an organisation's strategy.
- "The qualification enables learners to progress into or within employment and/or to
  work toward a relevant master's programme **with advanced standing**."

⚠️ **"With advanced standing"** is a stronger formulation than the usual
dissertation-only phrasing and could easily be read as a promise of credit transfer.
Qualifi names no university, publishes no credit-transfer arrangement and gives no
terms. Report the phrase as Qualifi's own words, then state plainly that advanced
standing is granted by the receiving university, not by the awarding body.
→ `GAP-SOURCE-QF-31`, with this specific wording flagged.

⭐ "Practice using real-time activities" indicates applied work. Useful, though Qualifi
does not explain what the activities are — do not elaborate.

## 4. Units (6, verbatim)
1. Contemporary Issues and Principles of Marketing
2. Consumer Behaviour and Market Communications
3. Digital and Social Media Marketing
4. Contemporary Challenges and Strategic Marketing
5. Strategic Brand Management
6. Marketing Research Project

Six units, 120 credits, no split and no per-unit credits → GAP-SOURCE-QF-08.

Strong, coherent list. **Digital and Social Media Marketing** and **Strategic Brand
Management** are the clearest differentiators, and the **Marketing Research Project**
means applied independent work.

⚠️ Units 1 and 4 both begin "Contemporary", and the distinction between "contemporary
issues and principles" and "contemporary challenges and strategic marketing" is not
explained. Note it; do not resolve it.

## 5. Learning Outcomes (9, published as bullets rather than numbered)
1. Gain a sound and coherent foundation in strategic marketing, sufficient to define and execute practical strategic marketing interventions in professional practice.
2. Apply analytical ability and problem-solving skills in marketing management; synthesise and critically evaluate theoretical frameworks of strategic marketing, consumer behaviour, brand management, digital marketing, market research, and design and innovation as applied to marketing.
3. Demonstrate understanding of the practical value of these frameworks across a range of strategic business situations.
4. Create frameworks for understanding and analysing strategic marketing processes in diverse organisations facing different and changing marketing environments.
5. Develop practical research and analytical capabilities in market-driven strategy, particularly in formulating value propositions in provider/customer relationships.
6. Review, modify, synthesise and apply transferable strategic marketing, branding, consumer behaviour and digital marketing concepts in managing product and market development.
7. Critically assess market-sourced data and information from a variety of organisations, and use it for strategic marketing and decision making.
8. Evaluate creative approaches to product and service design, creativity and innovation that may contribute to organisational wealth and solve organisational problems.
9. Demonstrate personal development and learning skills applicable to marketing practice.

⭐ **These are bespoke, subject-specific and substantial** — not the generic eight-item
management set recycled across S.No 49, 50 and 51. Much easier to write from, and a
genuine quality signal for this award. Outcome 2 is overloaded and may be split when
writing the `learning` field.

## 6. Entry Requirements
- Centre reviews ability to complete and meet the demands; considers available support.
- Accessible without artificial barriers. **Applicants must be aged 21 or over** —
  ⚠️ the highest age minimum found anywhere in the Qualifi set (others use 16, 18 or 19).
- Expected to hold a **Level 6 qualification or a first degree**.
- IELTS 6 (or equivalent) where English is not the first language.
- International qualifications checked (postgraduate clause — correct at this level).
- Normally two supporting references, at least one preferably academic.

The age 21 minimum is a real, publishable differentiator. Report it prominently.
