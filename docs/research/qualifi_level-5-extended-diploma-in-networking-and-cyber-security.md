## 3. ⭐ KEY FINDING — Qualifi publishes the award's provenance

Qualification Overview, verbatim in substance:

> "The aim of the QUALIFI Level 5 Extended Diploma in Networking and Cyber Security is
> to provide learners with an extended range of understanding of networking and
> cybersecurity.
>
> The units for this qualification have been taken from existing Qualifi
> qualifications:
> - Qualifi Level 4 Diploma in Cyber Security (603/3331/5)
> - Qualifi Level 4 Diploma in IT – Networking (603/4782/X)
> - Qualifi Level 5 Diploma in Cyber Security (603/4139/7)
> - Qualifi Level 5 Diploma in IT – Networking (603/4792/2)
>
> It is envisaged that learners who successfully complete this qualification will have
> gained a broader understanding of cyber security and how to implement networking
> systems to remove any such risks.
>
> Successful completion … provides learners with the opportunity to progress to further
> study or employment."

**This is the only Qualifi page that states, with QANs, exactly which existing
qualifications its units were drawn from.** It is unusually transparent and explains
the award's identity precisely: a deliberate merge of the networking and cyber security
routes at Levels 4 and 5.

⚠️ Those four source QANs belong to **other qualifications not in our tracker.** Record
them as provenance only. Do **not** create pages for them (Phase 8 rule: nothing
outside the tracker list).

⚠️ "remove any such risks" overstates what any qualification can do. Security work
reduces and manages risk; it does not remove it. **Do not reproduce that phrasing.**
→ **GAP-SOURCE-QF-27.**

## 4. Units (11, verbatim — no mandatory/optional split, no headings)

1. Cyber Security Threat and Risk
2. Network Security and Data Communications
3. Database Security and Computer Programming
4. Incident Response, Investigations and Forensics
5. Security Strategy: Laws, Policies and Implementation
6. Physical IT Networking
7. Cryptography
8. Digital Investigations and Forensics
9. System Administration
10. Network Routing and Switching
11. Network Design and Administration

**Differentiation against the two neighbouring awards — the key editorial material:**

| Compared with | Shares | Differs by |
|---|---|---|
| **S.No 43** Cyber Security | Units 1–5, 7, 8 (threat and risk, network security, database security, incident response, security strategy, cryptography, digital forensics) | S.No 43 adds *Cyber Wars*, *Cyber Security Threats and Risk: Banking and Finance*, *Communications and Incident Management* and *Strategic Leadership* — the **management and leadership** half. This award drops all four and adds four **networking** units instead (6, 9, 10, 11). |
| **S.No 40** IT-Networking | Units 6, 9, 10, 11 | S.No 40 adds the general IT core (IT ethics, maths and statistics, PC maintenance, graphics and databases, technopreneurship, C#.NET) and *Logical IT Networking*. This award drops the general IT core in favour of seven security units. |

**Plain summary for the reader:** S.No 43 is security with management; S.No 40 is
networking with general IT; S.No 44 is the hands-on overlap of security and networking,
with neither the leadership content nor the general IT grounding.

⚠️ **Eleven units against 240 credits** — same ratio gap as S.No 43.

## 6. Entry Requirements (verbatim substance)

- Approved centres decide on the applicant's ability to complete and meet the demands;
  initial assessment considers available learner support.
- Accessible without artificial barriers. **Applicants must be aged 18 or over.**
- IELTS 6 (or equivalent) where English is not the first language.
- International qualifications checked for appropriate enrolment.
- Normally two supporting references, at least one preferably academic.

**No prior qualification level and no experience clause** — notably different from
S.No 43, which asks for Level 3 qualifications, technical/risk experience or a Level 4
in another discipline, and imposes a Level 4-before-Level 5 sequencing rule. On paper
this award is the **easier of the two to enter**. Worth stating honestly.

Carries the incongruous "postgraduate programmes" clause — **do not repeat it.**
