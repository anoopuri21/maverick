- Year 1: `Tourism & Hospitality Maths`, `English for Tourism 1`, `Introduction to Hospitality`, `The Business of Tourism`, `HACCP&TQM Applications in Tourism`.
- Year 2: `Front Office Management`, `Travel Agency Operations`, `Housekeeping Operations`, `Front Office Reservation Application`, `Food & Beverage Service`, `Tourism and Hospitality Accounting`, `Nutrition & Sanitation`, `Tourism Economics`; `THM001 Internship` printed with 0 in right-hand column.
- Year 3: `Tourism Marketing & Sales`, `Human Resources in Hospitality`, `Hospitality Finance`, `Hospitality Law`, `Marketing Research in Hospitality`, `International Hotel Management`; `THM002 Internship` with 0 in right-hand column.
- Year 4: `Tourism Policy Planning`, `Revenue Management`, `Hotel Operations Management`, `International Catering Management`, `Purchasing & Cost Control Analysis`, `International Resort Management`, `Graduation Seminar`.
- `THM338 Culinary Practices 2` / its credit cells and THM427/428 electives are malformed in the web extraction. Do not transcribe their tabular values without checking the original timetable.

## Admissions, mode, cost, USPs — search results and gaps

- **Entry:** no subject-specific academic threshold in T1. G4 says documents vary by degree; G5 gives general English-medium exemption conditions. No claim that IELTS alone secures admission.
- **Mode:** hotel training and two internship entries are published; no distance/online or London delivery claim. Ask GAU where attendance and training occur.
- **Cost:** T1 has no programme-specific sum. G6 has a 2025–2026 Faculty of Tourism and Hospitality annual tariff plus additions; do not put a numeric amount into public copy or imply it holds for 2026–2027.
- **USP:** training *opportunity* at a named university training hotel; a syllabus spanning hotel operations, food service, revenue and tourism policy. These are source statements, not promises of placement.
