## 3. Qualification Overview (verbatim)

> "The Level 5 qualification a combination of the Level 4 Diploma in Education and
> Training and Level 5 units that give learners the opportunity to progress in specific
> subject areas. Learners will develop and enhance knowledge and skills in the areas of
> learning and assessment, behaviour management, learning and communication, concepts
> of lifelong learning, resources planning and management, leadership and continued
> professional development.
>
> **Learners who do not complete the Extended Level 5 Diploma may be awarded the Level 4
> qualification if they complete all Level 4 units.**"

*(sic — "The Level 5 qualification a combination" is missing a verb.)*

⭐ **The fallback award is published here too** — the second Qualifi page to offer one,
after Psychology (S.No 35). Phrased as "may be awarded" rather than "will be", so
report it as a possibility, not an entitlement. Genuinely reassuring; feature it with
that precise hedge intact.

## 4. Units (12, verbatim)

1. Fundamentals of Education and Training
2. Design of Education and Training Resources
3. Engaging Learners
4. Developing a Work Plan
5. Programme Evaluation in Education and Training
6. Professional Development and Reflective Practice
7. Applied Theories and Principles of Education and Training
8. Lifelong Learning
9. Resource Management in Education
10. Developing and Evaluating Operational Work Plans
11. Principles of Educational Leadership and Management
12. Reflective Practice and Professional Development

**Clear 6 + 6 structure**, consistent with the overview's "Level 4 Diploma plus Level 5
units": units 1–6 are the Level 4 half, units 7–12 the Level 5 half. The pairing is
visible — unit 4 *Developing a Work Plan* maps to unit 10 *Developing and Evaluating
Operational Work Plans*, and unit 6 *Professional Development and Reflective Practice*
maps to unit 12 *Reflective Practice and Professional Development* (the same two words
reversed). This is the clearest two-block structure of any Qualifi page seen.

⚠️ The 6+6 reading is a **well-supported inference from the overview**, not an explicit
statement. Present it as how the award appears to be structured, with the Centre
Specification as the authority. → GAP-SOURCE-QF-28.

⚠️ Units 6 and 12 are near-identical in title. Note it rather than implying they are
different subjects.

## 5. Learning Outcomes — published as three prose areas, not a numbered list

Qualifi's framing sentence:

> "The overall learning outcomes for the qualification covers the knowledge and skills
> required by teachers/trainers in the further education and training skills sector:"

Then three areas, verbatim in substance:

1. **Theories, principles and models** in education and training — learning,
   communication, assessment, curriculum development and evaluation, and how to apply
   them to one's own practice.
2. **Teaching, learning and assessment** — roles, responsibilities and relationships;
   using initial and diagnostic assessment to agree individual learning goals with
   learners; planning and delivering inclusive teaching; creating and maintaining a
   safe, inclusive teaching and learning environment; and assessing learning.
3. **Professionalism and context** — professional values in education and training, the
   policy context, the impact of accountability to stakeholders and external bodies,
   the organisational context, and contributing to an organisation's quality
   improvement and quality assurance arrangements.

⚠️ **Format divergence:** three dense prose bullets, not the usual 8–12 numbered
outcomes. The `learning` field needs 8 items. Each of the three areas contains several
distinct capabilities and may be **split into separate plain-language items without
adding anything** — every item must be traceable to text above. Do not invent a ninth.
→ GAP-SOURCE-QF-29.

⭐ **Most valuable phrase on the page:** "the knowledge and skills required by
teachers/trainers in the **further education and training skills sector**". This is
Qualifi naming the intended audience precisely — FE and training, not schools.

## 6. Entry Requirements (verbatim substance)

- Approved centres decide on the applicant's ability to complete and meet the demands;
  initial assessment considers available learner support.
- Accessible without artificial barriers. **Applicants must be aged 18 or over.**
- "It is envisaged that learners entering the Level 4 Diploma will have a Level 3
  qualification. Learners entering the Level 5 Diploma will have a **Level 4
  qualification**."
- IELTS 6 (or equivalent) where English is not the first language.
- International qualifications checked for appropriate enrolment.
- Normally two supporting references, at least one preferably academic.

Identical in structure to S.No 34. "Envisaged" is softer than "required" — preserve
that distinction. Carries the incongruous "postgraduate programmes" clause —
**do not repeat it.**

## 7. Career outcomes

No destinations, salary data or employment rates. Three published progression routes.

⚠️ **MANDATORY DISCLAIMER.** Qualifi publishes **nothing** about Qualified Teacher
Status. This is **not** a QTS route, not initial teacher training, and does not confer
any licence or registration to teach in schools in England or anywhere else. Given that
the audience is precisely people who want to teach, the page must state this plainly
and prominently rather than let the title imply otherwise. The award is aimed at the FE
and training skills sector, which is Qualifi's own stated framing.
→ **GAP-SOURCE-QF-30.**

## 9. Notes

- The audience is trainers and FE teaching staff — corporate trainers, workplace
  assessors, college tutors — not schoolteachers. Stating that clearly is the single
  most useful thing the page can do for a reader.
- Behaviour management appears in the overview but has no matching unit title; it is
  presumably inside *Engaging Learners*. Do not assert that mapping.
- Two awards in the Level 5 group now publish a fallback (this and S.No 35). Worth
  tracking whether this recurs at Level 7.
