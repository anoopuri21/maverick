## 4. Notes

- **Interesting composition.** The B2B/B2C pair is only two of twelve units. The rest is
  general IT plus interface and mobile programming. An honest page should say that this
  is an IT qualification with an e-commerce specialism at the end, not a digital
  marketing or online retail course.
- **Android programming inside an e-commerce award** is unusual and genuinely
  defensible given mobile commerce. Qualifi offers no rationale, so present it as a
  factual observation about the unit list rather than as Qualifi's reasoning.
- Splitting B2B and B2C into separate units is a real distinction, since the two
  operate on very different sales cycles. Good, concrete material for the page.
- The shared overview's remark that e-commerce is "much in demand in modern business"
  is Qualifi's only subject-specific sentence in that boilerplate. It may be
  paraphrased, but not as evidence of demand — no data supports it.
