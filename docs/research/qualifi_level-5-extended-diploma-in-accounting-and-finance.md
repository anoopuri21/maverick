## 3. ⭐ KEY FINDING — Qualifi's own caveat on degree progression

The progression route here reads, verbatim:

> "Final year of a HE qualification/degree in related area **(subject to acceptance by
> the awarding institution)**."

This is **Qualifi stating in its own words** that the degree route depends on the
receiving university accepting the learner. It is the first explicit confirmation of
the position we have been taking editorially since S.No 27 as **GAP-SOURCE-QF-02**.

**Consequence for the whole project:** the honesty caveat applied to every Qualifi
degree-progression claim is no longer an editorial judgement — it is the awarding
body's own published qualification. This parenthetical may be quoted on any Qualifi
page that mentions degree progression. Record this in `qualifi_SHARED-FACTS.md`.

⚠️ **PARSING WARNING.** Items 2 and 3 are almost certainly mis-split by Qualifi's own
page. Learning outcome 3 refers to "mathematical skills required for financial
accounting", which indicates the real units are **"Economics for Business"** and
**"Mathematical Accounting Methods"**, with the line break falling in the wrong place.
That reading gives 12 units, the same count shown. **Do not silently correct the
awarding body's list.** Reproduce it as published and tell the reader the line break
looks misplaced and that the Centre Specification is authoritative. Same class of
problem as GAP-SOURCE-QF-07 on S.No 32.

## 6. Learning Outcomes (12, verbatim)

1. Understand the micro- and macro- economic factors that affect business
2. Understand the business environment's impact on financial accounting;
3. Develop mathematical skills required for financial accounting;
4. Understand and prepare financial accounts;
5. Understand the purpose of and use management accounting techniques
6. Understand the role of leadership and management in accounting
7. Financial theories and working capital management and the management of an organisation's global financial risk;
8. Corporate governance relating to financial planning and control, the economic and financial environment in general and potential investment appraisal;
9. Organizational business assets management and the preparation of financial reports;
10. The principles of taxation and the requirements of personal and business tax;
11. People management in organizations throughout the human resources (HR) cycle;
12. Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) in business.

One outcome per unit, in unit order. Outcomes 7–12 drop the verb and read as topic
labels. US spellings ("Organizational", "organizations") are Qualifi's; use British
English in our own prose.

## 7. Entry Requirements (verbatim substance)

- Approved centres decide on the applicant's ability to complete and meet the demands;
  initial assessment considers available learner support.
- Accessible without artificial barriers. **Applicants must be aged 18 or over.**
- IELTS 6 (or equivalent) where English is not the first language.
- International qualifications checked for appropriate enrolment.
- Normally two supporting references, at least one preferably academic.

No prior qualification level stated and **no experience clause** (unlike S.No 35).
Carries the incongruous "postgraduate programmes" clause — **do not repeat it.**

## 8. Career outcomes

No destinations, salaries or employment rates. Three published progression routes,
one of which is employment in an associated profession.

⚠️ As at Level 3 (S.No 31): this is **not** a professional accountancy qualification
and Qualifi publishes **no** exemptions from ACCA, CIMA, ICAEW or any other body. Make
no exemption claim.

## 10. Notes

- Substantially broader than the three-subject Level 3 award (S.No 31): adds
  economics, taxation, financial reporting, governance, HR and CSR.
- Ethics and CSR as a full unit is a real differentiator worth featuring.
- Relationship to S.No 31: same subject, same awarding body, two levels up, four times
  the credit, and a Level 6 or degree-final-year exit instead of a Level 4 one.
