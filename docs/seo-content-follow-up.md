# SEO and content follow-up

## P1 — Homepage

1. Review and approve the homepage meta title and description in Homepage → Homepage SEO.
2. Provide an approved, publicly accessible social share image and check previews after deployment.

## P2 — SEO and content

1. Prioritize course and pathway pages; write unique, accurate titles and descriptions and verify awarding-body names.
2. Review homepage images; add accurate alt text to informative images and leave decorative images empty.
3. Approve a structured-data strategy and verify facts before adding schema. Correct invalid existing schema; enter JSON without script tags.
4. Verify the executive MBA article’s 35–50% salary and 2023 GMAC claims against original sources, including cohort and timeframe. Cite, qualify or remove unsupported claims.
5. Review advice articles for grammar, dates, qualifications and recognition claims.
6. Provide Search Console and analytics access if available; review priority landing pages and field Core Web Vitals.

## P3 — Events and contact details

1. Verify UK and UAE addresses, phone numbers and email addresses; update shared site settings after approval and check the contact page and both footers.
2. Review eligible business listings and update them with approved contact details.
3. Keep event dates and publication status current; review past-event details and recaps.

Ongoing SEO, reporting and business listing management require a separate scope agreement.
