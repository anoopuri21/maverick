# SEO and content follow-up

Items below are outside technical implementation and require client/SEO or editorial review. Do not treat them as completed or approved. No CMS content is modified by this document.

## P1 — Homepage metadata

- **Pending client/admin:** Review and approve the homepage meta title and description in Homepage → Homepage SEO. Existing CMS values are not overwritten.
- **Pending client/admin:** Provide and configure an approved, publicly accessible Open Graph share image; verify social previews after deployment. Twitter image may fall back to the OG image.

## P2 — SEO and editorial review

- **Pending SEO/client:** Prioritize course and pathway pages, research and write accurate distinct titles and descriptions, and verify awarding-body names. No automatic truncation or bulk overwrite.
- **Pending SEO/client:** Inventory homepage informative images lacking meaningful text alternatives, classify decorative/redundant images, and supply accurate descriptions where existing CMS data is insufficient.
- **Pending SEO/client:** Decide and approve structured-data strategy and verify all organization, breadcrumb and editorial facts before any new schema is introduced. Audit existing CMS schema and correct invalid or script-wrapped entries; paste JSON only, without script tags.
- **Pending editorial/client:** Verify the executive MBA article's 35–50% salary and 2023 GMAC claims against original sources, including cohort and timeframe. Cite reliable sources or remove/qualify unsupported claims. Check grammar, dates, qualifications and recognition statements.
- **Pending SEO/client:** Provide Search Console and analytics access if available; review organic landing-page priorities and field Core Web Vitals. Reporting and ongoing SEO work require separate scope agreement.
